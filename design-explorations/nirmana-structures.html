Alternative Structures for the Instrument Pane

Five replacements for the generic orbital sphere — each grounded in the MARSYS language (black ground, gold ramp, sacred geometry) and, where possible, in Jyotiṣa's own visual grammar. Live motion runs in this file; the notes call out what each buys and what it costs.

A · classical instrument

Armillary Sphere

A real pre-telescopic astronomical instrument: gold great-circle rings tilted around a central sun. Layers become rings at growing radius and tilt; assets are beads riding them. Reads instantly as “a precise classical instrument,” which is exactly what Nirmāṇa is.

+ Most on-brand & thematic + Genuine 3-D depth & life at idle ~ DAG edges harder to read on rings
B · jyotiṣa-native dial

Nakṣatra Dial / Astrolabe

The chart’s own grammar: a circular dial divided into segments (rāśi / nakṣatra), concentric rings for the six layers, and a slow sweep hand that lights each asset as it passes — the build literally becomes a transit. An operator reads it like a kuṇḍalī.

+ Speaks Jyotiṣa directly + Sweep = elegant build progress ~ Dependencies implied, not drawn
C · readable topology

Ascension Column

Literally the brand line, “from earth to intelligence”: L0 Brahmagyāna at the base, L5 Mīmāṃsā at the crown, each layer a stratum of nodes with edges flowing upward as light. The clearest read of the actual dependency DAG, and ignition rises from the ground.

+ Best for scanning dependencies + Embodies the tagline ~ Least “wow”, more diagram than icon
D · celestial field

Constellation Field

Assets as stars, each layer its own named constellation, joined by faint star-chart lines. It twinkles at idle so the pane is never dead, and a building asset flares like a nova. Atmospheric and unmistakably astrological without being literal.

+ Always alive, low-key + Thematic (stars = jyotiṣa) ~ Layout looks scattered, not ordered
E · sacred geometry as structure

Śrī Yantra Mandala

The brand says sacred geometry is the only decorative motif — here it becomes the structure itself. Interlocking yantra triangles and a lotus surround a central bindu (ROOT); layers are nested rings, assets sit on petal tips, and a build illuminates outward from the centre. The most ceremonial option.

+ Purest brand expression + Ceremonial, memorable ~ Fixed symmetry fights a changing DAG
