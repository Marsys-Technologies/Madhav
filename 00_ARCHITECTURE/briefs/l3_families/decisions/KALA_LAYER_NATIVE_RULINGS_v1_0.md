### 1.1 `NR-KALA-R13-20261006` — the four semantic corrections are doctrine

**Question.** Does the plan adopt Astra's four semantic corrections as binding doctrine for every Kāla stage and read model?

**Ruling. Yes.** Binding from this date:

- **(a) Distinct daśā constructions, never aliased.** Vimśottarī, Aṣṭottarī, Yoginī, Kālacakra, Cara (Jaimini rāśi daśā), kāraka-kendrādi, Mūla, Naisargika, Mudda and the KP sub-lord reading are separate named methods with their own L1 producer and applicability. Sārāvalī PG155 is the Mūla daśā rule, not a Vimśottarī start variant; Lagna-star and Satyācārya variants apply to the nakṣatra ladder only. Kālacakra is not confined to health (BPHS 46.131–134). Descriptive tags and dependency metadata replace competence classes; shared ancestry forbids independence claims, nothing forbids comparing the same outcome across methods.
- **(b) Effective cancellation states.** Cancellation is rule-targeted defeat (`defeats` / `excepts` edges on a specific conclusion), with the candidate conclusion and its defeating evidence both retained and an **effective state** every consumer reads. A cancelled obstruction is never operationally active; a defeated adverse conclusion is not support for the opposite.
- **(c) Path-specific period gating.** A read model carries the judge's own period anchor and applies no universal lord-period intersection; "the constituents' daśā" is an annotation unless Pravāha admits it as a rule path with a locator. PD-level records remain testimony and never become scored timing (AM-21 part 4, `NR-P1-PD-LEVEL-20261002`).
- **(d) Separate estimands on shared preparation.** The jury's incremental-agreement null, the whole-pipeline selection null and the forecaster's class-specific field-maximum null are three experiments with their own conditioning and statistics; they share the shift schedule and segment preparation only. Denominator = non-identity shifts + the observation, declared.

**Effect.** The doctrine as written in `KALA_ASSET_ALGORITHM_ELEVATIONS_v1_1.md` §3.1, §3.3, §3.4, §3.5, §3.19, §3.20 and `KALA_LAYER_CODE_ARCHITECTURE_PLAN_v1_1.md` §1, §6.2 is sealed for the packet briefs. **Overturn:** a new ruling id citing a served locator or a measured defect.
