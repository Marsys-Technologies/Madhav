- **Still procedural, not SQL:** that the approver saw the digest; that the sealing workflow calls the brief code honestly (the approval-gated workflow is part of the trusted system); that the steward approves only on a clean brief and reports every approval to the owner.
- **What is NOT claimed:** "no numerical result exists in this generation" — it is not a property of the database. What is enforced is: no record, window or membership link carries a numeric result or a qualified valence under the all-NULL policy; and legacy projection rows for generation `'5.0'` are refused by `legacy_projection_rows_present`.
