### 4.1 What `ga_dashas_writer.py` (origin/main 7a6481ad4) actually writes — read, not assumed

- **Window and clipping, every level.** `WINDOW_START/END :96–97`; `min_jd = julday(1950,1,1,0)`, `max_jd = julday(2100,12,31,0)` (`:1166–1167`).
