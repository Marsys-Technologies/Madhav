## The ruling
P1 **pratyantar-level (PD)** readings are authorised **TESTIMONY**, not scored-path members, for the all-NULL milestone:
- they carry `operator_role = 'testimony'` and the named limitation **`p1_pd_level_no_source`**;
- they are stored, verified and served with their anchor; they are **never counted** in any admitted-score set, qualified P1 scoring (AM-19), burden/ranking surface or the candidate gate's scored path.

## Basis
Phaladīpikā XX.34–38 (served corpus; cited by locator in `design/GOCHARA_SPECS_V1_5_AMENDMENTS_DRAFT.md` AM-21 parts 1–4) name the **daśā and bhukti** lords only. The PD level is **the frozen specification's own extension**, with **no served source**; AM-21 part 2 already flagged it ("no verse supports it"). Authorised testimony is the honest tier for an extension the corpus does not carry — the same "honest tier over an invented judgment" principle as CLAUDE.md §N.4/§N.7.
