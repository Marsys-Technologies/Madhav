## 2. Source-kind grading (K1 / K3 / K2) — confirmed as the review insists

- **K1** = a verse that supports the MEANING of a house or planet. Examples confirmed above: PhD I.12-16 (house significations), UK significator items, BPHS 11.x house meanings, PhD II.1-7 planet significations. A K1 locus supports "the 10th house = honour/profession", never "a transit over the 10th produces a recognition event".
- **K3** = the step from a meaning to an EVENT rule, or from a Moon/dasha-relative statement to a lagna-transit rule. Every one of the eight classes' windows is a K3 derivation. Note the Yavana Jataka planet-by-house transit chapters (45-50) are the closest thing to a direct transit-event statement in the served corpus; the readers confirm what they say, but whether their reckoning is from the lagna or the Moon is not decided by the text I read and must be settled before any YJ verse is used as K1 for a lagna-transit rule.
- **K2** = scored use of a derived rule: only through an owner ruling (ND-H-20261005; ND-P2-20261005). This is spec §0 and is already how both delegate decisions are written.
- The memo's claim "every class has at least two K1 houses" is true only as a statement about house MEANINGS; as event rules none is K1. The delegate decisions already adopted this grading.
