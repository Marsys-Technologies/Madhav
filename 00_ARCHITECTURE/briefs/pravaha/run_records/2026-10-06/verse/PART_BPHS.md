**Important mechanics.** In the served corpus the `chapter` field (and the `read_chapter` argument) is the PDF page number, not the text's own chapter. Example: `read_chapter(bphs, 11)` returns Santhanam's translator's introduction, not BPHS ch.11. Locators `PGn:Cm` are page n, chunk m. The printed page number is n+1 for BPHS (PG121 shows printed "122"). All PG locators below were read via `read_chapter(text_id, chapter=n)`. BPHS ch.7 / ch.15 as `read_chapter` arguments return only front-matter (TOC and intro), so they were not used.

Coverage: no claim in this share cites Jaimini Sutras (`bphs_jaimini`) or Brihat Jataka (`brihat_jataka`). Hora Sara appears once, Saravali three times (four locators), BPHS in many rows.

## Notes

1. **Numbering.** For every BPHS claim where a chapter.verse was given, the served verse numbers match the claim (7.39-43, 11.2-13, 15.2-4, 23.7, 24.18, 41.23-27, 52.60-61, 53.62). Chapter attribution is inferred from page headers or the TOC in the corpus. PG locators in the claims match the served PG (not printed page numbers, which are +1). The only numbering deviation is row 17, where 15.14 sits inside a served "10-14" block. Verse numbers inside OCR-reflowed chunks are sometimes fragmented but legible.
2. **Page fragmentation.** A verse can straddle two PG chunks (7.39-43 starts on PG102:C1 and continues on PG103; BPHS 32's father/mother sentence begins on PG319). The PG that carries the claimed words is the one cited, but a verse-level citation should cite both pages.
3. **Translator notes vs śloka.** Rows 13 (PG324:C2, Ketu emancipation), 23 (gaṇḍānta definition), 12 (the "three verses" note) and the note text in row 3 and row 9 are Santhanam's commentary, not Parasara's verse text.
4. **OCR.** Devanagari lines are garbled throughout and were not used. English text is generally reconstructable. OCR errors seen: "trrdc" (trade), "parent'a death", "Clapter 52", "Chaper 53", "Chapter 21" at PG198 (really ch.24, noted only for context), "Paaravatamsa".
5. **Version.** Santhanam translation (ref: archive.org/details/BPHSEnglish), tagged [HIGH] in `source_citation`.
