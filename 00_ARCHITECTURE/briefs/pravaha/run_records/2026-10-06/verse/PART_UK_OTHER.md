## Notes

- Locator semantics: PGn:Cm is page n, m-th chunk, **per text_id**. The ND-H "New loci" paragraph is HIGH-accurate on text content and item numbers but two of its seven loci are one chunk off: Mars (81) is on PG125:C1 (not PG124:C1), Mercury (19) is on PG125:C2 (not PG125:C1). The 12th-house, Jupiter, Saturn, Rahu and Ketu loci are exact. All are `uttara_kalamrita`, Sastri's translation. In UK PG121:C1 contains only the end of the 12th-house list plus the start of the Sun list, so it is a different content from bphs PG121:C1 (BPHS 11.x), which is a separate claim.
- OCR damage in the UK significator pages: "signiflcator", "sigmificator", "Iecturtng", "(1 n Great penance" (item 11), "gth/gth" style slips in BPHS PG320. No quoted item text was materially damaged; Devanagari lines are garbage and were ignored.
- UK semantic search did not help: `find_verses_about(... text_ids=['uttara_kalamrita'])` returned 0 results and `read_classical_text` surfaced Nadi texts first; the verification relied on `read_chapter` by page number, which is deterministic, plus one hybrid-search hit that confirmed chunk order (`uttara_kalamrita:PG125:C2`).
- "Items counts": the Mars list in the book has two "Land" entries ((2) and (91)); Rahu (13) is specifically "going to a different country", Mars (20) "Going to a foreign country" (not claimed but relevant to foreign_settlement).
- PG84:C1 and PG119:C1 are commentary on illustrative charts, not Kalidasa's verses (the claim that they are "commentary only" is correct).
