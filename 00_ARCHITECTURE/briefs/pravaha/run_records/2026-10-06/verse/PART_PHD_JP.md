# PART_PHD_JP: Phaladeepika (PhD) and Jataka Parijata (JP) citations vs the served corpus

Verified 2026-10-05, read-only, via `read_chapter` (text_id `phaladeepika`, `jataka_parijata`). Editions as served: PhD = V. Subrahmanya Sastri, 2nd ed. 1950 [HIGH]; JP = Subrahmanya Sastri.

## How the served corpus is indexed (read this first)

- The served `chapter` field is the **PDF page number**, not the adhyaya. `verse_ref` is `PG<n>:C<m>`. `read_chapter(chapter=1)` returns front-matter junk; `chapter=45` returns PG45. The claims' `PGn:Cm` locators are therefore directly usable; the Roman-numeral chapter and the sloka number come only from the OCR running headers (`Adh. XV`, `Sloka 22`) and the Contents pages (PhD PG22-23, PG31).
- Each page is a single chunk (C1); a verse can straddle two pages.
- **Systematic numbering mismatch: none** for PhD I, II, XV.15-24, XXIII, XXVI and JP I, VIII. The chapter.verse numbers in the claims match the served running headers / sloka labels, with one exception (row 25, XV.22 at PG186 is XIV.22 in the served text). OCR mangles some sloka labels (noted per row), but the numbering sequence is recoverable.

## Notes

1. **Only locator error found**: "PhD XV.22 PG186:C1 'heaven'" should be **PhD XIV.22 PG186:C1**. Evidence: running header "Adh. XIV" on PG185 and PG187 (the latter also carries the continuation of Sloka 23 to 25, and Slokas 24-28 end with the past/future-birth rules, all XIV), and the Contents page PG22 listing the after-death region and refuge of the departed under ADHYAYA 14. XV.22 proper (PG197) is the Sun-as-father's-Lagna rule, which is the verse cited by Source 3 for "Sun = father's lagna" and is fine.
2. **XXIII.13 quote fidelity**: "exact degree" and "reveals the full effects" are the TOC paraphrase (PG31). If the memo's BASIS line says the verse "body" says that, it overstates it; the served body says the planet "traverses through so much of the distance in that house, it produces the effects due to that Bhava". The statement that non-exactness is honestly lower is the memo's inference, not the verse's. The memo flagged "verse body unread"; having read it, treat the body wording as authoritative.
3. **II.1 and bereavement**: PhD II.1 gives the Sun as significator of "father", nothing about death or bereavement. Registry labelling "Sun kāraka of bereavement ... verse_cited (PhD II.1)" is a one-step inference.
4. **Sloka-number fidelity**: Slokas 11 and 12 of Adhyaya I share one label ("Slokas 11-12", PG43) covering the 3rd, 4th and 5th houses, so the claims' "I.12" for the 4th and 5th house terms is consistent with the served range but cannot be split from I.11. I.13 to I.16 are individually labelled. The 7th-house terms (Adhvan, Marga) sit under sloka 13 per label order, as claimed.
5. **OCR damage** (none materially affects a quoted claim word): PhD sloka numerals often misread ("25" for 15, "73" for 13, "70" for 10, "Sloka d." for 8, "Jhka i" for 24); Devanagari headwords are garbled throughout; PG43-45 gloss brackets are mostly intact. JP: "Kshttt/Marn/Patfufa", "Gnnia" for Jnana, "Bhuhthi", "Sloka 01" for 64, page-header numerals garbled ("Si 49-ra"). No Sanskrit or English word the claims quote was lost.
6. **Not examined**: BPHS, YJ, Saravali, Hora Sara and Uttara Kalamrita citations are outside this share. Rate limiting (HTTP "rate_limit_exceeded") was hit once; all reads were completed on retry, so no row rests on a failed call.
