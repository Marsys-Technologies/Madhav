# Independent read-only investigation (Fable, for the steward) — window:marriage:P3 member geometry failure
Conclusion: representational mismatch, VERIFIER side. Confidence in mechanism: high. Not confirmed by computation.
- Builder (record_store.solve_point_edges 230-303, _in_orb_span_around_root 182-227): P3 point contacts are bounded within the exact root's own monotone ARC; arcs end at stations (arcs.py 267-298, 300-354). A station inside the 1 degree band with the ray level crossed on both sides is stored as TWO abutting, strictly positive contacts [entry,S] and [S,exit] sharing the station instant S. Zero-length rows are dropped and forbidden by CHECK (migration 1153:1102); the two log lines with one timestamp are two contacts, not one zero-length contact.
- Verifier (window_verifier.verify_member_geometry 628-669, _probe_contact 538-598, _probe_contact_derived 601-625): demands every non-horizon end be an orb-edge crossing (outside probes at t_in-dt and t_out+dt must be outside the orb). At S the body is inside the band, so A fails just after its end and B fails just before its start. A true tangency would fail the INSIDE probes with a different message.
- Prior ruling already in force: A5_3_REGISTERED_WRITER_BRIEF v1.36 (lines 1160-1166): per-branch episodes may end at an arc seam inside the band; certification compares the UNION; a seam between abutting episodes is not a boundary of the contact SET. Implemented in contact_certify.py 26-32, 69-95 (why verify:marriage passed) but NOT in verify_member_geometry. WP2_FIXTURES case 01 (close-station trap) expects stored ends at exact roots.
- Only P3 (and P4, which reuses P3 edges for Jupiter and Saturn and will fail the same way) use degree_point conjunction/aspect edges; P1/P2 are residence spans.
- The instants match Venus direct station (1998-02-05 UT) and Mercury retrograde station (2000-06-23 UT), from memory of the ephemeris, unverified.
- Chart-independent in practice: over decades some station lands within 1 degree of some ray level on any chart.
Proposed minimal fix (verifier stays ephemeris-only and no weaker for genuine single spans): in verify_member_geometry group member contacts by (body, relation, canonical target); union spans whose consecutive ends abut within the derived tolerance (bm.boundaries_agree); keep every per-contact INSIDE probe; run OUTSIDE probes only at the union's outer ends (unless horizon-open); for each interior junction J independently confirm it is a legitimate seam (a station: time_tolerance_seconds is None) and that the body is inside the band at J plus and minus the margin; a gap beyond tolerance keeps outside probes on both sides. Add a real-sky test driving a station-in-band pair through verify_member_geometry.
