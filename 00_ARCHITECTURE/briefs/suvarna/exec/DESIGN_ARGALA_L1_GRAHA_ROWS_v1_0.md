## 0. What is ruled, and what goes to J1 by name

AR-1: L1 owns the pairing **2-12, 4-10, 11-3, 5-9**; obstruction applies to benefic and malefic argala; the canonical outcome is by count and asserts only what a count can certify (`unobstructed`; `argala_prevails` when the causers outnumber the obstructors; otherwise `undetermined`, never `obstructed`), and the any-obstructor reading is stored beside it as a named variant (section 0, outcome paragraph). **Goes on the J1 reviewers' list by name:** (1) the pairing 2-12, 4-10, 11-3, 5-9 (the 11-3 pair rests on the worked example, because the translator's note is OCR-garbled; the 5-9 pair rests on one sentence); (2) the reversal for both Rahu and Ketu; (3) **argala outcome: only prevails-by-count is asserted; defeat needs a strength basis; vipareeta flagged, not modelled**; (4) **argala outcome rule: count vs any-obstructor**, with the divergence stated next to the question: on the canonical chart (Lahiri, pre-ephemeris estimate) the any-obstructor reading calls **13 of 32** rows obstructed, while the canonical count reading calls **0** of 32 defeated (19 unobstructed, 8 `argala_prevails`, 5 `undetermined`; malefic-source rows: 9 of 19 obstructed by any-obstructor against 0 by count; the other three ayanamshas agree, surya_siddhanta gives 10 of 28 against 0). Under the earlier equal/fewer reading the count rule would have called 2 obstructed; the disjunctive rule removes even those. L2 consequence: no argala edge is cancelled from the canonical field (section 2).

**The outcome is two named readings, and the canonical one asserts only what a count certifies (SS changes 1 and the outcome decision).** The BPHS worked example (`bphs_pg0312_c01`) calls all three argalas "countered". The rule in both texts is **disjunctive**: the argala prevails if its planets are stronger OR more numerous than the obstructors (`bphs_pg0311_c01`; Jaimini Su. 8). With "stronger" null (no sourced measure), by count alone exactly one outcome is certain. So **`outcome_by_count`** (CANONICAL, carried by `fact_value_text`) is `unobstructed` (no obstructor in the paired sign), `argala_prevails` (more causing grahas than obstructors: the "more numerous" half holds), or `undetermined` (obstructors not fewer: defeat needs the strength comparison). **`obstructed` is not emitted by the canonical field while `strength_comparison` is null.** The facts are stored plainly: `obstruction_present`, `argala_count`, `obstructor_count`, `count_relation` (`obstructors_more` / `obstructors_equal` / `obstructors_fewer`) and `outcome_reason`. **`outcome_any_obstructor`** (`obstructed` whenever `obstruction_present`, else `unobstructed`) stays as the named variant matching the worked example's wording and today's L1 virodha score. On the worked example the canonical field reads Mars/Saturn `undetermined`, Sun-Mercury/Venus `argala_prevails`, Jupiter/Moon-Rahu `undetermined`, while the variant reads all three `obstructed`. **Vipareeta** (`bphs_pg0311_c01`: three or more malefics in the 3rd give "vipareeta Argala ... harmless and very favourable"; Su. 6): when three or more **natural** malefics (the L0 constant: Sun, Mars, Saturn; the nodes are stated separately and not counted, a reading recorded for J1) stand in the 3rd from the reference, the row sets `vipareeta_condition: true` and the 11th/3rd pair is never read as obstruction (`undetermined`, reason `vipareeta_condition`); Jupiter in the 11th with Sun, Mars and Saturn in the 3rd is the test case. Full vipareeta modelling is post-J1. `formula_provenance_text` states all of this. The golden test asserts both readings and kills the rule variants (obstructed emitted, unobstructed and undetermined swapped, vipareeta ignored).

## 5. What reads `integrity_check_sql`, what gates on it, and the frozen-manifest effects (SS changes a and f)

Read from the code and the live registry/trigger definitions (read-only); not exercised by a dispatch.

**Readers of `integrity_check_sql`:**

| reader | what it does | gates anything? |
|---|---|---|
| `asset_runner._run_data_writer` (post-write, lines ~1167-1209) | runs the SQL after a writer finishes; red means `mark_asset_error` ("post-write integrity check failed"); a light writer rolls back, a heavy one (ga_structural has substeps) keeps its substep commits but the asset is not accepted | **Yes: the ga_structural build itself, at S-L1** |
| `asset_runner._probe_asset` (preflight, ~1589-1640) | skip-if-green / regenerate-if-red, only when `rebuild_on_probe_fail` is true | **No**: live value is false for ga_structural, ga_strength, ga_condition, ga_yoga |
| `definitions.ts` `integrity_verified` (~1900-1980) | the Nirmana acceptance evidence executes the SQL and requires `integrity_passed = true` | **Yes**: ga_structural's own L1 acceptance evidence |
| registry-contract fingerprint (`definitions.ts:135-157`, `dispatch_nirmana_campaign_wave.py REGISTRY_CONTRACT_FIELDS`) | `count_sql` and `integrity_check_sql` are both fingerprint fields | binds accepted evidence to a generation; see below |
| `asset_census.py` (Build.count_integrity) | checks only that the text is present | no |
| `monitor.ts`, `snapshot.ts` | load the column to compute the fingerprint | monitor status only |
| `provenance` / `asset_freshness` trigger `nirmana_registry_receipt_invalidation` (migration 596) | fires on UPDATE OF `depends_on, natural_key_partition, health_probe, integrity_check_sql, target_floor, asset_kind, asset_type, scope, has_writer, is_active, target_table` (**not `count_sql`**) and marks the asset's `asset_freshness` `stale` (`registry_changed`) | **Yes, by side effect**: direct dependents fail DEP-ASSERT (`deps_unsatisfied` needs lit **and** fresh: `ga_structural(receipt:stale)`) until a governed receipt |

Nothing else reads the verdict: dependents' gating is `lit` plus `fresh` of their direct dependencies, not the integrity result; the ka_gochara cutover scripts carry their own text for their own asset.

**Consequence for 1221.** Something does gate on it (the S-L1 build, ga_structural's acceptance evidence, and the freshness side effect), so **1221 applies as a named step of S-L1, immediately before the ga_structural launch, with or after the writer deploy** (never before it: the old writer image fails the post-write check), not with 1219. Applied then, the red state never stands on live data and the stale freshness is cleared by the rebuild that follows.

**Consequence for floors.** `target_floor` is in the trigger's column list. A floor re-declaration migration AFTER S-L1 marks each edited asset `stale` (`registry_changed`) again, so its direct dependents are blocked until the next governed receipt; the delta-skip path clears it on a byte-identical re-dispatch (`asset_runner`: "a `registry_changed`-stale flag must clear here"), at the cost of one no-op dispatch of each edited asset. 1219 therefore touches no floor, and the post-S-L1 floor migration should be scheduled with that dispatch in mind (or the floors declared before S-L1, where the rebuild clears the flag).

**Frozen manifests (Nirmana, definition `t3-2026-09-11-8b884eac`, all 128 assets, the ga_* assets included).** `registry_contract` holds `count_sql` and `integrity_check_sql` (not `target_floor`, not ownership, not the digest spec). `assertManifestMatchesRegistry` mismatch gives the monitor status `evidence_refresh_required`, naming the assets whose contract differs:

- after **1219**: `ga_strength` and `ga_condition` (count_sql edited). `ga_structural`'s count_sql text is unchanged, so it is not named.
- after **1221**: `ga_structural` (integrity_check_sql edited).

No other manifest is touched (`depends_on`, layer and `has_writer` are unchanged, so no `plan_adaptation_required`). Accepted evidence for an edited asset is bound to its registry fingerprint and is refreshed at that asset's next acceptance; `dispatch_nirmana_campaign_wave.py` rebuilds the live contract only for the wave's own assets and checks layer and `depends_on`, and an accepted rebuild bound to a past generation no longer blocks redispatch (adjudication #2276).
