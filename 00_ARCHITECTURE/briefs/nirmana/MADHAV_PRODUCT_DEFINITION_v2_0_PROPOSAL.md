# Madhav — Product Definition

> **Madhav is a whole-chart Jyotish instrument a person can interrogate and hold to account.** It holds more of a chart and of the tradition than any one acharya can hold at once; it reads all of it before it speaks; every claim it makes cites its evidence, and every significant one names its rivals and says what would prove it wrong; every time-bound claim is written down when made and later scored against what actually happened; and a person can ask, challenge and return to any of it in plain language, without becoming an astrologer.

## 0. How to read this document

**What it is.** The product-level reference that every component of Madhav — the six data layers, the retrieval registry, the Paripraśna conversation, the managed and direct MCP doors, the portal, the evaluation loop, the operator instrument — will be aligned to. It says what the product is, whom it is for, what a person gets, what the instrument may never do, how we will know it deserves its claims, and what the native must decide before alignment begins.

**What it is not.** It is not a plan, a brief, a readiness claim or an authorization. It changes no governed rule by itself (§13 lists the rules it asks the native to change). It asserts no chart fact, no date, no accuracy figure and no delivery date.

**How it is organized.** §1 the product · §2 the person · §3 the standard · §4 the six signature experiences · §5 breadth and its honest dispositions · §6 the intellectual model · §7 how the intelligence works · §8 every component's binding contribution · §9 the experience around the insight · §10 boundaries · §11 proof · §12 what changed from v1.1 and why · §13 the nineteen decisions for the native · §14 sequence · Appendices A–D (glossary, technique coverage today, disposition of v1.1's portfolio, evidence basis).

**Vocabulary** is defined once, in Appendix A, and used consistently. Six words carry most of the weight: *Evidence*, *Claim*, *Reading*, *Receipt*, *Inquiry*, *Observation*.

## 1. The product

### 1.1 The mission, restated without demotion

The governed mission has three numbered obligations, none subordinate to another (MACRO_PLAN_v2_0.md:105-107; CLAUDE.md §A): **(1)** read the chart with acharya-grade depth; **(2)** surface patterns and contradictions across layers and systems that no individual astrologer could hold in working memory; **(3)** make time-indexed, probabilistic, calibrated predictions testable against lived reality and correctable from outcomes — and then extend the method beyond this native so the instrument becomes a research tool for the discipline. The mission is bounded: probabilistic, calibrated, auditable outputs, for consenting audiences, under stated disclosure classes; **never a fortune-telling product.**

v1.1 kept obligations (1) and (2) and demoted obligation (3) to "a major capability within" understanding. This document restores it, for a reason that is not sentimental: **obligation (3) is the only one that can be wrong against the world rather than against the instrument's own evidence.** Source fidelity, computation and interpretive specificity have their own tests (§11), but a structural reading that never produces a testable consequence can be beautiful, specific-sounding and unfalsifiable at once — the Barnum condition. The frozen, scored claim is what keeps the other two obligations honest. It is the promise, not a feature.

**The wedge.** The first thing that must be undeniably excellent is one *kept* reading on the native's chart — whole-chart, receipted, drillable, with its rivals and falsifier shown — whose forward claims are captured and whose first window closes and is scored. Everything in §4–§8 exists to make that reading routine, then to widen it to consenting others. Nothing widens before that reading passes its own test (§11).

### 1.2 What the person experiences: understand, navigate, account

To the person, the three obligations become three verbs:

- **Understand** — what is distinctive in my chart, how its parts relate, where the tradition argues with itself about me, and what a competent simpler reading would have missed.
- **Navigate** — how that structure moves through time; why one window differs from another; how a real choice compares under stated objectives; what the tradition prescribes and with what standing.
- **Account** — what you told me, when, on what basis; what happened; how often such claims hold, or that nobody yet knows; and what would change your mind.

Every signature experience in §4 belongs to one of these verbs. Every component in §8 serves at least one.

### 1.3 The unit of value

v1.1 never named what a person keeps. This document does. The durable objects are five, and each already has (or is a proposed extension of) a home in the system:

```
EVIDENCE   L0 passage/rule · L1 fact · L2 signal/mechanism/contradiction · L3 window · L4 anchor
   ↓  cited by reference, never restated (§N.5)
CLAIM      a typed statement about this chart with its evidence and typed confidence (PPR-03);
           a SIGNIFICANT claim (domain verdict, time-indexed, remedial, prediction-detected,
           rules-in-tension) also carries ≥3 candidate readings, the selected one with
           rationale, and its falsifier — or a monitored waiver (PPR-02). Computed facts such as
           a daśā boundary are facts, not forecasts, and carry none of this
   ↓  assembled into
READING    one turn's plain-language answer over its claims, in one register, with its RECEIPT —
           the machine-checkable record that says which evidence, which coverage, which gaps,
           which safety decision, which confidence types (AcharyaReadingReceipt v1, NCD-3)
   ↓  held open by
INQUIRY    a question the person is pursuing across readings and time, with a closing state
           (settled by observation · by source · by computation · closed by the person · lapsed ·
           unverifiable) — proposed as an extension of the Samīkṣā ledger and thread, not a new asset
   ↓  answered by
OBSERVATION  the outcome recorded against a frozen time-bound claim: happened · did not · partial ·
           can't tell — human-confirmed, never inferred, never fed back into generation
```

A **reading is a view over an inquiry at a moment**; the inquiry persists. The person keeps the inquiry — its settled claims, its open contradictions, its frozen windows, its outcomes — and can return to it years later and find it unedited. This is the "living" quality v1.1 wanted from its portrait, obtained without a narrative authority that could drift into a personality diagnosis: every entry has a closing condition and an evidence chain.

### 1.4 One standard, one register, no tiers

Everyone entitled to a reading receives the same reading: full planner pipeline, full whole-chart floor, full receipt (D-15). Plain language is the higher standard, not the lesser one; audit detail is an affordance anyone can open (the receipt, the citation chip, the working region), never a mode. Internal identifiers never appear in prose, for anyone including the native (D-14). *Disclosure class* — native_self · cohort_subject · acharya_reviewer · public — is the consent-and-access axis, never a quality axis (PARIPRASHNA_ARCHITECTURE_v1_0.md §1, PPR-24). v1.1 stated the first half of this rule; both halves are needed, and §2 applies them. One consequence v1.1 missed: *research operators* (ablations, cohort pooling, held-out evaluation) are not a richer reading for researchers — that would be a tier. They live on the retrieval/research plane (raw MCP, evaluation harnesses), which carries no reading guarantee at all (§7.1), and their outputs enter a reading only through the same receipt everyone gets.

### 1.5 What the instrument is, in relation to the tradition

Madhav is not a new school and does not average the existing ones. It is a **śāstric working memory with an accountable mouth**: it holds the texts with their editions and exceptions, the computations under stated conventions, the relationships and contradictions the computations imply, the windows the clocks imply, and the record of what it said. The name of its conversational face, Paripraśna, is the Gītā's word for thorough questioning of a teacher (4.34); the product's stance is that inquiry, not pronouncement, is how understanding is earned. That stance is the whole of §4 E2.

Ring 0 carries an obligation v1.1 left unspoken: the native is at once subject, operator and the person whose life record sits in a hosted repository and flows to model providers. The definition therefore requires, before any second subject, that class C3 content (prediction ledger, outcomes, Brier, calibration) never enter a synthesis prompt or leave to a provider, that class C1 content (birth data, life events, health and relationship content, conversation prose) enter authorized synthesis only under the documented provider controls, that life events and outcomes never feed prospective generation (PPR-31), that the per-provider data posture be documented, that the repository's visibility be an explicit operational decision, and that the native's own self-experimentation bias be named in every evaluation that uses the native's outcomes (the LEL gap audit already does).

Minors (< 18) are excluded from any cohort and are not servable beyond a guardian context; people in active mental-health crisis or without capacity to consent are excluded; exclusions are logged (MP §3.5.F; PPR-14). None of this is a quality tier. It is who may be a subject, what may be produced about them, and what is disclosed about method and uncertainty. The rings are a reading aid; the governing dimensions are four and distinct — *subject kind* (native_self, consented subject, minor-in-guardian-context), *disclosure class* (MP §3.5.B), *operator role* (person, practitioner, researcher, reviewer) and *transport* (portal, managed MCP, raw MCP) — and each ring above names which of them it is.

### 2.2 What a person should gain

Kept from v1.1, tightened, and each now tied to the object that delivers it:

- **Recognition without stereotyping** — a Reading whose claims cite this chart's facts, not traits that fit anyone (proved by the blinded specificity test, §11).
- **Coherence without forced certainty** — contradictions served as contradictions, with both poles and a disposition (surfaced · adjudicated · open), never averaged away.
- **Temporal perspective** — windows as typed quantities (eligibility, activation, obstruction, contact), never one favourable-date list.
- **Discernment** — every significant Claim shows its rivals ("read it another way") and its falsifier ("what would change my mind").
- **Agency** — the person owns the decision; the instrument compares, it does not command; practices are attributed to the tradition, never imperative.
- **Continuity** — an Inquiry that accumulates without rewriting; a later reading discloses what changed (input, build, priors, date) rather than pretending the earlier one is reproducible.
- **Better questions** — the instrument asks back only when the answer would change, and names an unasked question only when it can say what answering it would change.
- **Stable understanding under uncertainty** — which claims survive the variations actually examined (today: five ayanāṃśas), and which depend on an input nobody has.
- **Access to the discipline** — the verse, its edition and its exception, one tap from the claim.

### 2.3 The person is not the chart

Chart-derived claims, user-reported circumstances, stated objectives and observed events stay distinguishable objects. A circumstance makes an answer relevant; it never becomes chart evidence. A prior reading is a `prior_reading`-grade citation that can never satisfy a whole-chart floor (recall/index.ts:4-8). Disagreement is a reason to re-retrieve, not to re-word. The chart-first, valence-neutral reveal stands (J.5): the instrument does not ask for goals before it describes the chart; goal-conditioned comparison is available on request.

## 3. The standard

### 3.1 The test that already exists

An independent senior acharya, reviewing a Reading, must conclude one of three things: *"this is my own level"*, *"this is above my own level"*, or *"this reveals things I would not have seen on first pass."* Anything less is incomplete (PROJECT_ARCHITECTURE_v2_2.md:94; CLAUDE.md §J). The panel is n ≥ 3, blinded, verdicts logged and never averaged (MP §3.7). **This is the standard.** "Beyond-Acharya" adds nothing to it that can be tested and invites a claim engineering scale cannot demonstrate; this document retires the phrase from product language until the panel has ruled, and keeps the test.

### 3.2 Where the instrument honestly exceeds a human, and where it does not

The third conclusion — *reveals what I would not have seen* — is the one Madhav is built to earn, and it is earnable on exactly these axes, each of which is a mechanism rather than a mood:

| Axis | What the instrument does that an acharya cannot | Mechanism that must exist and be tested |
|---|---|---|
| **Capacity** | Holds 30 vargas, 8–9 computed daśā systems, five ayanāṃśas, 169 fact categories and every contradiction pair at once (coverage_matrix.ts:14-184) | Whole-chart floor compiled before any model runs; completeness receipt per reading |
| **Exhaustiveness** | Applies every modelled rule with its cancellation and partial-formation conditions, every time, without fatigue | Yoga catalogue with cancellation/bhaṅga/threshold fields (migration 239); firing vs catalog-only kept distinct (§N.6) |
| **Contradiction retention** | Keeps every pair where the chart argues with itself, both poles served, disposition recorded | Contradiction register (`bodha_contradictions`) — today yoga-vs-doṣa on shared domain and graha only; §13 D-11 proposes widening |
| **Cross-system concordance, honestly counted** | Lays Parāśari, Jaimini, KP, Tājika testimony side by side and **collapses correlated witnesses** — three systems reading one placement are one witness | Triangulation surface with witness-independence rule; correlated-witness test (§11) |
| **Frozen memory** | Remembers every time-bound claim verbatim, with the build, priors and date it was made under, and cannot edit it afterwards | Samīkṣā ledger freeze trigger (migration 470:168-204); D-16 stamp copied, never referenced |
| **Calibration over time** | Reports, per cell, how often such claims held — or that the cell has not reached minimum n | Interval-only serving above the ruled activation gate (NCD-7: ±0.15 half-width, ≥60% coverage); `insufficient_calibration_data` otherwise |

And what a master acharya has that the instrument does not, which the product must not pretend to: judgment of *deśa-kāla-pātra* (the person, place and moment in front of them); *nimitta* (the omen of the consultation itself); the trained restraint to not answer; the relationship within which a remedy is prescribed. The instrument's answer to these is procedural — ask back, decline, attribute, defer — not imitative.

### 3.3 What does not qualify

Length; technique count; the same fact counted through three systems; a prestigious source name without its exception; a positive score; a ranking without a rival; novel-sounding prose; a probability without a band, an n and a coverage figure; a "verified" flag with no detector behind it (§N.8). An ordinary result, a genuine tie, an unresolved disagreement and "the chart does not distinguish these" are valid outcomes and must be common.

### 3.4 The śāstric spine the whole-chart floor must encode

"Whole-chart discipline" is empty unless it names the tradition's own reading order. The compiled floor (§7.2) is where that order lives, and a reviewer must be able to see it in the receipt: **(1)** lagna and lagneśa, then Moon and Sun as alternate lagnas; **(2)** the kāraka framework (naisargika and cara); **(3)** bhāva lords, their placement, dignity and condition, with bhāva-bala; **(4)** the varga hierarchy — rāśi, navāṃśa and the vargas that bear on the question, with vargottama and varga-concordance noted; **(5)** yogas with their formation, cancellation and partial-formation conditions, catalogue-match kept apart from firing; **(6)** the daśā system(s) applicable to this chart and question, at the level the claim needs, with the daśā-lord's capability read from the chart; **(7)** gochara as confirmation, never as sole cause — the double-transit discipline where the tradition applies it; **(8)** aṣṭakavarga as gate and weight on transit claims; **(9)** the system-specific voices (Jaimini ārūḍha/argalā, KP sub-lord, Tājika varṣa) laid beside Parāśari as witnesses, correlated ones collapsed. **The reading is phala-oriented**: what the chart indicates as results across bhāvas, weighed by the tradition's own importance instruments — ṣaḍbala, bhāva-bala, aṣṭakavarga, vargottama — not by psychological trait language; psychological framing is admitted only where classical phala supports it and is labelled as interpretation.

A **discriminating distinction** has an operational meaning here: a claim is discriminating when its rival is stated and refuted by cited evidence from *this* chart, when removing a duplicated witness does not rescue it, and when an ablation of the purportedly decisive relationship changes the verdict. A served concordance, priority or outlook figure that averages across these is not discrimination; it is its erasure.

## 4. The six signature experiences

Obligations on the whole instrument, not six screens. A person enters through any question and moves among them. Each names what exists today so the gap is honest; no chart fact or date is asserted.

### E1 · The Reading — "What does my chart actually say about this?" *(Understand)*

**What happens.** The question is classified and scoped; safety classification runs before planning; the whole-chart floor and daśā-context floor are compiled before any model runs (compiled_floor_adapter.ts; PPR-15). If the question is a pinpointed fact, the proportionality carve-out applies: one call, one fact, frame check, and a one-line escalation flag if the fact sits inside a live contradiction, firing yoga or open window (RS-4; PROJECT_ARCHITECTURE B.11). Otherwise the engine gathers the domain reading, the contradictions, the cross-domain cells and the active daśā context; for every significant judgment it forms at least three candidate interpretations, weighs them, selects one with rationale and states the falsifier (B.4; PPR-02). Prose carries zero internal identifiers; citations ride as chips graded *verified · corroborating · catalog_only · prior_reading · unverified*; catalog-only yoga matches are never presented as confirmed (§N.6). A claim the receipt cannot ground is removed or emitted as an honest gap, never as a plausible default (§N.7 item 6; B.12).

**The revealing moment.** The reading names the thing you did not ask about — the seventh-house mechanism inside your career question — shows the exact condition and the exact passage behind it, and says in one sentence what observation would make it wrong.

**Today.** Floor compilation and B.11 enforcement are live on the portal route; the receipt, typed confidence and interpretation-set machinery exist in code but are flag-gated default OFF (feature_flags.ts:314-422); the portal itself is `PARIPRASHNA_ENABLED: false` in code defaults (feature_flags.ts:523) — production configuration is confirmed only by the baseline step (§14).

### E2 · The Inquiry — "I'm not sure what I'm asking." *(Understand)*

**What happens.** Three things v1.1 scattered across E2, the "question compass" and "uncertainty resolution" become one obligation: *the instrument may ask*. (a) **Ask-back**, in exactly the three acharya-grade cases and no others: material under-specification (at most one question, never two in a row, always with "read it as asked"); material provenance drift since the last reading; an unresolved prediction window overlapping the question (PARIPRASHNA_TARGET_ARCHITECTURE_v0_1.md §6.6; the planner's live third outcome `clarification_needed`). (b) **The reframe**: when "strain" contains two questions — cash availability and profitability — the reading proceeds *and shows the fork* as two named sub-inquiries. (c) **The decisive unknown**: the instrument partitions the inquiry's claims into *survives every variation examined* and *depends on X*, names the one input whose resolution would move a claim across that line, and says whether resolving it is legitimate and worth the burden. Today the only variation axis served is the five real ayanāṃśas (ayanamsha_variation.ts:60-74); birth-time perturbation does not exist as an input or operator (kala_uncertainty.ts:28-33) — so the honest output there is "unexamined on that axis; rectification is a governed hypothesis process." (d) **The unasked question**: at most a handful, each with its reason stated as a consequence — *answering this would change a claim you were given, close an open contradiction, or resolve a window that has closed* — drawn from stored contradictions, discovery rows carrying `why_an_acharya_misses_it`, and the inquiry's own unknowns; ranked by explicit qualitative rules, never a score; "nothing worth asking now" must be common.

**The revealing moment.** "The question I brought had two questions inside it, and the chart speaks to them differently." Or: "I do not need my exact birth time for this question. I would for that one — and here is why."

**Today.** Clarification outcome live; no inquiry object persists the fork; the nearest "suggested questions" surface is 4–6 LLM-generated prompts from daśā/transit context (api/consume/suggestions/context/route.ts) — which E2(d) replaces.

### E3 · Time — "When, and why does one window differ from another?" *(Navigate)*

**What happens.** Structure is compiled to time through eligibility, activation, obstruction and contact, each served as a **typed quantity** — astronomical instant, activation measure, valence, timing shape, model rate with baseline, event probability over a horizon, empirical reliability, salience — never collapsed into one scalar (L3_KALA_VALUE_ARCHITECTURE §quantity types). *Earliest*, *strongest*, *most robust* and *best suited to your constraints* are four different answers, and the reading says which clock and which contact makes each one different. The daśā–gochara double-confirmation discipline and aṣṭakavarga gating are named where they bear. An empty obstruction table is served as `not_computed`, never as "nothing obstructs" (L3_STRATEGIC_STOCKTAKE §5). **Every forward-looking *outcome* claim is either a sealable Claim — claim, window, mechanism, falsifier, ready for the ledger without transformation — or an explicitly declared non-claim.** Computed calendar facts (a daśā boundary, a transit contact) are facts, not forecasts, and are never scored as predictions; model probability, the person's confirmed band and empirical calibration remain three distinct quantities.

**The revealing moment.** A list of dates becomes an intelligible sequence of conditions and trade-offs, and the reading says plainly which of them it is willing to be scored on.

**Today.** 13/23 Kāla assets frozen under Nirmāṇa; five temporal-output tables were at 0 rows on the canonical chart at the stocktake; the served priority scalar self-describes as a "salience monoculture" (kala_views/priority.ts:16-23) and `phala_outlook` still emits a mean-of-anchors confidence (phala_outlook.ts:13-21) — both are defects this definition retires from consumer surfaces (§13 D-12).

### E4 · The Choice — "How should I approach this decision?" *(Navigate)*

**What happens.** Real alternatives are compared against stated objectives and constraints. The astrological comparison is kept separate from practical assumptions; stable differences are separated from fragile ones; **a tie and "the chart does not distinguish these" are first-class results.** Action timing uses action-specific doctrine over actual candidate intervals (the electional surface, `kala_elect_get`), never a generic auspiciousness total. Practices are explained with their source, intent, standing and burden, in the attributive register — *"the tradition prescribes"*, never *"you should"* (PPR-04; §13.8 remedy register) — with no commerce, no unsolicited prescription, and the mortality hard-exclusion already applied before any upāya read (kala_views/upaya.ts:14-22). Controlled contrast — *vary one permitted input, one disputed method or one real alternative and show what changed, what survived and why* — is the interactive form of this experience; today only ayanāṃśa variation exists as an operator, so method-exclusion and reading-vs-reading diff are a deferred capability, stated as such.

**The revealing moment.** The person understands the trade-off and keeps the decision, instead of receiving an unexplained command.

### E5 · The Challenge — "Why that, and not the other one? Show me." *(Account)*

**What happens.** *"Show me"* drills the chip: claim → signal → `fact_id` or verse chunk in at most two hops, with the passage's edition and licence attached (RS ≤2-hop rule). *"Read it another way"* opens the non-selected candidates; *"what would change your mind"* shows the falsifier — both affordances, never forced into the prose (PPR-05). *"That's wrong"* is a **dispute row** keyed to the claim span and carrying the provenance stamp; the engine **re-retrieves, never re-words**, and does not fold when the data supports the claim (PPR-31; sycophancy defences). A fact the person supplies is recorded as user-reported circumstance and the inquiry re-runs. *"What changed since last time?"* compares **two states of the inquiry** by their stamps — build, priors, formula versions, ranking config, date — so change is attributed, not narrated.

**The revealing moment.** An interpretation you liked loses on the evidence — a duplicated signal counted twice, a yoga present only as a catalogue label — and a less obvious one survives; you saw it happen rather than being told. Or: it did not fold, and showed you why.

**Today.** Drill and grades live; dispute capture is unbuilt and the feedback endpoint discards ratings (PARIPRASHNA_TARGET_ARCHITECTURE_v0_1.md §14.8); no reading-vs-reading diff surface exists.

### E6 · The Return — "You said something about this period. Did it hold?" *(Account)*

**What happens.** Every forward Claim was captured as `detected` at turn commit; the person confirmed it with a probability **band** (never a point) or dismissed it with a reason; a database trigger froze the claim and its stamp; a daily job closed the window mechanically; the person resolves *happened · did not · partial · can't tell*, and can't-tell is excluded from scoring three independent ways (samiksha/schema.ts; migration 470; brier.ts). Coverage is reported as a neutral statistic; lapses are gaps, never debt. Where two readings disagreed and the evidence could not settle it, the instrument had frozen an **observation brief** — the alternatives, the discriminating observation including "neither", the window, what counts as support, contradiction or insufficient, and when the person saw it — and now returns to it. When a closed window overlaps a new question, the instrument asks before it answers. With permission, the person's event log is aligned against the ledger: supported, mixed, unsupported, unassessable — with unmatched activations and non-events counted, and a present-day reading of history never advertised as a past prediction (the LEL's 25 yes / 21 partial / 0 no tally is a memorable-event log with a declared sampling bias, not an accuracy figure).

**The revealing moment.** The instrument shows its own scorecard on your chart, misses as prominently as hits, "can't tell" counted honestly, and "not enough data yet" said plainly. **The first honest null, appearing where a number would otherwise have been faked, is the first proof of the product.**

**Today.** The Samīkṣā lifecycle is built and was proven live end-to-end on 2026-08-01 (CLAUDE.md footer, C4-LOOP-LIVE-PROOF); the aggregate calibration write is PARKED (samiksha/outcome_recorder.ts:16-22); `record_outcome` over MCP is retired; observation-brief shape, exposure time and dispute rows do not exist; the window-ask interrupt is unbuilt.

### The small form that runs through all six — the proportionate lookup

"Just tell me one thing" gets one thing: `depth: retrieval`, one call, one fact, frame check, done — plus the escalation valve when the fact touches material context (RETRIEVAL_STRATEGY §3.6). It is not a lesser experience; it is E1 at its correct size.

## 6. The intellectual model

### 6.1 Authenticity is a chain — and the tradition already has the vocabulary

v1.1's six links are kept and given the tradition's own names for the means of valid knowledge (*pramāṇa* — the L2 asset that maps them is `bo_pramana_mapa`):

1. **What the source says** — *śabda / āgama*, testimony: exact passage, text, edition or translation, licence, context. (L0: 16 texts, 10,651 chunks, one English translation per verse, OCR confidence recorded.)
2. **What a qualified rule means** — its prerequisites, exceptions, scope, conventions, school. (L0: 175 yogas with cancellation/bhaṅga/threshold; **3,002 regex-extracted rules with no exception or scope field and confidence fixed at 1.0** — a fidelity limit the product must disclose, not hide.)
3. **What is computed for this subject** — *gaṇita*: reproducible facts under stated conventions. (L1: five ayanāṃśas, PyJHora/DE441; 80.7% of the canonical chart's facts single-tier at last measurement, verification PARKED — *reproducible* is not *verified*, and the reading says which.)
4. **What interpretation is proposed** — *anumāna*, inference: mechanism, rivals, counter-evidence. (L2/L4.)
5. **What is claimed about reality** — an observation, or a bounded, testable forward claim. (L4 anchors with falsifiers; the ledger.)
6. **What evaluation has earned** — *pratyakṣa*, observation against the claim: computational correctness, explanatory value, empirical performance, separately. (L5, structural mode.)

An authentic passage does not make its prediction validated; a correct calculation does not select one manifestation; an elegant inference does not establish causation. **Fidelity and validity are different properties and are graded separately.**

### 6.2 The truest available forms, and how disagreement is handled

Fidelity to sources, not a smoothed consensus. Variants, scope and unresolved readings are kept. Where texts or schools disagree, the reading identifies the precise premise on which they diverge, what each is entitled to address, the shared evidence, and what observation or source adjudication could discriminate. **The hierarchy of testimony is stated, not assumed**: a foundational text (BPHS) outranks a later compendium on a rule both state; a rule with a modelled exception outranks a bare regex match; a school-variant is served as a variant with its school, never silently chosen. **Correlated witnesses collapse**: three systems, three agents or three readings that consume the same placement are one witness; agreement among them is not corroboration. Multiple AI roles are an implementation choice, not a definition of truth.

### 6.3 Classical content that is harmful — a governed stance, not silence *(§13 D-6)*

The corpus contains fatalistic, casteist and misogynistic passages, and doctrines of death, disease and progeny. "Fidelity to sources" cannot mean reproducing them as guidance. Proposed rule: every rule and passage carries a **content class** (neutral · fatalistic · status-based · gendered · mortality · health · progeny), and the reading applies a **reported-not-applied** discipline — such content may be *shown* in E5 with its source when the person asks what the text says, but is never *applied* as a claim about the person, never ranked into a verdict, and never used to justify blame or deserved fate. Mortality, health and progeny classes route through the hard stops (§10).

### 6.4 The null-result behaviour *(§13 D-7)*

v1.1 never said what the product does when the evidence goes against a claim family. Stated: when a calibration cell reaches activation and shows no skill over the climatology baseline, that claim family is **demoted to structural-only** — served with its structure and its source, without predictive language, with the finding disclosed in the calibration disclosure — never silently kept predictive, never silently dropped. Rule-level findings ("classical claim holds/fails on scope S") are published as findings of the research programme. A research programme whose null-result rate is zero is not testing anything.

### 6.5 Three kinds of discovery, one discipline

*Within an individual* (an unnoticed configuration, cross-domain relation, exception, recurrence difference, counter-example — admissible into a reading when its chain and significance are established); *recovery of knowledge* (a located passage, an adjudicated interpretation and an AI reconstruction carry different labels and authority; no surviving evidence means no claim to recovered wording); *new hypotheses* (a surprising correlation is a candidate, never doctrine or a forecast). The sequence is fixed: **candidate → source/logic examination → rival and negative-case testing → independent review → bounded admission, revision or rejection.** Searches and hypotheses are registered *before* evaluation in a hypothesis registry that records the number of candidates examined, the pre-specified test and the correction applied, so that a survivor's significance is reported against everything that was tried; exploratory and held-out evidence are separated (nine LEL events are sacrosanct held-out, LEL_HELD_OUT_PARTITION_v1_0.md); learned parameters reach serving only through separately admitted, versioned releases after shadow mode (MP learning-discipline rules; SHADOW_MODE_PROTOCOL).

### 6.6 The research programme — inside consent, after the natal proof

The "scholarship laboratory" and the "contrastive case observatory" are real ambitions and belong to MACRO_PLAN M7–M9 (population extension, classical cross-reference, multi-school triangulation), not to the consumer product's core. Today there is no consented real-case store (the 110,000-chart cohort is explicitly synthetic base-rate material), no parallel-witness or commentary layer, and the only classical-attribution registry is LLM-judged (2,330 attributions, 75% silent). The honest statement is: **the person's reading is a data-generating process for the discipline only under a purpose-scoped consent act, never by default**; withdrawal reaches derived cells; related subjects, repeated events and repeated readings retain their dependence and are never counted as independent cases; case-selection and similarity rules are fixed before outcomes are revealed; ordinary circumstances must be able to explain a pattern as well as the chart does.

## 7. How the intelligence works

### 7.1 One core, three doors

The engine is callable headlessly and never branches on which door it serves (PARIPRASHNA_ARCHITECTURE_v1_0.md §3):

```
NormalizedQuery → EntitlementDecision → SafetyPolicyDecision
  → ScopeTuple | ClarificationRequest → AcharyaPlan (floor + band)
  → ToolBroker → EvidenceBundle → Interpretation & Adjudication (≥3 candidates, falsifier)
  → Grounding / Safety Validation → SemanticReadingParts
  → TurnProvenance + AcharyaReadingReceipt → [prediction candidates → ledger]
```

| Door | What it is | Reading guarantee |
|---|---|---|
| **Paripraśna** (portal) | browser, streaming, persistent threads, the only place an Inquiry lives | FULL: core + receipt + all gates + ledger capture + Samīkṣā |
| **`prashna_ask`** (managed MCP) | any LLM client, OAuth, job handle | FULL is the target (parity, Gate 4); **today** a deterministic pre-fetch plus one non-agentic synthesis, register/sentinel gates not on its route, no durable reading identity (prashna_ask_synthesis.ts:16-27; MCP durable memory is OT-6, a *proposed* lean awaiting ruling, not settled doctrine) — parity is evidence-equivalence, never byte-identical prose |
| **Raw MCP tools** | profile-projected retrieval plane (`full` / `compact` / `consult`) | **No reading guarantee** — the client's LLM plans, loops and writes; whole-chart discipline and register are unenforceable there. Safeguards still apply: authorized, entitlement-scoped access; truthful self-describing envelopes (density contract, judgment flags, reader labels, honest coverage); correct operators; sensitive classes excluded from `consult`. "No reading guarantee" is not "no safeguards" |

v1.1 §7.3 said the two managed doors "share investigation and evidence semantics." That is the target and the parity invariant (PPR-30); it is not true today, and the definition says so.

### 7.2 Accountable coverage, with the pause rule

"All relevant data" means the compiled whole-chart floor (Bodha consultation, cross-domain cells, daśā context) plus the machine band the plan adds, with a **completeness receipt** per item — *served · empty · dark* — so "thorough" is a checkable property, not a vibe (RETRIEVAL_STRATEGY §S-3). Trim is lossy in context and lossless in reachability: every cut is declared (`trim_report`, `recover_via`), and a **dissent quota** of contradictions, anomalies and low-confidence-high-impact rows survives any budget cut (`hardFloor`). Where a required L1 cell is missing, **interpretation names the gap and pauses** — it does not paper over with "likely" (B.12). A useful partial answer is one that says which floor items were dark and how that bounds the conclusion; it is never a softened claim.

### 7.3 A rich answer is a short answer with depth behind it

The default answer makes clear, as applicable: the most important conclusion and why it matters to *this* question; the distinctive configuration behind it; the meaningful rival, contradiction or limit; the temporal or practical distinction that changes the reading; and what further information, observation or assumption would change the answer. Technical terms are used where they carry meaning and glossed inline. Internal names never appear. Difficult findings lead with their uncertainty, in shorter blocks, and never with severity the person did not ask for (the emotional-register obligation, PARIPRASHNA_ARCHITECTURE §4.4 / PPR-04); a reading that would make a person more dependent on the next reading, rather than more able to question this one, has failed even if every citation holds. Evidence, a timeline, a comparison or a focused relationship diagram is one tap away when it clarifies something; the receipt and the working region are the audit affordances behind every reading.

### 7.4 The experience, made tangible

A hypothetical shape, not a reading; no fact or date is asserted.

> **User:** When will my financial strain get over?
>
> **Madhav:** Before I answer, one question — or I can read it as asked. By "strain" do you mean money not arriving, or the business not making enough? The chart speaks to those differently, and I would rather not pick for you.
>
> **User:** The second. Profitability.
>
> **Madhav:** Then here is the reading. *[the structure behind profitability, cited; the enduring pattern vs what is active now; the nearest window and the most robust window, and why they are not the same; the one contradiction that bears on it, both sides]* … I am willing to be scored on one thing here: *[a window, a direction, a mechanism]*. If that does not happen by then, this reading was wrong about the mechanism, not just the date. Shall I write it down?
>
> **User:** Why that mechanism and not the other one people always mention?
>
> **Madhav:** *[the rival, what supports it, what refutes it in this chart, and the passage the rival rests on — with its second clause, which most readings omit]*

At any point the person can open the receipt, the rival readings and the falsifier. That is the same reading viewed more closely, not a tier.

### 7.5 Importance, effort and stopping

Within the supported inquiry, the instrument prioritises unresolved distinctions that could change the answer, its scope, its counter-evidence or its robustness — by explicit qualitative rules first (bearing on the question over magnitude; contested-but-material over confirmed-but-trivial), never by an invented information-gain score. It distinguishes a completed answer, a useful partial answer and a proposed extension. "Stable" means stable over the inputs, methods and ranges actually examined; where exploration was bounded, the answer says so and does not imply that every alternative was eliminated. Effort is bounded by the ruled spend ceilings ($2 per turn, $40 per day, pre-dispatch, both doors — NCD-8); a budget is never an excuse to present an under-investigated answer as complete.

## 9. The experience around the insight

v1.1's §9 was a list of prohibitions. A definition should storyboard.

**Day one.** The person (today: the native, or a consenting adult once PPR-14 is live) opens Paripraśna on a chart that is already built. They are told, in one screen, what the instrument knows about this chart (build date, ayanāṃśa, which parts of the whole-chart floor are dark), what it will never do (§10, in plain words), and that anything it says about a period of time will be written down and revisited. No biography is requested. They ask anything. The first reading arrives as prose; behind it, chips and a receipt they may ignore. If the question was two questions, they are asked one question, once, with "read it as asked" offered.

**The subject's lifecycle, stated.** A subject is created with birth data whose quality tier (exact · rectified · approximate · unknown time), time standard and location precision are recorded; a chart is built only by the operator (the lean in the decision register is super-admin-only build rights with a "request a rebuild" path — OT-4, *proposed and awaiting ruling*), the build's duration and any unbuilt or dark assets are visible before the first question, and every reading carries the `build_id` it was made under. A rebuild does not overwrite history: a settled reading is preserved as its immutable evidence package (snapshot-on-consume, PPR-20) and remains recoverable exactly; a later reading of the same question is generated fresh under the current generation and says what changed.

**Day thirty.** A window the instrument committed to has not closed; nothing has happened; the instrument is silent — unchanged evidence is a reason for quiet. A window has closed: the person is asked, once, what happened, with *can't tell* offered without penalty. They disagree with a reading; the disagreement becomes a row, the engine re-retrieves, and either holds its ground with the evidence shown or changes with the change attributed. They ask "what should I be asking?" and get two questions with their consequences, or "nothing worth asking now."

**Year three.** The person opens an inquiry from two years ago. Every claim is as it was; the ones with windows carry outcomes; the scorecard shows misses first. A later reading of the same question discloses what differs — a rebuild, a priors version, a corrected input, the date — rather than pretending the earlier one is reproducible. Where enough claims of a kind have closed across consenting charts to pass the activation gate, a trust question gets an interval, an n and a coverage figure; where they have not, it gets "not enough data yet, and here is what it would take." If the person withdraws, identifying and derived personal rows are deleted, pooled cells are recomputed, and the ledger records that a deletion occurred as an audit fact, not content.

**During an investigation.** Useful progress, not tool transcripts: calculating, comparing, checking a source, handling a gap. A failure or budget stop preserves usable work and names the partial result's limits.

**Language.** Plain English by default; Sanskrit terms glossed inline where they carry meaning; precise technical meaning must survive any future translation (Hindi/Odia are a separate decision, §13 D-14). Chart visuals, tables and timelines serve comprehension and are never prerequisites for asking.

**Long-term relationship.** Optional review points chosen by the person; no compulsory alerts, omen feeds or fear-based re-engagement. Preferences shape language and relevance, never conclusions. Remembering a preference, preserving a reading, recording an observation, evaluating a claim and training a model are different purposes with different controls, and the person can see which is which.

## 11. Proof

### 11.1 Four proof obligations, never merged

1. **Computational and source correctness** — reproducible under declared conventions; passages faithfully represented; verification tier disclosed.
2. **Explanatory and discriminative value** — a supported, personally specific distinction that a competent simpler reading misses; rivals and limits handled correctly.
3. **Empirical outcome performance** — frozen prospective claims vs a climatology baseline, per cell, above minimum n, with interval and coverage.
4. **Understanding and agency** — the person can explain why, recognize uncertainty, ask a better question and keep the decision. Valuable in itself; never a substitute for 1–3.

Safety, identity, faithful evidence and no-fabrication failures are non-compensable: no composite score offsets them.

**How a structural claim can be wrong.** Outcomes score time-bound claims; structural claims (a capacity, a tension, a mechanism) need their own falsifiers, and the definition names them: the cited fact is wrong or re-derives differently under the stated convention; the rule's modelled exception applies and was not honoured; the claim fails the blinded specificity test (a reader cannot tell it from a decoy chart's reading); an ablation shows the purportedly decisive relationship did not bear on the verdict; or a correlated witness was counted twice. A structural claim carries one of these as its falsifier (PPR-02), never an event it cannot have.

### 11.2 The north star — three instruments, no invented number

None of the three carries a target today; the first act of §14 is to measure the baseline. Thresholds follow measurement.

- **Kept-reading rate.** An interpretive reading is *kept* when its receipt reports `measured` on coverage, facts-by-reference, derivation chains, honest gaps and safety decision — where coverage is the **held fraction**: the share of classical units bearing on the question that are `served` or honestly `empty_for_this_chart`, versus `not_computed`, `not_joined`, `salience_floored` or `not_yet_available` (the `reading_checklist` receipt's own states; silent absence of a bearing unit is a defect and goes to zero first); every claim drills to a `fact_id` or verse in ≤2 hops; every significant claim shows its three alternatives and falsifier; and every forward claim it made appears as a `detected` ledger row that was confirmed or dismissed, never silently dropped. Measured weekly from receipts and the ledger, native's chart first.
- **Accountability fraction.** Of forward claims emitted: the share captured; of those, the share attended by a human; of closed windows, the resolved-or-can't-tell coverage — with `unverifiable` and `lapsed` reported as honesty and coverage figures, never as failure. Brier stays per row until the calibration write is unparked; **no aggregate accuracy is claimed while L5 is STRUCTURAL.**

  *Why the calibration number is null today, by design and by defect — so nobody mistakes its absence for modesty:* (1) the aggregate calibration write is PARKED — Brier is computed per resolution but `calibration_persisted` is always false (samiksha/outcome_recorder.ts:16-22); (2) L5 is STRUCTURAL — 50 build-frozen predictions, all nine multipliers `prior_only`, no empirical cycle run (L5_SEAL_AND_SHIP_REPORT_v1_0.md:23-27); (3) the file ledger holds about twenty rows against M6's gate of fifty closed windows (MACRO_PLAN M6); (4) the build-time matcher scores by window containment and never checks emission-before-event (mi_pramana.py:309-327); (5) pooling needs consenting charts beyond the native, and consent enforcement ships flag-OFF. The first milestone is not a better number; it is that the number exists.
- **The panel gate.** The three-conclusion test, blinded, n ≥ 3, verdicts logged not averaged. Passing is required before any audience beyond the native and before any superiority language appears anywhere.

Watched beside them, separately: engagement (never a proxy for benefit), optimism drift (predicted-favourable rate vs base rate over time), identical-question diffing, waiver rate on interpretation sets, register-lint firing rate. The retrieval plane's own hard targets are adopted as gate criteria rather than re-invented (RETRIEVAL_STRATEGY §7): every substantive table served or declared; a standard deep-dive in ≤ 10 umbrella calls; orientation in ≤ 2,000 tokens; every claim drillable to `fact_id` in ≤ 2 hops with zero dead ends; completeness receipts on 100% of planned investigations; a grounded verdict within ≤ 3 calls for every domain.

## Appendix A — Glossary

- **Evidence** — an L0 passage or rule, an L1 fact, an L2 signal/mechanism/contradiction, an L3 window, an L4 anchor; always cited by id, never restated.
- **Claim** — a typed statement about this chart carrying its evidence, ≥3 candidate readings, the selected one with rationale, its falsifier and its typed confidence.
- **Reading** — one turn's plain-language answer over its claims, in one register.
- **Receipt** — `AcharyaReadingReceipt` v1: the machine-checkable record a reading must earn (coverage, facts consumed, derivation chains, cross-domain block, evidence grades, honest gaps, safety decision, calibration disclosure, prose binding, provenance, hash). Every field earned by a detector or null.
- **Inquiry** — a question held open across readings and time, with a closing state; proposed extension of the Samīkṣā ledger and thread.
- **Observation** — an outcome recorded against a frozen time-bound claim: happened · did not · partial · can't tell. Human-confirmed.
- **Window** — the date range a forward claim is scored against; fixed at emission, never modified.
- **Register** — the single prose voice: acharya-grade and reader-legible, for everyone (D-14/D-15).
- **Disclosure class** — the consent/access axis: native_self · cohort_subject · acharya_reviewer · public (+ proposed consenting_individual). Never a quality tier.
- **Door** — a transport into the engine: Paripraśna, `prashna_ask`, raw MCP tools.
- **Floor** — the compiled minimum retrieval a plan must satisfy (whole-chart, daśā context); the B.11 enforcement point.
- **Escalation valve** — the one-line flag a proportionate lookup carries when its fact touches an active contradiction, firing yoga or open window (RS-4).
- **Frozen (asset)** — Nirmāṇa's five-marker evidence chain for a build asset. **Frozen (claim)** — a confirmed ledger row made immutable by trigger. Different words would be better; the overloading is inherited.
- **Structural mode** — L5's sealed state: calibration values empty by design until prediction→outcome data accrues; `evidence_grade = structural_no_calibration`.
- **Hard stop (HS-n)** — a safety rule that blocks output regardless of who asks.
- **Maturity states** — present → qualified → consumed → effect traceable → served → value evaluated; never averaged.
