# Madhav data plane — L0 Brahmagyan strategy

## 1. Decision and authority

`DP-SD-009` adopts `MADHAV_DATA_PLANE_VALUE_ARCHITECTURE_v2_0.md` **with
control amendments as the governing data-plane planning basis**. This decision
settles DP-P1 and permits the strategy task to issue sequential layer execution
briefs. It does not make the proposal a canonical product definition, modify
Product Definition v3.0, prove implementation, reopen a Nirmana campaign, or
authorize execution without a separately approved brief.

The amendments are binding:

1. The six foundation artifacts at execution commit `d838af455` control every
   execution brief, especially F01-F28, DP01-DP18, compatible generations,
   protected evaluation and the producer-ready/integrated/deployed/value gates.
2. The current generated planning denominator is 36 L0 writers plus four
   non-writers. Historical 12-asset and row-floor campaigns remain bounded
   evidence and preserved capital, not the current product-value target.
3. L0 acceptance means qualified, versioned, testable producer capability. Row
   volume, a lit tile, a build seal or source abundance cannot substitute.
4. No source, rule, convention, right or doctrinal scope is upgraded by this
   decision. Unsupported or disputed meaning remains explicitly unqualified.
5. The first L0 execution ends at `PRODUCER_READY`. L1 planning may consume that
   accepted contract; integration, deployment, consumer value and empirical
   evaluation remain separately evidenced states.

## 2. L0's distinctive contribution

Brahmagyan supplies the shared language and qualified knowledge from which the
rest of Madhav can calculate, interpret, temporalize, explain and later evaluate.
It must let every consumer answer five questions without inventing local truth:

- What entity, role, method, rule, source or reference quantity is this?
- Which exact meaning, convention, witness and semantic release applies?
- What prerequisites, exceptions, cancellations and disputes constrain use?
- Which service or representation can supply it, at what grain and context?
- What remains readable-only, unresolved, unsupported, unavailable or restricted?

L0 does not determine a person's fate, rewrite a chart fact, convert textual
testimony into probability, store private biography as global doctrine, diagnose
illness, establish remedial efficacy, or make a temporal/manifestation claim.

## 6. Required internal interplay

The qualified path is:

`source witness -> passage -> rule clause -> concept/entity IDs -> method and prerequisites -> exception/cancellation -> executable status -> generated adapter/service -> downstream consumed release`

The following separations are mandatory:

- ontology owns identity; specialized assets own qualified semantics;
- source indexes navigate, while passages testify and rule dossiers qualify;
- reference samples nominate, while numerical services compute at required grain;
- rule catalogs define possible operators, while L1 proves chart occurrence;
- event/activity vocabulary defines meaning, while L5 owns observations;
- priors/cohorts support controlled research, not individual forecasts;
- Vidhi floors trigger minimum investigation and omission checks, not truth.

## 7. First producer slice

The first slice is `L0-SLICE-RESOURCE-CONFIG-01`: a source-qualified semantic
package for a resource-creation-versus-retention question involving configuration
formation, cancellation and only the Bhavat Bhavam scope supported by admitted
sources.

The pinned competent legacy baseline is the coexistence at repository baseline
`9c497f3a7` of `BHAVAT_BHAVAM_MAP_VERSION = 1.0` in
`platform/src/lib/jyotish/bhavat_bhavam_map.ts`, the narrower non-generating L2
amplifier in `platform/python-sidecar/bodha_writers/`, and the D8 checklist claim
`bhavat_bhavam_rule: not_joined / computation absent`. Review finding S03 proves
the contradiction but does not prove why the missed user query occurred. The
slice must prevent both false blanket absence and false blanket implementation;
it does not claim to repair that unreplayed query.

Its exact grain is one row/object per
`semantic_release_id + method_id + rule_clause_id + source_passage_id + operator_scope_id`.
It is global/reference-scoped: `subject_id`, `chart_id`, birth data and personal
observations are forbidden. Every object must contain:

1. stable entity, alias, role, domain, outcome and method IDs, including the
   resource-creation/retention distinction;
2. work, edition/translation, passage, ancestry and existing rights/use status;
3. clause, operator, conjunction/disjunction, prerequisites, exceptions,
   cancellations, dispute and qualification state;
4. `READABLE_NOT_EXECUTABLE`, `QUALIFIED_EXECUTABLE`, `UNQUALIFIED_SOURCE`,
   `UNSUPPORTED_SCOPE` and `METHOD_INAPPLICABLE` as distinct detectable states;
5. exact convention prerequisites and downstream field/grain expectations for
   L1/L2, without a chart occurrence or interpretation;
6. semantic/interface version, generation, supersession, affected adapters,
   compatibility, stale behavior and rollback; and
7. the full lineage drill from concept to passage and qualified clause.

For Bhavat Bhavam, the only preapproved operator candidate is the preserved
non-recursive odd-house map `1->[1,7]`, `3->[2,8]`, `5->[3,9]`, `7->[4,10]`,
`9->[5,11]`, `11->[6,12]`; even primary houses have no derivation. The existing
restraints—never a generator, no chaining and never outranking the primary—remain
invariant consumer requirements, not new L0 testimony. Execution may mark this
operator `QUALIFIED_EXECUTABLE` only when an exact already-held passage, edition/
translation and existing rights/use status support that exact scope. The source
steward is `Strategy — Data Plane` under native source authority; execution may
link and classify existing evidence but cannot acquire a source, infer a right or
expand doctrine. If the pin is absent, the honest expected state is
`UNQUALIFIED_SOURCE`, and producer readiness requires the gap to remain explicit.

Predeclared changed outputs are: label-only/blanket-absence representations gain
the structured identity, witness, qualification, scope and generation fields;
Python/TypeScript producer adapters resolve the same IDs/states; and the fixture
distinguishes exact qualified, readable-only, unsupported and inapplicable cases.
Predeclared invariant outputs are: source text and historical rows are unchanged;
the odd/even map and non-recursion restraints are unchanged absent source-authority
return; alias presentation cannot change identity; no L1 formation, L2 amplifier,
D8 checklist, retrieval result, personal reading, timing, forecast or evaluation
is changed in this stage.

The fixture pack must therefore contain at least one positive qualified clause if
an admitted witness exists, one readable-but-not-executable clause, one missing-
witness case, one even-house inapplicable case, one prohibited recursive case, one
alias-parity case and one changed-generation rollback case. If no admitted witness
exists, the positive fixture is honestly `NOT_REACHABLE`; the stage may still
complete only if all unsupported states and the preserved narrower kernel are
correctly represented and no broader claim is made.

## 9. Acceptance

L0 is producer-ready only when:

- all 40 identities have evidence-backed current-state and terminal disposition;
- DP01 and DP02 contracts are versioned and their owners are unambiguous;
- relevant aliases and physical variants pass cross-language parity;
- passage, rule-clause, method, prerequisite, exception, dispute, rights and
  executable/readable status remain distinct and testable;
- the dignity/constant, concordance and Bhavat Bhavam conflicts are resolved as
  qualified variants or retained honestly as unresolved—never papered over;
- numerical and Panchanga services carry full context, precision and failure;
- restricted assets remain restricted and no safety/source-rights claim expands;
- the first producer slice and downstream fixtures pass qualification, relevant/
  irrelevant-input, duplication, missingness, boundary and omission tests;
- a change/invalidation/adapter-generation/rollback contract is proved;
- independent review finds no unowned HIGH/CRITICAL issue; and
- the diff proves no L1-L5 implementation, protected-main, production, deploy,
  campaign, Product Definition, CCD or canonical-manifest mutation.

The acceptance receipt may claim `PRODUCER_READY` only. It must explicitly mark
`INTEGRATED`, `DEPLOYED_OPERATIONALLY_ACCEPTED`, `CONSUMER_VALUE_DEMONSTRATED`
and `EMPIRICALLY_EVALUATED` as not reached.
