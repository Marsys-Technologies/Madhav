# Madhav: consumer product vision and definition

## 1. The product we are building

**Madhav is a Jyotish intelligence and discovery instrument for understanding a life, investigating a question and navigating unfolding possibilities. It connects the deepest qualified knowledge of the tradition with precise computation, relational investigation and accountable discovery—making patterns across structures, domains and time intelligible at a breadth and depth beyond unaided human working memory.**

Its center is personal understanding. Its long-term boundary is the meaningful inquiry, not a particular chart type, screen, technique catalogue or current implementation.

Its purpose is not simply to calculate a chart, reproduce a learned interpretation, or announce a future event. It is to help a person discover what is distinctive in their configuration, understand tensions and possibilities they could not previously connect, investigate when and how these may become relevant, and approach their life with greater understanding and agency.

The experience we want to earn is:

> “This has revealed something important and specific that I could not see before. It has connected what appeared unrelated, explained the distinction that matters, and helped me understand what to explore or prepare for. I can question it, follow its evidence, and return to deepen or correct the understanding.”

**The product's defining quality is earned insight.** Extraordinary breadth is the means; a meaningful, discriminating insight is the result. The richness should be felt in the understanding, not in the burden of reading a long report.

The second-pass ambition is stronger than an exceptional answer service: **Madhav should help a person discover the question worth asking, uncover the decisive relationship, explore the alternatives, and know what would make their understanding change.** A useful interaction can yield an insight, a better question, a clearer choice, a meaningful observation to make, or a well-founded recognition that the evidence cannot settle the matter.

“Beyond-Acharya” is the target standard for integration, investigation and discovery. It is not a claim that the product is already superior to every expert, knows destiny, or has established the truth of every astrological proposition. A universal claim about what no Acharya has ever conceived cannot be demonstrated by engineering scale. We can demonstrate specific capabilities and compare their results rigorously.

The user's aspiration for a profound, even spiritually moving experience belongs in the vision. The system earns that response through specificity, depth and integrity; it does not claim divine authority. Where a tradition offers spiritual interpretation, Madhav can explore it with seriousness and respect without presenting metaphysical interpretation as an observed fact.

### 1.1 The strategic shift

The previous work asked what Kāla should deliver and how its assets should be elevated. This definition asks the prior question: **what human value must the entire instrument deliver?**

The design order becomes:

**Consumer promise → signature experiences → intelligence obligations → component contributions → layer/asset contracts → separately authorized execution.**

Existing assets and layer plans supply evidence, useful capabilities and preservation obligations. They do not define the ceiling of the product. The six-layer implementation vocabulary remains governed; neither six layers nor any earlier six-view interface determines how a user must think or ask.

This document proposes the product-level reference for subsequent planning. It does not silently amend the canonical architecture or start, stop, reopen or redirect any execution task.

### 1.2 The dream, grounded in reality

I would want Madhav to make a life intelligible without pretending to contain it.

Imagine entering with a question about work and discovering a deeper tension between recognition, independence and security. The instrument can investigate whether that connection is actually supported, show where it appears elsewhere, distinguish enduring structure from the present chapter, and compare alternative expressions. The person can examine the account, challenge its premise, and find that one apparently powerful conclusion disappears when a duplicated piece of evidence is removed. Another, less obvious conclusion survives the relevant tests actually performed.

The result is not only something to read. It is something to explore: a relationship one can follow, an assumption one can vary, a period one can compare, a contradiction one can keep open, a question one can return to. Over time, the person gains a more coherent understanding and a better ability to question it.

Behind that experience, the instrument should be capable of bringing a scholarly library, a calculation laboratory, a critical interpretive dialogue and a carefully governed research programme to bear on the same inquiry. Their contributions must remain distinguishable even when the experience feels seamless.

The ambition has two directions: **depth for the individual and discovery for the discipline**. A neglected qualification in a source may illuminate one reading. A repeated failure across independently observed cases may reveal a limit in a rule. A new candidate relationship may suggest the next serious study. Personal data does not automatically move into that research programme; permission and study design are part of its legitimacy.

The realistic limit is not the present asset inventory. It is what can be sourced, calculated, meaningfully interpreted, affordably investigated and honestly tested. We should design boldly within that distinction.

## 2. The consumer and the value delivered

### 2.1 Who this is for

The immediate proving context remains the authorized native and existing permitted use. The target experience is designed to be reusable for consenting people with varied astrological knowledge, and useful to authorized practitioners and researchers. Expanding audiences, cohort research or public personalized service requires its own approved policy and operating readiness; a vision is not public-release authority.

These are use contexts, **not intelligence tiers**. Everyone entitled to a reading receives the same standard of investigation and grounding. Plain language is the default. Technical evidence is available through inspection, not reserved as a better answer for experts. This preserves the settled one-reading principle. [S3]

### 2.2 What a person should gain

- **Recognition without stereotyping:** an account of distinctive configurations and tensions, not flattering traits or an identity imposed by a chart.
- **Coherence without forced certainty:** useful connections among life domains, with contradictions and unexplained parts retained.
- **Temporal perspective:** an understanding of chapters, transitions, recurrence and qualified possibilities rather than isolated favorable dates.
- **Discernment:** the ability to distinguish plausible explanations, manifestations, choices and timing alternatives.
- **Agency:** a clearer relationship between interpretation, practical circumstances, values and choices—not dependence on the product for permission to live.
- **Continuity:** an understanding that can deepen across questions and years without silently rewriting previous conclusions.
- **Access to the discipline itself:** the ability to learn why an interpretation exists, where traditions differ, and what remains open to investigation.
- **Better inquiry:** discovery of a consequential question the person had not known to ask, and the next admissible information that could resolve it.
- **Stable understanding under uncertainty:** a clear account of what survives plausible input or method changes, even when finer details remain unsettled.

A person may seek money, marriage or a date, but may also seek meaning, vocation, family understanding, a repeated dilemma, a spiritual question, or a better question than the one with which they arrived. The product must not reduce all of these to event forecasting.

### 2.3 The person is not the chart

Keep chart-derived propositions, user-reported circumstances, personal objectives and observed events distinguishable. Circumstances make an answer relevant; they must not be laundered into astrological discoveries. A user's disagreement is a reason to investigate, not automatic proof that either the user or the computation is wrong.

The chart-first exploration remains available without asking users to supply goals that predetermine the answer. Goal-conditioned inquiry is also available when requested. Success, prosperity, stability and fulfillment are not universal scalar quantities; clarify their meaning when that distinction affects the answer.

The product should increase the person's capacity for discernment, not merely their dependence on future readings. Understanding a limitation, recognizing a weak inference or asking a sharper question is an important success. Meaning and contemplative exploration remain valuable even when the user has no event to predict or decision to optimize.

## 3. What beyond-Acharya means operationally

The benchmark is not a longer reading or a larger technique inventory. It is a demonstrated improvement in the quality of understanding.

| Dimension | Consumer-visible achievement | What does not qualify |
|---|---|---|
| Authentic depth | The actual formation, exceptions, cancellation, context and interpretive lineage matter to the answer. | A prestigious source name or a stock yoga description. |
| Integrative reach | Relevant divisions, relationships, systems, domains and time scales are connected without losing their identities. | Many methods listed, or the same fact counted repeatedly. |
| Personal specificity | A meaningful change in this person's configuration produces an appropriately different interpretation. | Statements that fit almost any person. |
| Discrimination | The answer explains why one interpretation or period differs from a plausible rival. | A positive score or an unsupported ranking. |
| Discovery | A previously unasked connection is found, checked and shown to matter. | Novel-sounding prose, graph centrality or rarity alone. |
| Temporal resolution | The answer distinguishes enduring potential, qualified activation, possible manifestation and feasible action. | Turning a broad period into an exact event date. |
| Intellectual continuity | Earlier claims remain inspectable; new information produces an explained revision. | Rewriting the story to fit every outcome. |
| Human clarity | Deep work yields an intelligible answer with useful next questions and inspectable evidence. | Jargon, mystique, verbosity or emotional intensity. |
| Inquiry intelligence | The instrument identifies a consequential unasked question and the smallest useful next investigation. | Endless suggested prompts or intrusive biography collection. |
| Robust understanding | It distinguishes conclusions that survive alternatives from those that depend on one fragile assumption. | A single confidence badge or the most convenient convention. |

The aspiration includes patterns an individual might miss because of the number of interacting relationships. The product must nevertheless show **which additional connection changed the understanding**. If a simpler competent reading is equally informative, extra complexity has not earned its place.

Not every question has an extraordinary answer. An ordinary result, a genuine tie, an unresolved disagreement or an inability to conclude must remain valid outcomes. Requiring a spectacular revelation every time would corrupt discovery.

## 4. The signature experiences

These experiences are obligations, not six mandatory screens, six report sections or six new assets. Users can enter through any natural question and move among them.

### E1 — A living portrait: “Help me understand my deeper pattern.”

Madhav develops an integrated, revisable portrait of distinctive capacities, tensions, competing tendencies and possible expressions. It explains how the same configuration may touch work, relationships, resources and inner life differently. It gives the user a way to understand a paradox rather than merely labeling a planet strong or weak.

“Living” means the understanding and its evidence can develop; it does not mean natal facts change with the conversation. The portrait must not become an unchallengeable personality diagnosis. A prior summary is navigation into evidence, not a replacement for it.

Give that portrait a **living atlas of understanding**: a navigable set of supported relationships, open contradictions, temporal episodes and questions. A person can ask “what is most distinctive here?” without first supplying a domain question, then follow the most consequential connections. Importance comes from explanatory reach, relevance, counterevidence and robustness—not from positivity, rarity or a global strength rank. The atlas is a product-level capability, not a requirement for a new graph database or another narrative writer.

**The revealing moment:** “These two aspects of my life are not simply inconsistent. I can now see the proposed relationship—and where that explanation stops.”

### E2 — A penetrating inquiry: “There is something I cannot explain.”

A question initiates an investigation, not a template fill. Madhav identifies what must be established, consults the whole-chart context where interpretation requires it, examines qualified evidence and serious alternatives, and asks only clarifications that could materially affect the conclusion.

It can question a premise respectfully: “There may be two separate issues here; the evidence for them differs.” It can follow a newly discovered relationship into another domain without losing the original question. It must also test whether a supposedly meaningful connection is absent.

Include a **question compass**: select a small number of evidence-grounded questions whose answers could materially improve the inquiry. Pair it with an **uncertainty-resolution step**: identify which unknown matters, whether resolving it would change the conclusion, and the least burdensome legitimate way to investigate it. Sometimes the missing item is a source qualification, not more private history. Sometimes the best answer is that further detail would not change the useful conclusion.

**The revealing moment:** the answer uncovers the decisive distinction that the initial question obscured.

### E3 — A navigable unfolding: “What is changing, and what should I prepare for?”

Madhav connects structural potential to time: sustaining periods, qualifying conditions, actual contacts, openings, inhibition, recurrence and transitions. It presents alternative manifestations and the reasons one interval differs from another.

Earliest, strongest, most robust and best suited to the user's constraints are separate comparisons. A favorable indication for visibility may coexist with pressure elsewhere. Relief from a continuing difficulty is not the same question as the occurrence of one favorable event.

Show a **conditional map of expression**, where supported: what appears enduring, what is active now, which alternative manifestations are plausible, and what would distinguish them. Separate preparation, opening, expression and consolidation only when a qualified model supports that sequence; do not force every event into four stages. Move between life-scale context and a narrow interval while preserving the same evidence identities. This is a map of interpretive possibilities, not a simulation of destiny or proof that a graph path causes an outcome.

**The revealing moment:** a list of dates becomes an intelligible sequence of possible developments, conditions and tradeoffs. Event probabilities or narrow dates appear only where their evidential basis supports that meaning.

### E4 — A thoughtful choice: “How should I approach this decision?”

Madhav compares real alternatives against stated objectives and constraints. It separates the astrological interpretation from practical assumptions, identifies stable versus fragile differences, and can conclude that astrology does not distinguish the alternatives.

Action timing uses action-specific doctrine and actual candidate intervals, not a generic auspiciousness total. Optional traditional practices can be explained with their source, intent, uncertainty and practical burden. They are not sold as guaranteed causal interventions or substitutes for qualified care and judgment.

Make comparison interactive. Vary one permitted input, disputed method, practical constraint or actual alternative, then show **what changed, what survived and why**. Keep input sensitivity, interpretive alternatives and action scenarios separate. Explore options that remain reasonable across several plausible assumptions; allow ties and genuine tradeoffs rather than forcing a winner. Removing a method tests dependence on it, not the causal effect of removing a planet from someone's life.

**The revealing moment:** the user understands the tradeoff and retains ownership of the decision, rather than being given an unexplained command.

### E5 — A truthful life-pattern inquiry: “What connects my history, and what does not fit?”

With permission, a person can supply an event log and examine alignment, recurrence, contradictions, unaccounted events and activations without observed outcomes. The interface accepts corrections, uncertain dates, partial outcomes and “cannot tell.”

The product clearly distinguishes a present-day interpretation of history from a reconstruction of what could have been known then. A story that used the outcome to select its explanation cannot later be advertised as a successful prior prediction.

Where appropriate and voluntarily accepted, convert an unresolved interpretation into an **observation brief**: the alternatives, the observation that could distinguish them, the relevant period and what counts as support, contradiction or insufficient information. Capture non-events and ordinary periods where relevant, not only memorable peaks. Record when the person saw the interpretation, since the reading may influence attention or behavior. This is an inquiry aid, not a self-experiment proving astrological causation or an obligation to document life continuously.

**The revealing moment:** some connections become clearer, and some cherished explanations are revised or rejected. Both are valuable.

### E6 — An enduring inquiry and knowledge observatory: “Go deeper. Challenge this. What changed?”

The user can reopen a claim, inspect its supporting chain, explore another tradition, vary a permitted assumption, compare an earlier reading and continue from the same evidence context. A disagreement becomes a durable investigation, not a discarded rating or merely a nicer paraphrase.

Anyone with access to the evidence can move from an intelligible answer into source passages, calculations, competing interpretations and research questions. Research facilities add operators and study controls, not a higher-quality consumer tier.

A continuing inquiry can retain its central questions, settled distinctions, disagreements and next useful tests. On user request, compare not only two answers but **two states of understanding**: whether a change came from corrected input, new evidence, another objective, a revised source/model or an earlier mistake. Optional update subscriptions should surface only material changes within the user's chosen scope. Unchanged evidence is a reason for quiet, not another reading.

**The revealing moment:** understanding accumulates while errors remain visible and correctable. The product becomes a dependable instrument of inquiry, not a sequence of disconnected performances.

### 5.1 Natal depth is the proving center, not the ultimate boundary

The target should admit different inquiry families when they earn their place: personal natal and temporal inquiry; Praśna/question or event inquiry; election of an action time; authorized relationship or multi-subject comparison; appropriately founded organizational or collective inquiry; and source-only or empirical research.

Each needs its own qualified method contract: what the subject is, which instant/location or records matter, how those inputs are established, what completeness means, which techniques apply, and what kind of claim can be made. These are horizon capabilities, not claims that all such families have valid implementations or demonstrated predictive value. The name **Paripraśna** for the chat does not itself implement a **Praśna** method.

Do not invent a birth time for an organization, manufacture a natal chart for a source question, or silently treat repeated question times as interchangeable. Pin the meaning and origin of each input; require method-specific treatment of ambiguous or repeated inquiries. When no qualified foundation exists, offer the supported scope rather than a synthetic certainty.

The proposed architecture is **method-native and question-led**. Existing natal whole-chart-read obligations remain binding today. Any future non-natal completeness contract and necessary charter amendment must be explicitly ratified before execution; this vision is not an exception that current callers can invoke.

## 6. The intellectual model: preserve knowledge, investigate meaning, discover responsibly

### 6.1 Authenticity is a chain, not a label

The product must distinguish:

1. **What the source says:** exact passage, source lineage, language, edition/translation, context and rights to use it.
2. **What a qualified rule means:** prerequisites, exceptions, scope, conventions and disagreements.
3. **What is computed for this subject:** reproducible facts, relationships, method identity and input precision.
4. **What interpretation is proposed:** how the evidence supports a particular meaning, with rivals and counterevidence.
5. **What is claimed about reality:** an observation or a bounded, testable prospective proposition.
6. **What evaluation has earned:** computational correctness, explanatory value and empirical outcome performance, separately.

An authentic passage does not make its prediction empirically validated. A correct calculation does not select one external manifestation. An elegant interpretation does not establish causation. These distinctions belong in the underlying system and should become visible when consequential, without drowning every answer in a disclaimer.

### 6.2 The truest available forms of the tradition

Pursue fidelity to sources and methods rather than smoothing all schools into a consensus. Maintain qualified variants, scope and unresolved readings. Use multiple methods where they contribute distinct, applicable testimony. Do not average incompatible meanings or count derivative sources as independent corroboration.

A qualified classical rule may need correction in its software implementation; a faithful implementation may still have unproven empirical significance. Both situations must be representable. Neither technological confidence nor reverence for a source should suppress investigation.

Make methodological disagreement productive: identify the precise premise on which interpretations diverge, what each method is entitled to address, the shared evidence and the observation or source adjudication that could discriminate. Several AI roles can assist source examination, computation, interpretation and attempted refutation; their agreement is not independent confirmation if they share the same evidence or model weaknesses. Multiple agents are a possible implementation choice, not a new definition of truth.

### 6.3 Three kinds of discovery

**Discovery within an individual:** find a previously unnoticed configuration, cross-domain relationship, exception, recurrence difference or counterexample within supported evidence. It can qualify for an answer when its chain and significance are established at the appropriate interpretive level.

**Recovery and reconstruction of knowledge:** locate and compare accessible sources; preserve variant readings; recover information from damaged extraction or fragmented references; propose missing links only as reconstructions. A newly located passage, an expert-adjudicated interpretation and an AI-generated reconstruction carry different labels and authority. Do not fill a textual gap with fluent invention and call it recovered tradition.

The horizon here is a **scholarship laboratory** connecting authorized textual witnesses, commentaries, diagrams, tables, terminology, translation alternatives and executable examples. It should direct expert attention to consequential disagreements: an omitted exception, an ambiguous term or a conflicting formulation. Its value is earned when a recovered distinction changes the correct downstream interpretation, not merely when another document becomes searchable. No surviving evidence means no claim to recovered wording or ancient authorship.

**New research hypotheses:** search for patterns or rules not already established in the corpus, then test whether they survive alternative explanations, appropriate controls and independent evaluation. A surprising correlation remains a candidate; it does not automatically become doctrine or a consumer forecast.

### 6.4 The discovery discipline

Use a separated sequence: **candidate → source/logic examination → rival and negative-case testing → independent review/evaluation → bounded admission, revision or rejection**.

Register searches and hypotheses sufficiently to account for multiple testing. Separate exploratory cases from held-out or prospective evidence. Include failed discoveries, comparison baselines, sample limits and permitted populations. New models, reconstructed rules or learned parameters enter serving only through separately approved, versioned releases.

The ambition is to become an instrument with which the discipline can discover what is supported, what is uncertain and what fails. It must remain capable of finding that a proposed rule contributes nothing or does not generalize. That is advancement of knowledge, not failure of the mission.

### 6.5 A contrastive observatory: learn where an interpretation works and breaks

The research horizon should include a consented, provenance-preserving **case observatory**, organized around meaningful structural differences, comparable questions and observation coverage—not an anecdote search for people who resemble the current user.

It should support questions such as: “Where does this pattern appear without the proposed outcome?”, “Which condition distinguishes these otherwise comparable cases?”, and “Does the interpretation add information beyond what is already known from ordinary circumstances?” Positive and negative analogues belong together. Similarity must be defined for the inquiry; a nearest-neighbor match is not an individual's event probability, and a population association is not a personalized destiny.

One result would be a **map of a rule's support, exceptions and unresolved scope**: textual basis, computational qualification, interpretive uses, comparable successes and failures, study limits and conflicting findings. This can direct scholarship toward the most consequential missing condition instead of indiscriminate corpus expansion.

A second result would be **research-question and study design assistance**: identify competing explanations, relevant baselines, prospective observations, sampling needs and leakage risks before collecting or fitting more data. Expert judgment, appropriate statistical review and independent evidence remain necessary. Cohorts, protected data access and model promotion need separate authority; personal use does not imply participation.

Fix primary case-selection and similarity rules before revealing the evaluation outcomes. Preserve practical/contextual comparisons as well as astrological ones: the product must be able to find that ordinary circumstances explain a pattern just as well. Public biography is not automatically a reliable research record; removing a name does not necessarily make a detailed birth/event history safe to expose. A refined rule must retain the original, its proposed changes and failures, rather than narrowing its scope after every miss to manufacture success.

Related subjects, repeated events, shared source records and repeated readings must retain their dependence; they cannot silently become additional independent corroborating cases.

Instruments that inspect structured evidence, compare cases and organize studies are plausible engineering targets. Whether the resulting discoveries are reproducible or useful is an open empirical question. That distinction is part of the design, not a reason to omit the ambition.

## 7. How the intelligence should work for a user

The target is a coherent investigation over evidence, not simply retrieval followed by eloquent summarization.

```text
Person's question + authorized context + consequential clarifications
                               ↓
Frame the inquiry and its applicable completeness obligations
                               ↓
Retrieve qualified facts, relationships, doctrine and temporal evidence
                               ↓
Form competing interpretations → test, compare and expand as needed
                               ↓
Deliver a clear answer with claim-level support and material limits
                               ↓
Continue, challenge or revisit the same investigation

Separate path: frozen claims + admissible observations → evaluation
              → independently admitted future knowledge/model versions
```

The AI supplies question understanding, investigation planning, synthesis and hypothesis generation. Deterministic tools supply the calculations and structured operators for which they have authority. Source and applicability contracts constrain interpretation. Validators check specified properties; they are not oracles proving that a grounded conclusion is true.

For current natal interpretation those completeness obligations include the governed whole-chart protocol. Future method-native contracts follow §5.1; they are not active bypasses.

### 7.1 “All relevant data” means accountable coverage

It does not mean indiscriminately loading every row into a model. It means the system identifies the supported evidence families and material relationships relevant to the question, preserves mandatory contradictions, expands when a finding creates a new obligation, and reports consequential omissions.

Evidence compression may reduce repetition, not erase a decisive exception. Top-K relevance or response budget cannot silently turn an incomplete investigation into a complete one. The system can stop with a useful partial answer, showing what remains unexamined and how that limits the conclusion.

A factual lookup remains proportionate under the existing frame-check/escalation rule. An interpretive answer retains whole-chart discipline. The user need not select a quality level. [S1, S2]

### 7.2 A rich answer need not be a long answer

The default answer should make clear, as applicable:

- The most important conclusion and why it matters to this question.
- The distinctive configuration or relationship that supports it.
- The meaningful alternative, contradiction or limitation.
- The temporal or practical distinction that changes the interpretation.
- What further information, observation or assumption would change the answer.

These are quality obligations, not mandatory headings. Technical terms are used where they carry meaning and are explained inline. Evidence, a timeline, comparison or graph should be inspectable when it clarifies something important. Internal asset names and build diagnostics stay out of ordinary prose.

### 7.3 The meaning of an answer must survive its channel

Paripraśna and managed MCP `prashna_ask` should share investigation and evidence semantics for equivalent authorized questions. Transport, presentation and budgets can differ; omitted coverage must be explicit. Identical prose is not required.

Direct MCP tools expose the same qualified evidence identities, operators, provenance and limits. An external AI controls its own investigation and prose, so Madhav cannot promise its full managed-reading guarantees for a raw-tool-only answer. That boundary must be understandable to the integrating client. [S3, S4]

### 7.4 Make the experience tangible

The following is a **hypothetical interaction design, not a chart reading or a result of an executed analysis**. No dates or chart facts are asserted. A real response could use this shape only after establishing its supporting evidence.

> **User:** “When will my financial strain get over?”
>
> **Madhav:** “The nearest opening would not, by itself, justify saying the strain is over. The important distinction in this reading is between an opportunity arriving and money becoming reliably available. We should compare temporary relief with a sustained change, not simply select the first favorable period.”
>
> **User:** “Why are you making that distinction?”
>
> **Madhav:** “The earlier candidate is supported more strongly for opportunity than for realization. Here are the specific relationships and timing conditions behind that distinction, the evidence against it, and the practical assumption on which the comparison depends.”
>
> **User:** “Actually, by strain I meant low profitability, not cash collection.”
>
> **Madhav:** “That changes the question. I will compare profitability rather than reuse a cash-availability conclusion. The earlier answer remains recorded with its original meaning; the new conclusion needs a fresh comparison.”

The evidence expansion must supply the real configuration, qualified rules, intervals and counterevidence—not merely repeat those labels. At any point, the user can ask **“What is strongest here, what is speculative, and what would change your conclusion?”** This inspects the same reading; it is not a quality or depth tier.

### 7.5 The understanding should be explorable

The minimum durable unit should be an **inspectable insight**, not only a paragraph: the claim, relevant relationships, source/derivation, alternatives, material uncertainty, context/version and ways to compare or challenge it. This is a proposed semantic contract that should extend existing claim/receipt mechanisms where suitable, not automatically create a parallel store.

The user should be able to ask: “Show the relationship,” “Why this interpretation rather than the other?”, “Which detail matters most?”, “What survives if that method is excluded?”, and “What would help us settle this?” The answer should invoke the relevant comparison or retrieval operation rather than improvise a second persuasive explanation.

Use the smallest useful presentation: a short explanation, a before/after comparison, a temporal interval or a focused relationship diagram. An impressive three-dimensional cosmos is not the objective. A precise relationship made understandable is.

### 7.6 Importance, effort and stopping are part of intelligence

Within the supported inquiry, prioritize unresolved distinctions that could change the answer, alter its scope, expose counterevidence or improve robustness. Establish this contribution with explicit qualitative rules first; do not invent numerical information-gain or confidence scores for sophistication's sake.

The instrument must distinguish a completed answer, a useful partial answer and a proposed extension. Extensive work can be resumable under applicable authorization and resource controls, with the current result and unresolved questions preserved. It must not require endless computation or personal disclosure to be useful. Nor should a user's access to a particular budget become an excuse to present an under-investigated answer as meeting the full quality standard.

“Stable” means stable over the specified inputs, methods and ranges actually examined. Several models can share a blind spot; agreement is not a guarantee against it. Where an exploration was sampled or bounded, the result must not imply that every possible alternative was eliminated.

## 9. The experience surrounding the insight

### First use and incomplete information

Explain what information matters and why. Do not make a long biography or flawless birth time a universal entrance requirement. Identify what can be answered now, what is sensitive, and which additional information would improve a particular question. Rectification is an explicitly governed hypothesis process, not an invisible convenience.

When inputs are uncertain, establish the **stable core first**. Show which relevant conclusions hold across the permitted range and which do not. Narrow inputs only when it materially helps the inquiry and can be done legitimately; sometimes an interval or an admitted inability to resolve it is the right product result.

Support language and accessibility needs without reducing intellectual quality. Precise technical meaning must survive translation. Chart visuals, tables and timelines serve comprehension; they are not prerequisites for asking a question.

### During an investigation

Show useful progress and consequential clarifications, not internal reasoning transcripts or a parade of tool calls. Let the user understand whether the system is calculating, comparing, checking a source or handling an evidence gap. A failure or resource limit should preserve usable work and explain the supported partial result.

### After the answer

Make “why?”, “show the source,” “compare,” “challenge this,” “what changed?” and “this did not happen” natural continuations. Preserve a durable reading identity, material assumptions, evidence/version context and corrections. A later source or model change must not silently alter an earlier answer.

A saved prior reading can guide retrieval but cannot serve as independent corroboration of itself. User preferences may shape language and relevance, not tune conclusions toward praise or agreement.

### Long-term relationship

Offer optional review and user-chosen follow-up; no compulsory alerts, omen feeds or fear-based re-engagement. Sensitive history remains under clear access, retention and reuse rules. Refusing a claim, withholding a false precision or saying “we do not know” is compatible with a valuable long-term relationship.

A useful update should explain the material change: a corrected calculation, new qualified source, revised constraint, scheduled user-requested review or an admitted model change. A subscription to one inquiry is not permission for an unrestricted feed of predictions. The person can pause, narrow or end it without losing control of their record. A portable reading should carry the relevant evidence and limits; sharing it does not grant access to another person's private data.

Remembering a preference, preserving a reading, recording an observation, evaluating a claim and training a model are different purposes with different controls. Withdrawal and correction must account for derived summaries and caches as well as source records. Reconcile protected historical integrity with explicit retention/deletion policy; do not promise both unconditional erasure and universal immutability. Personal continuity must be selective and legible to its owner, not an invisible accumulation of intimate context.

## 10. Boundaries that protect the vision

These are product behaviors, not a disclaimer pasted onto an otherwise deterministic answer.

- Do not guarantee wealth, marriage, childbirth, disease, death, a partner's intentions or other external outcomes from a chart.
- Do not diagnose health or fertility, declare medical safety, or use astrological timing to displace appropriate care. Financial and other consequential choices retain real-world evidence and qualified judgment.
- Do not frame suffering, identity or family circumstances as proof of moral inferiority, blame or deserved fate.
- Do not claim supernatural access, divine endorsement or certainty about a user's spiritual status.
- Do not coerce practice, spending, disclosure or dependence. Traditional practices require qualified explanation and a clear distinction between tradition and established efficacy.
- Do not infer research, model-training, cross-subject or provider-sharing permission from participation in a personal reading.
- Do not hide unavailable evidence, failed checks, unsupported methods or model disagreement behind a reassuring score.
- Do not treat a selected log of memorable events, expert approval or an emotionally powerful response as proof of prospective accuracy.

These boundaries leave ample room for ambitious, specific, tradition-grounded interpretation. They prevent apparent profundity from replacing earned understanding.

## 11. How we will know the product deserves the vision

No current accuracy percentage, superiority score or delivery date is asserted here. Establish baselines and evaluation protocols before setting quantitative improvement targets.

### 11.1 Separate proof obligations

**Computational and source correctness:** are calculations reproducible and sources faithfully represented under the declared conventions?

**Explanatory and discriminative value:** does the richer investigation produce a supported, personally specific distinction that a simpler baseline misses? Are alternatives and limitations handled correctly?

**Empirical outcome performance:** where a prospective claim is made, does it outperform appropriate baselines on admissible independent evidence, with meaningful uncertainty and scope?

**Consumer experience:** can people understand, question and use the answer while retaining agency? This is valuable in its own right but does not substitute for the other proofs.

### 11.2 Evaluation portfolio

| Evaluation | What to measure | Essential control |
|---|---|---|
| Source and calculation fidelity | Passage-to-rule support, conventions, reproducibility, boundary behavior, qualified missingness. | Wrong/ambiguous source, uncertain input and unsupported-method cases. |
| Personal specificity | Whether material configuration changes alter the appropriate claim. | Blinded different-chart and irrelevant-input comparisons under equal budgets. |
| Incremental depth | Additional correct distinctions, counterevidence and useful connections beyond competent simpler readings. | Ablation of the purportedly valuable relationship or method; verbosity and jargon not rewarded. |
| Expert comparison | Blinded judgments of source fidelity, interpretation, discrimination and missed connections. | Qualified reviewers, common case/context, transparent rubric and disagreement; no universal extrapolation. |
| Evidence delivery | Whether decisive evidence survives retrieval, compression, adaptive passes and synthesis in managed channels. | Sentinel distinction plus duplicate-evidence and context-isolation tests. |
| Temporal and outcome evaluation | Frozen claim performance at declared outcome/horizon/population; calibration where justified. | Held-out/prospective partitions, simple baselines, knowledge-time controls, false alarms, misses and observation coverage. |
| Discovery quality | Candidate yield that survives independent tests, source scrutiny and replication. | Multiple-search accounting, negative results and independent cases. |
| Understanding and agency | Comprehension, ability to explain why, recognition of uncertainty, useful follow-up and retained decision ownership. | Satisfaction and emotional intensity are not correctness metrics. |
| Continuity and reliability | Faithful prior-claim recovery, explained revisions, failure recovery, latency and actual task completion. | Stale/mixed builds, partial outages and disconnected follow-ups. |
| Question and uncertainty intelligence | Whether a proposed question or requested input resolves a consequential ambiguity with acceptable burden. | Already-sufficient questions, irrelevant private information and unknowns that cannot be resolved. |
| Contrast and stable-core understanding | Whether users can explain which claims changed, why, and which survived controlled variation. | Irrelevant changes, disputed-method removal, tied choices and unsupported causal interpretations. |
| Observation and method-native inquiry | Whether criteria discriminate alternatives and the selected inquiry contract is appropriate. | Hidden-outcome cases, missing natal inputs where irrelevant, repeated-question ambiguity and unavailable methods. |
| Attention and learning value | Whether optional updates improve understanding and whether users become better at questioning conclusions. | No-update baseline, unchanged state, unwanted interruption and engagement measured separately from benefit. |

Track these separately. A weighted “beyond-Acharya score” that masks a critical failure would defeat the purpose. Safety, identity, faithful evidence and no-fabrication failures are not compensated by a beautiful answer.

For predicted outcomes, unknown is not false; event time is not knowledge time; repeated readings of the same fact are not independent trials. All indirect fitting inputs—including rectification, selected priors, summaries and model versions—must respect the evaluation boundary.

### 11.3 Three proving journeys

The first complete benchmark set should include more than one temporal example:

1. **Integrated understanding:** “Why do I appear highly capable in one setting yet repeatedly face a different outcome in another? Show the strongest explanation and challenge it.” Proves structure, distinctiveness, source fidelity and alternatives without requiring a forecast.
2. **Structure through time:** “When could my financial potential become relevant, why has it not yet translated into relief, and how do the nearest and most robust windows differ?” Proves the user's central L3 expectation without conflating money, activation and relief.
3. **Accountable continuity:** “Compare my earlier reading with what actually happened; which claims held up, which did not, and what genuinely changes now?” Proves preserved claims, honest misfit handling and separated evaluation.

These are test prompts, not readings of the native's chart and not claims that the target is already delivered. Each must include an evidence-poor or premise-false variant and a precise lookup control. Add the wider P01–P21 portfolio as the relevant capability contracts mature.

The second-pass proving extension adds: “What important question am I missing?”, “Which of these unknowns would actually change the conclusion?”, and “Compare these options after excluding the disputed method.” A future non-natal case must also demonstrate an appropriate method-native contract without a fabricated natal input. These additions test genuinely new value rather than simply longer answers to the old prompts.

## 13. From product definition to asset elevation

### 13.1 The contribution contract

Every asset, tool, view, service and operating mechanism must answer:

1. Which consumer question, experience or indispensable enabling obligation does it serve?
2. What unique information, relationship, operation or reliability guarantee does it contribute?
3. Who consumes that contribution, at which exact semantic boundary?
4. What would become less correct, less discriminating, less understandable or less reliable if it were removed?
5. Which evidence proves correct use and incremental value, and which proof remains pending?
6. Should it be retained, corrected, enriched, integrated, split, consolidated, kept as an alternative, or superseded after migration?

A foundation primitive need not answer a natural-language question itself. Its contribution can be indirect and indispensable. A compatibility view, source archive or reliability service can also justify its existence. Conversely, an impressive asset with no qualified consumer path has not delivered consumer value.

Use the layer-value-elevation skill to perform that research. Use the existing asset-elevation execution process only in separately authorized implementation tasks. Skill use does not override the project lifecycle.

### 13.2 The next sequence

**First, agree this product definition.** Refine its promise, breadth, signature experiences and quality bar with the user. Do not start by debating asset names.

**Second, freeze a versioned consumer acceptance portfolio.** Develop representative questions, expected distinctions, illustrative answer forms, negative cases and proof requirements. It is a testable baseline, not a claim to cover every possible question.

For the second-pass additions, use the low-cost discriminating tests in §12.1 before committing to large implementations. Keep a requirements horizon distinct from the next executable slice. Define data/source admission tracks for scholarly and non-natal capabilities without delaying useful work behind an unlimited promise of completeness.

**Third, derive the whole-product capability and gap map.** Reconcile current evidence against this definition. Trace layers and cross-cutting services together so a successful producer cannot mask a broken retrieval or consumer seam. Preserve historical acceptance while identifying new obligations.

**Fourth, qualify upstream contracts in dependency order.** Use L3's already researched demands alongside the broader product demands to review L0, then L1, then L2. Reconcile the L3 plan with accepted inputs rather than speculative downstream repairs. Define L4/L5, serving and evaluation interfaces early, but preserve governed build and feedback boundaries.

**Fifth, issue bounded execution briefs in other tasks.** Each names the approved consumer outcome, preservation choices, exact contracts, owners, tests and lifecycle gates. Revalidate actual deployment and use before claiming the product gained that capability.

Upstream-first does not mean every imaginable source or technique must be perfect before any useful end-to-end capability can exist. It means the required producers are qualified for the selected capability, while stricter campaign gates remain binding.
