## 2. Ratified product requirements inherited by the plane

The plane must enable a connected path from question to delivered understanding:

`question -> applicable concept -> qualified rule -> canonical fact -> structural relationship -> temporal mechanism -> manifestation or explicit gap -> delivered finding -> protected evaluation`

Binding requirements:

- Beyond-Acharya is an ambition measured by applicable coverage, relational depth, source
  fidelity, temporal discrimination, manifestation specificity, controlled comparison and
  accountability; it is not a current superiority or accuracy claim.
- Astrological completeness is accountable against explicit applicable-concept and relationship
  obligations. Applied, inapplicable, unavailable, unqualified, contradictory and unexplored
  remain distinct states.
- Facts, qualified rules, structural inferences, forecasts and evaluation evidence retain their
  different authorities. Repeated representations of one root do not become independent support.
- Relevant domains, participants, polarity, exceptions, cancellations, alternate interpretations,
  uncertainty, precision, conventions and source lineage must survive every material handoff.
- The same structural identity must persist from whole-chart meaning through temporal activation
  and manifestation. Nearest, strongest, robust and merely present are different selections.
- Permitted predictions are specific when earned, frozen as delivered and evaluated without
  hindsight or outcome leakage. The chain may stop honestly at a missing manifestation bridge.
- Life observations receive maximum justified, purpose-qualified use without becoming global
  doctrine, rewritten natal truth or same-claim prospective input.
- Paripraśna and managed MCP owe equivalent authorized evidence and completion semantics;
  raw MCP owes a narrower qualified-evidence guarantee. Findings must survive persistence,
  continuation, replay and permitted export.
- Source testimony, executable rule, computed configuration, interpretation, real-world claim and
  evaluation remain distinct. Method-native coherence precedes comparison across schools.
- Existing safety exclusions, authorization boundaries, consent posture and C1/C3/PPR-31
  firewalls remain unchanged.

## 5. Assumptions, contested findings and evidence limits

### Working assumptions

- The current six-layer ownership remains viable; value gaps are primarily semantic qualification,
  relationship preservation, integration and delivery until evidence establishes otherwise.
- Existing physical tables/writers can often satisfy richer logical contracts without a new asset.
- The financial/configuration plus Bhāvat Bhāvam/cancellation journey is the highest-fan-out
  first structural slice, before temporal and manifestation extension.
- Current generated inventory is the planning denominator; future layer audits may discover
  additional services or historical authorities without changing the target by fiat.

### Contested or unresolved interpretations

- Bodha's old handoff describes persisted outputs as deterministic structure and puts narrative at
  serve time; the new product target requires structural meaning and alternatives. The L2 plan must
  define typed structural propositions without moving unsupported prose into writers.
- Current L1 contains astronomical, classical-rule, approximate and judged outputs. L1 membership
  does not by itself settle epistemic type, although downstream values still may not be silently
  re-derived.
- Project Architecture v2.2 still describes an older five-layer/L2.5 conceptual model and stale
  counts. Reconciliation of that product-wide governance surface belongs to the parent strategy.
- Similar structural, temporal, publication, source-navigation and model components may be
  complementary stages, projections or duplicate authorities. No consolidation is decided before
  exact semantics, callers, history and discriminating value are proved.
- Source observations S01-S24 are code-observed risks/opportunities, not established live incidents.
  The user-reported missed-chain/Bhāvat Bhāvam case remains unreplayed.

### Evidence not established here

Production population and generations; deployed behavior; complete direct/transitive/dynamic
caller reach; source rights; doctrine correctness of disputed operators; empirical predictive
performance; consumer comprehension; cost/latency of proposed enrichment; generalization beyond
the repeatedly studied charts.
