`DP-SD-013` approves this L1 strategy and its paired execution brief under

The following controls are binding:

1. The exact execution base is the accepted L0 terminal revision `f6fed12c7`.
2. The current generated inventory is 19 L1 writers and zero formal L1
   non-writers. Historical `ga_chart_service` is adjacent service capital whose

   the formal denominator.
3. L1 owns reproducible chart/context calculation and explicitly typed
   rule-derived facts. It does not own L2 structural interpretation, L3 temporal
   integration, L4 manifestation, L5 evaluation or final synthesis.
4. L1 membership is not an epistemic type. Astronomical calculation,

   other rule-derived products do not become raw astronomy by location.
5. L1 consumes the accepted L0 semantic release and qualification states without
   restating, overriding or locally repairing L0 meaning. The accepted first L0
   Bhāvat Bhāvam package remains `UNQUALIFIED_SOURCE`; L1 cannot emit a positive
   qualified application from it.
6. The first L1 execution ends at `PRODUCER_READY`. No L2 edit, retrieval
   cutover, managed-channel integration, deployment, database rebuild, active
   campaign mutation, consumer-value claim or empirical claim is authorized.

## 2. L1's distinctive contribution

Gaṇita turns a qualified method and authorized calculation context into facts a
later investigator can trust, compare and challenge. Every material L1 result
must let its consumers answer:

- exactly which subject/chart/build, instant, location and method produced it;
- which ayanāṃśa, node, frame, house reference, varga formula, karaka school,
  unit and precision apply;
- whether it is measured/calculated, deterministically derived, rule-derived,
  approximate, unsupported, unavailable, floored or failed;
- whether a named configuration is formed, partially formed, not formed,
  cancelled, inapplicable or unqualified, with its participants and clauses;
- which components caused a condition or strength conclusion, including real
  zeroes and disagreements; and
- whether a different admissible input or convention changes the fact, crosses
  a boundary or leaves it invariant.

The earned product contribution is not more rows. It is preventing wrong-chart,
wrong-frame, false-zero, label-only-formation, discarded-precision and local-
rederivation errors while making the exact chart facts needed by Bodha, Kāla,
Phala, discovery and controlled comparison available under one reproducible
contract.

## 6. Internal interplay and adjacent boundaries

The qualified producer path is:

`accepted L0 identity/convention/rule state -> authorized calculation context -> numerical primitive -> typed fact/condition/configuration/clock -> stable generation and dependency lineage -> later consumer`

Mandatory separations:

- `ga_positions` supplies roots; derived writers cite exact root fact identities
  rather than re-deriving positions under hidden conventions.
- conditions retain constituents and units; totals and normalized judgments do
  not replace them or count as independent evidence.
- `ga_vargas` owns divisional calculation; downstream assets cite its facts and
  formula rather than reconstructing a second varga authority.
- `ga_yoga` can assert occurrence only from an admitted, executable L0 rule.
  Catalog labels/citations alone remain candidates. Cancellation and stronger
  manifestation are separate states.
- `ga_structural` emits calculated relationship primitives; L2 owns their
  connected structural meaning. `ga_vichara` retains its registered judged-
  structure role but declares that epistemic class explicitly.
- `ga_dashas`, `ga_sade_sati`, `ga_tajaka` and transit anchors retain exact
  clocks/geometry; L3 owns multi-clock activation and nearest/strongest logic.
- birth Pañcāṅga, arbitrary-day/place Pañcāṅga, Praśna moment and Muhūrta
  interval are different operations even when they share terminology.
- medical, Āyurdāya, Vāstu and dormant Praśna capital retain current safety,
  purpose and activation restrictions.

## 7. First producer slice: L1-SLICE-RESOURCE-CONFIG-01

The first L1 slice converts the accepted L0 resource/configuration package into
an exact, non-personal chart-context fact bundle for later structural use. It is
not a Bhāvat Bhāvam application and does not edit L2.

### Fixed entry

- L0 semantic release `l0.semantic.2026-09-13.1`, digest
  `665096a74a59ea7e0e50ce98fc685899b89f325aca0d91c214f0040e4d259dd1`;
- L0 resource/config package `l0-resource-config-g1`, digest
  `d516aecff9d4e05d929dc7fd71a113fd5c53d1f6ea1eb2582caafd3a339c279a`;
- Bhāvat method state `UNQUALIFIED_SOURCE`, never-generator/no-chain/primary-
  precedence restraints, and L2 application ownership;
- deterministic synthetic, non-person fixture(s) only; no stored personal chart,
  private row or life observation; and
- current L1 producer baseline at `f6fed12c7`, with historical results treated as
  regression evidence, not expected truth.

### Required output envelope

At minimum the slice must version and test:

1. calculation context: synthetic fixture ID, chart-context ID, build/generation,
   exact instant/location/timezone and input precision, engine version, sidereal/
   tropical frame, ayanāṃśa, mean/true node, house convention, D1 plus selected
   varga formula/domain, and compatible L0 release/package pins;
2. canonical position/house/lord/karaka-role inputs required for the resource
   question, each with stable fact ID, unit, grain, verification class and source
   dependency;
3. separate condition components and reasons, including a real zero, unavailable
   or inapplicable fixture so those states cannot collapse;
4. divisional comparison and at least one precision/boundary perturbation with
   changed/unchanged/unsupported classification;
5. configuration occurrence schema with participants, satisfied/failed clauses,
   exception/cancellation slots and `formed | partial | not_formed | failed |
   method_inapplicable | unqualified_source`; and
6. the L0 Bhāvat operator carried as `UNQUALIFIED_SOURCE`/not applied, while the
   exact primary-house facts and context needed by a future L2 application remain
   available.

The implementation may use engineering fixtures to test the configuration state
machine, but it may not label a doctrinal occurrence `QUALIFIED_EXECUTABLE`
without an admitted L0 rule dossier. If no current L0 rule satisfies that gate,
the slice exits successfully with the positive doctrinal arm `NOT_REACHABLE` and
the numerical/context/negative-state arms proved.

### Required discriminating tests

- Same semantic identity expressed by an admitted alias leaves facts invariant;
  mean/true node remains distinct.
- Changing a material instant/convention/precision input changes only declared
  dependent facts and records why; cosmetic order/alias changes do not.
- Wrong chart/build/ayanāṃśa/varga/generation cannot join silently.
- A relevant condition or clause changes the occurrence state; an unrelated fact
  cannot; duplicate/shared-root representations do not inflate support.
- Zero, unavailable, floored, unqualified, inapplicable and computation failure
  remain distinct through serialization and replay.
- Exact clock or boundary values remain timestamps/intervals until presentation;
  no date truncation in the producer package.
- A deliberate numerical error cannot become Aries/0°, empty-success or a normal
  fact.
- Prior compatible generation remains replayable and the rollback selector does
  not rewrite historical rows or delivered readings.

## 8. Compatibility, correction and rebuild contract

L1 execution must publish a compatible-generation matrix for the 19 assets and
the first slice. At minimum it distinguishes:

| Change | Required consequence |
|---|---|
| L0 identity/physical variant or L1 context-key change | Invalidate affected fact/configuration IDs and adapters; never coerce prior rows into the new identity. |
| Alias-only L0 addition | Adapter refresh only unless identity resolution changes; calculations remain compatible. |
| Calculation/engine/formula correction | Stale exact dependent facts and derived consumers; preserve prior generation for replay; rebuild only under separate operational authority. |
| Precision/location/timezone/house/varga/node change | New context identity and controlled comparison; do not overwrite a different context. |
| Rule/source qualification change | Re-evaluate only dependent configuration states; preserve previous evidence and delivered snapshots. |
| Projection/ranking change | Does not rewrite producer facts; later consumer validation required. |
| Observation/outcome change | No effect on L1 facts; firewall violation if it enters calculation. |

Writers remain on frozen `WriterBase`, caller-owned transactions, layer
idempotency and shared Swiss-state serialization. Existing migrations are
immutable. New schema, if unavoidable, is forward-only, additive/compatible by
default, and accompanied by rollback/read-compatibility evidence. No destructive
rebuild or live migration is authorized here.
