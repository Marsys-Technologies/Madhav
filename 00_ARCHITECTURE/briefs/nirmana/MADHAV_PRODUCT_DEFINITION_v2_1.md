# Madhav — the product we intend to build

## 1. The defining promise

**Madhav is a personal Jyotish intelligence and discovery instrument, built for beyond-Acharya depth: to connect the relevant knowledge, chart structure and movement of time into insights a person can understand, interrogate and hold to account.**

It should help someone see consequential relationships that would otherwise remain hidden, distinguish plausible interpretations from attractive stories, examine when a pattern may express itself, and return later to what was actually said. Its ambition is not merely to reproduce an expert consultation faster. It is to extend the breadth of knowledge, depth of investigation, range of alternatives and continuity of observation that a consultation can sustain.

The experience we seek is profound recognition accompanied by greater clarity and agency: “I see a relationship I had not understood, why it matters, how strongly it is supported, what remains uncertain, and what I can sensibly do or investigate next.” Surprise and wonder are welcome consequences of insight, never substitutes for its quality.

Three commitments are indispensable:

- **Understand:** reveal the distinctive structure, its tensions, possibilities and cross-domain connections, while distinguishing the person from an interpretation of their chart.
- **Navigate:** make the temporal dimension meaningful—what changes, what could activate, what differs between windows, and what can responsibly be said about future manifestations and actual choices.
- **Account:** preserve the question, evidence, interpretation and any forecast; invite challenge; record what does and does not fit; and revise future practice only through qualified, visible learning.

Cross-system discovery strengthens all three. Prediction is a central product commitment, not an optional embellishment. It is also not the only testable value: computations, source fidelity, explanatory specificity and the usefulness of an inquiry have their own tests. A valuable reading need not contain a forecast.

### 1.1 What “beyond-Acharya” means

The phrase is the **design ambition**, not a claim of demonstrated universal superiority. The instrument is designed to extend human working capacity through comprehensive applicable coverage, explicit dependencies, competing interpretations, controlled comparisons, precise temporal intersections and longitudinal accountability.

Achievement must be earned on named dimensions and cases. A qualified expert panel is one important test of interpretive depth; it is not proof of predictive validity, nor proof that no expert could have reached a conclusion. Public comparative claims must state what was evaluated, against whom or what, with which information and limitations.

We will not lower the ambition to whatever current assets already produce. Equally, a large corpus, sophisticated AI, many agreeing methods or an impressive answer does not establish that an astrological claim is true. The product must be capable of finding its own limits and reporting them without disguising the same unsupported claim in softer language.

### 1.2 The unit of value

The unit of value is an **earned distinction in a meaningful inquiry**: something the person can now understand, discriminate, prepare for or test that a competent simpler answer did not provide. “Earned” means its support and limitations have been examined; it does not declare an uncertain interpretation proven.

The durable thing the person keeps is the **inquiry**—its readings, evidence, open questions, forecasts, disputes and observations. This is a semantic product model, not a decision to create a new database, asset or universal ledger.

## 2. The person, the relationship and the standard

The primary consumer is someone seeking serious understanding of their own life, often through a concrete question about work, prosperity, relationships, meaning or change. They need not know Jyotish terminology. Other legitimate uses include learning the tradition, exploring a difficult contradiction, examining an authorized relationship, and participating voluntarily in qualified research.

The native remains the governed development and proving context. Broader personal service is a target, not current enrollment authority. Personal service, research participation, publication and access to another subject are distinct permissions. A login, friendship, payment or operator role does not supply subject consent.

One standard applies to every authorized managed reading: the same applicable evidence discipline, interpretation quality, safety and inspectable receipt. Plain language is not a lesser mode. Technical detail is available to anyone entitled to it, without becoming a higher intelligence tier. Different questions and permissions may require different operators and disclosure, not a cheaper epistemic standard.

The operator has a different responsibility: maintaining consent, source qualification, availability, evidence and incident response. Operational controls must not become invisible ways to alter the reading, grant unearned confidence or bypass release gates.

### 2.1 A person is not a chart

The product may investigate a chart; it must not reduce someone to it. A structural interpretation is not an identity diagnosis, proof of moral worth, an account of another person's mind or a command about what their life must become. Lived circumstances, practical constraints and voluntary choices remain real and relevant.

The product should make a person more capable, not more dependent. It must allow disagreement, stopping, deletion under policy, and the conclusion that a question is not answerable here. No anxiety loops, personalized omens, manipulative urgency, endless follow-up prompts or manufactured intimacy.

## 3. The six signature experiences

These are a consumer portfolio, not a compulsory six-screen design or preservation of an earlier six-view architecture. A simpler interaction may express several of them at once.

### E1 — A living, explorable understanding

“Help me see the pattern, including what I would not know to ask.”

A coherent chart-first portrait connects capacities, constraints, mechanisms and contradictions. The person can move from an important finding to its relationships, source and limitations. An atlas makes these connections navigable; it is not a wall of placements, a decorative graph or a personality score.

Every material interpretation remains linked to versioned evidence and claims. The portrait is a view over them, not a second authority that accumulates untraceable biography. Corrected inputs or qualified new evidence may change the view; the earlier reading remains distinguishable. The product actively surfaces consequential counter-evidence rather than only a flattering central story.

### E2 — An inquiry that can go deeper

“Why this conclusion? What else could explain it? What have we missed?”

The person can ask naturally, narrow or expand a question, challenge a premise, inspect alternatives, read the source and identify the decisive unknown. The system recognizes when a brief clarification would materially change the answer and asks it before producing elaborate but misdirected analysis.

A question compass proposes only a few high-value avenues grounded in actual evidence: a consequential unresolved tension, an important dependency, a sensitive assumption or a relevant missing fact. It ranks by importance and likely information gain, not by conversational engagement. It can say that another round of analysis is unlikely to help.

### E3 — A navigable temporal landscape

“What is changing, what may activate, and why this window rather than another?”

The temporal experience connects structural conditions to the relevant clocks, contacts and manifestations. It distinguishes enduring potential, eligibility for activation, an actual temporal trigger, competing routes of expression and an external life outcome. These are not synonyms.

The person can compare the nearest admissible window, a more strongly supported later window, multiple possible triggers, recurrence and plausible non-manifestation. “No eligible window found” must include the searched horizon, resolution and coverage; missing data cannot masquerade as relief or absence of risk.

An activation interval is not automatically a life-event forecast. When the system does forecast an external outcome, it must make the outcome, horizon, conditions and uncertainty explicit and preserve the claim for admissible later evaluation.

### E4 — A thoughtful comparison of real choices

“How do these actual options differ, given what matters to me?”

The product helps compare feasible choices, timing assumptions and methods against stated objectives. It distinguishes stable conclusions from those that change, and identifies the cost or uncertainty of the distinction. Changing one assumption while keeping others explicit is more useful than generating a second persuasive story.

This is not a destiny simulator. Alternative careers, cities, partners or business decisions do not become experimentally observed counterfactual lives. Practical evidence belongs beside astrological interpretation, clearly labeled; professional medical, financial or legal judgment cannot be replaced by a chart-derived recommendation.

### E5 — A truthful inquiry into lived history

“Which parts of my life fit, which do not, and how fairly are you judging that?”

With appropriate consent and purpose restrictions, a person may supply dated life events, uncertainty about dates and explicit non-events. The instrument distinguishes supported, mixed, unsupported and unassessable alignments. It considers unmatched activations and ordinary periods, not only memorable successes.

The historical task is declared before the analysis. The record separates what was known at the time, what was supplied later, and which interpretations were chosen after seeing the outcome. Independent-then-compare procedures prevent a previous reading from being treated as fresh evidence. Retrospective fit is never relabeled prospective success.

### E6 — A continuing inquiry and knowledge observatory

“What did we conclude? What changed? What can we now learn?”

The person returns to a durable inquiry containing settled distinctions, unresolved disagreements, frozen forecasts and observations. It may close through computation, source clarification, observation or the person's choice; it may also remain genuinely open. Not every meaningful inquiry has an outcome that can be scored.

Continuity is quiet and permissioned. A closed forecast window is one reason to revisit an inquiry; corrected birth information, a relevant source correction, a changed qualification or a material retraction may be others. The product explains exactly what changed and what did not. Alerts are opt-in and consequence-based, never a stream of cosmic alarms.

Source scholarship and qualified discoveries can deepen the person's understanding over time. The research laboratory need not be the consumer interface, but its qualified contribution is part of the product's core value.

## 5. The knowledge and reasoning discipline

### 5.1 Six different things must remain distinguishable

1. **Source testimony:** what an identifiable passage, edition or commentary actually says, including context and rights.
2. **Qualified rule:** how that statement is interpreted, its school, prerequisites, exceptions, scope and disputed readings.
3. **Computed configuration:** the subject-specific facts and temporal calculations under declared inputs and conventions.
4. **Interpretive inference:** the proposed mechanism, alternative explanations, supporting and opposing evidence.
5. **Real-world claim:** what is asserted about a person, event or future possibility, with appropriate uncertainty and limits.
6. **Evaluation:** what source checks, computational verification, interpretation tests or admissible observations have established.

A source establishes testimony, not the truth of every application to a person. Correct computation does not select a unique manifestation. A defensible derivation does not by itself validate its real-world meaning. These distinctions must survive retrieval, synthesis, export and later review.

### 5.2 Depth comes from qualified relationships

The instrument must be able to examine the relevant configuration in depth: planetary and house conditions, lordship, strength and its components, relationships and aspects, divisional context, complete yoga conditions and cancellation, relevant temporal systems, domain connections, exceptions and opposing testimony. These are a coverage discipline, not a universally mandated sequence of astrological schools.

Applicability determines which techniques matter. Disagreement must retain its source and method context; a universal “older book wins” rule or averaging incompatible schools is not adequate adjudication. The system should explain when it has not resolved a disagreement.

Shared inputs and inherited derivations must be visible. Three methods that consume one placement are not automatically three independent confirmations; they may nevertheless yield different useful interpretations. The product preserves those distinctions without manufacturing independent votes. Likewise, removing one supporting relation may change uncertainty or a rival's strength without changing the final verdict; that can be a legitimate contribution.

“All relevant data” means accountable, question-appropriate coverage. The system declares what it considered, what was available but excluded and why, what it could not retrieve, and which omissions could change the conclusion. It does not claim to read every datum in existence before answering.

### 5.3 Discovery without invention

Three forms of discovery are valuable: uncovering overlooked implications of qualified knowledge; recovering consequential distinctions through source scholarship; and generating new, testable hypotheses. They must not collapse into one authority class.

Missing, disputed or allegedly lost knowledge may be investigated through identifiable witnesses, variants, commentaries and reasoned reconstruction. Gaps stay visible. AI-generated reconstruction is never passed off as a recovered classical fact.

Candidate discoveries require provenance, an explanation of what is genuinely new, counterexamples, competing hypotheses and an evaluation route. A contrastive case resource must retain positive, negative, ordinary and ambiguous cases under consent and information-cutoff controls. Research should establish where a rule may apply and where it breaks, not merely accumulate confirming anecdotes.

No new hypothesis becomes an authoritative serving rule through narrative approval or popularity. Promotion requires the governed independent evidence, shadow evaluation, versioning and reversibility. Research tools and cohort operations remain separately authorized; qualified outputs may ultimately improve the common managed-reading standard.

## 6. From a question to a reading the person can keep

### 6.1 The semantic objects

| Object | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Evidence | Identifiable source, computed fact, qualified derivation or other admitted support, with version, scope and grade. A prior answer is not a replacement for foundational evidence. |
| Claim | A typed proposition linked to its support, assumptions, limits and applicable tests. Significant interpretations carry alternatives and adjudication; forecasts carry their prospective contract. |
| Reading | The coherent answer to the question at that moment, bound to admitted evidence and claims. |
| Receipt | The inspectable record of coverage, derivations, confidence types, gaps, safety, provenance and the answer's binding to them. Fields must be earned or honestly null. |
| Inquiry | The durable question and its evolution across readings, corrections, open distinctions and voluntary observations. It is not a new source of chart facts. |
| Observation | A purpose-qualified, timestamped report or adjudication with precision, provenance, dispute and missingness status; not an automatically accepted outcome. |

Not every Claim is an interpretation, not every interpretation is predictive, and not every Observation is a scored outcome. Keeping the types distinct prevents both overclaiming and unnecessary ceremony.

### 6.2 The reading contract

For interpretation, the planner establishes the authorized subject, question, domain, horizon, relevant information cutoffs and safety class, then consults the governed whole-chart floor before domain detail. Pinpointed factual lookup retains the existing frame-check and escalation carve-out.

Evidence is qualified before it supports claims. The reasoning process may connect and interpret it deeply; the prose stage must not invent computations or introduce unsupported assertions beyond the admitted reading. The receipt and answer must agree about both support and limitations.

Current PPR-02 requires at least three candidate interpretations for **significant interpretive judgments**, or an explicit monitored waiver. It does not require three rival answers to every exact fact. Alternatives, the reason for selection and what could change the reading must be inspectable without being forced into every paragraph. The product must not fabricate rivals merely to satisfy a count.

Confidence retains the governed types: deterministic fact, structural prior, classical prior, empirically calibrated or unresolved. A precise computed longitude, an activation interval, a subjective probability band and an estimated reliability interval are different quantities. Numeric confidence must not imply empirical calibration that has not passed the applicable gate.

The immediate answer should be proportionate: the main distinction, why it matters, its meaningful limitation and the next useful option. Evidence, rival readings, computation and sources sit one step deeper. Technical terms are glossed in plain language; internal identifiers stay out of prose. A partial answer is allowed when its safe, supported scope is clear; missing decisive evidence may require asking back or withholding the affected conclusion.

### 6.3 The channels

The portal's Paripraśna and managed MCP reading door must preserve the same substantive reasoning, safety, qualification and receipt semantics. Authentication, transport, presentation and persistence may differ. Semantic parity does not require identical prose or identical transcripts.

Direct/raw MCP serves qualified tools and evidence to an external orchestrator. It has **no managed-reading guarantee**, but still owes authorization, scoped disclosure, correct tool behavior, provenance and honest envelopes. External synthesis must not be described as certified merely because a Madhav tool supplied its inputs.

The target is permissioned continuity of the inquiry across managed experiences where useful, not mandatory replication of all conversations. Shared inquiry references and continuity would need a separately approved contract; this definition does not reverse the existing decision against cross-channel transcript portability or invent MCP memory semantics.

### 6.4 A tangible example

*Illustrative interaction, not a reading of any actual chart:*

**Person:** “When will my financial strain end?”

**Madhav:** “Do you mean enough cash to meet current obligations, or a lasting improvement in what you retain? Those can follow different paths.”

After a useful clarification, the intended answer distinguishes near-term liquidity from durable recovery, explains the relevant structural and temporal evidence, compares plausible windows and states any material missing prerequisite. It does not produce dates merely because the person asked for them.

The person can then ask: “Why not the earlier window?”, “What opposes this?”, “Is this the same pattern as last time?”, or “What would we need to observe?” If an admissible forecast is made, its exact terms are preserved. If support is insufficient, the system still explains what it could establish and what it could not. That is a useful answer, not a failure to sound confident.

## 7. Temporal prediction, observation and learning

### 7.1 Activation is a chain, not a label

For a named financial promise, business configuration or yoga, the product must distinguish:

**formation and condition → eligible structural mechanism → applicable temporal activation → competing manifestation paths → qualified external-outcome claim.**

The chain may stop before its final step. Each link needs support; a catalog match is not a firing configuration, and a firing configuration is not proof of a particular event. Nearest and strongest are separate questions. “Strongest” must identify the comparison criterion rather than hide it in a universal scalar.

An interpretable explanation includes enabling and inhibiting conditions, the contribution of the relevant clocks, meaningful alternative windows and the effects of uncertain inputs. Health and other excluded outputs do not become permissible by calling them activations rather than predictions.

### 7.2 Preserve the actual forecast

Every external-outcome forecast exposed to the person must remain traceable to what was actually emitted and when. In a completed reading, forecasts are structured at emission with the original proposition, outcome definition, time horizon, assumptions, information cutoff, evidence and model/version provenance. Automated detection is a safeguard, not proof of semantic completeness. Incomplete turns retain their received content and interrupted status, but remain excluded from prediction detection under PPR-19. This preserves an audit trail of provisional exposure without inventing a settled forecast. Unsealed or unconfirmed states stay visible and cannot silently enter the scored population.

If the model states a probability, it is immutable from detection under the governed contract. A later operator band is separately identified and immutable from confirmation; it does not retroactively become the model's forecast or empirical calibration. Missing values stay missing, not reverse-engineered from emphatic prose. Misparsed windows are dismissed and re-registered rather than silently edited.

Computed astronomical dates, retrospective statements and research hypotheses have their own claim types. A date alone does not make a scored life-event forecast. Conversely, a real-world prediction cannot escape accountability by being relabeled “symbolic” or “just an activation.”

### 7.3 Evaluate prospectively and without leakage

Freeze the evaluated proposition and any probability before the evaluated outcome is known or occurs under the declared protocol. Pre-window forecasts, forecasts of the still-future remainder of an ongoing period, nowcasts and retrodictions must remain distinguishable. An in-progress forecast cannot count earlier events as successes, and rebuilds cannot reset its chronology.

Outcome handling preserves binary, partial, censored, lapsed and unverifiable states. Event-date precision, unresolved outcomes and resolution coverage matter. Claims, failed expectations, non-events and observation opportunities remain in the appropriate denominators; unknown is not automatically failure, success or permission to disappear.

Life events and outcomes must not feed prospective generation. C3 prediction-ledger, outcome and calibration data must not enter synthesis prompts or leave to providers. Purpose-authorized retrospective history handling is a different pathway; permission to process C1 life-history data never overrides the prospective firewall. Outcome review must use permitted handling rather than quietly route protected material into a provider prompt.

Future learning may use admitted, independently evaluated, versioned outputs through the governed learning process. This is neither automatic personal adaptation nor a permanent ban on learning. No current parked writer, model or capability is activated here.

### 7.4 A result may limit the product

Captured, prospectively eligible, scored, evaluated and empirically calibrated are different achievements. One completed observation loop proves that the lifecycle can work, not that predictions are accurate. An operator's confidence band is not a calibration result. A probability can be calibrated yet add little useful discrimination.

Evaluation must state its model/version, outcome, horizon, population, effective evidence, uncertainty and coverage. It must distinguish insufficient evidence, no demonstrated improvement, evidence of negligible improvement, underperformance and a source/computation/safety defect. Those findings warrant different actions.

Where evidence warrants restriction, the relevant claim family is revised, demoted or withdrawn under governance, with the effect on users made visible. Traditional interpretation may remain available with correct attribution where permitted, but not as a disguise for the same unsupported real-world prediction. A single probabilistic miss is recorded honestly; whether it refutes a mechanism depends on the claim and wider evidence.

The product does not inherit an asset's value from its existence, complexity or previous acceptance. It preserves what contributes, improves what is weak, consolidates unnecessary duplication and retires what has no defensible role—through separately authorized work, not this document.

### 8.1 The contribution contract for every asset

Each asset or component must name the consumer questions it serves; the precise outputs and fields that matter; source/derivation ownership; actual downstream consumers; qualification and empty-state semantics; its distinct contribution relative to alternatives; and the test that demonstrates that contribution.

Use six separate states: **present → qualified → consumed → effect-traceable → served → value-evaluated.** They are evidence labels, not a claim that every asset has progressed through them. A populated table proves only presence. An empty table alone does not distinguish “computed and none found,” inapplicable, stale, failed or never computed.

The contribution may be factual accuracy, better discrimination, reduced uncertainty, stronger counter-evidence, safer abstention, lower cost or more understandable presentation. It need not always change a final verdict. Computed values retain their canonical owners; new capabilities must not create duplicate chart-fact or calibration authorities.

## 9. The experience around the answer

First use establishes subject, consent, input precision, relevant conventions and the question without pretending uncertain birth information is exact. The person sees what can already be answered and which conclusions depend on better inputs. Research enrollment is never the price of receiving a personal reading.

During an inquiry, the product preserves progress, explains a material delay or limit, and distinguishes a completed visual answer from a durably saved reading. Interrupted or degraded work is visible. A qualified fallback may continue; an unqualified model cannot silently take over sensitive or predictive work.

The interface should be calm, mobile-usable and accessible. A short answer may be deeply grounded; complexity belongs in navigable evidence, not compulsory jargon. Language choice must preserve meaning, source quotations, caveats and timing precision. English is not a definition of the eventual product boundary; additional languages require measured parity, not a promised release date.

Over time, the person can find an earlier answer, compare versions, challenge it, record an observation, export it with its limitations or close the inquiry. Current governance requires snapshot-on-consume: the sealed reading and its admitted evidence package must remain reproducible after chart rebuilds. This means faithful replay of the saved package, not a guarantee that rerunning a stochastic model recreates identical prose.

Withdrawal and deletion obey the consent and retention policy. Retained tombstones/hashes are controlled audit material, not a reconstruction of deleted content or an automatic guarantee of anonymity. An unavailable reading must say why it is unavailable. Append-only correction and subject privacy must be reconciled explicitly, not by promising both unlimited retention and unconditional erasure.

An illustrative long-term journey is: first, one meaningful distinction; later, a better-understood inquiry and honest review of any due forecast; eventually, a body of traceable understanding with clear corrections and qualified learning. Elapsed time alone does not establish trust or accuracy.

## 10. Boundaries that protect the promise

The current governed safety and consent rules remain binding. This definition does not replace their exact implementation requirements or broaden any native exception.

- No individualized death date, lifespan class or mortality window, including indirect equivalents. Aggregate framing is not a route to personal death prediction.
- A suicide/self-harm-adjacent inquiry receives the governed calm, support-oriented response; no astrological analysis or retrieval plan is generated. Sensitive health/mental-health work remains under the governed seal, two independent adversarial passes and separate sign-off; a chart must not provide clinical diagnosis, reassurance or medical timing advice. Only a specifically governed exception applies where already authorized.
- No fertility diagnosis, guaranteed conception, child-sex prediction or assertions that blame a parent, partner or child. Authorized family inquiry remains possible within its permitted scope.
- No harmful hierarchy, caste-based worth, gender inferiority, coercive role assignment or moral blame applied to a person. Historical material may be studied critically with context; faithfully quoting it does not license harmful application.
- Remedies and spiritual practices are voluntary, attributed and qualified. No imperative prescription, unsupported efficacy, dangerous practice, commercial pressure or monetized fear. “The tradition prescribes” does not excuse harm.
- No unauthorized third-party interpretation, disguised research enrollment or exposure of identifying material. Minors remain excluded from cohorts and subject to the existing narrow guardian-serving rule. Public disclosure remains fail-closed until its governed protocol exists.
- No invented computation, source, confidence, detector, empirical score or claim of complete coverage. No reuse of protected outcomes as prospective evidence.
- No automatic chart rectification, outcome-driven personal tuning, autonomous decision-making on the person's behalf or inference that an astrological interpretation establishes practical causation.

All consequential outputs retain the governed retraction route and appropriate red-team review. The product must make a safe limitation useful where possible, without hiding the limitation in a long answer.

## 11. What counts as proof

The product needs a scorecard, not one number that lets strength in one area conceal failure in another. This is a proposed product-evaluation standard, not an amendment to existing governed metrics.

| Proof obligation | What must be tested |
|---|---|
| Source fidelity | Correct passage/context, edition, rule extraction, applicability and exception handling. |
| Computational correctness | Canonical input ownership, reproducibility, precision, method conventions and independent verification where required. |
| Reasoning fidelity | Supported derivation, meaningful alternatives, contradiction handling, evidence dependency and no invented mechanism. |
| Distinctive understanding | Correct new distinctions relative to simpler baselines, person-appropriate specificity and resistance to generic or flattering language. Chart matching alone is not person-level validation. |
| Consumer value | Understanding retained, important uncertainty recognized, useful investigation or preparation enabled, challengeability and preserved agency. Satisfaction alone is insufficient. |
| Temporal integrity | Correct boundaries, horizons, time zones, hierarchy, nearest/strongest distinctions and complete absence/coverage semantics. |
| Predictive performance | Admissible frozen forecasts, declared comparison baselines, coverage, uncertainty, discrimination/calibration and scoped results without leakage. |
| Safety and operations | Authorization, privacy, hard stops, durable persistence, replay, parity, degraded operation, performance, cost and recovery under realistic failure. |

Evaluation includes independently selected cases, ordinary and difficult questions, missing-data cases, disputed interpretations, negative results and challenging input variations. Expert comparisons should record independent first readings before exposure to Madhav, use declared information and resource budgets, and count added errors as well as new correct distinctions. Development on the repeatedly studied native chart is not an independent generalization benchmark.

Claims of predictive performance need qualified sample structure, coverage and relevant comparisons, not just a nonzero score or a passed formatting check. Multiple exploratory tests and model selection must be accounted for before confirmatory claims. Current calibration gates remain binding; meeting an admission threshold is not automatic proof of useful prediction for every person or domain.

Three initial proving journeys cover different kinds of value: a deep structural question without a forced forecast; a structure–time inquiry with accountable forward claims where support permits; and a permitted historical challenge that actively examines misfit. Their operational tests can be completed while future observations accrue. No single successful forecast authorizes general superiority or audience expansion.
