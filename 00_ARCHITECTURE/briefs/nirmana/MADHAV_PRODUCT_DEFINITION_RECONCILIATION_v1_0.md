> Historical review, preserved for traceability. See the [v3.0 adoption record](MADHAV_PRODUCT_DEFINITION_V3_REVIEW_AND_ADOPTION_v1_0.md) for the final product authority. Original review conclusions below retain their original scope and date.

## 1. Recommendation and review boundary

Recommend a v2.1 synthesis: preserve the consumer-first, future-facing ambition of v1.1 and adopt v2.0's stronger accountability, durable inquiry model, operating responsibilities and baseline discipline. Do not replace the target product with today's available operators, or ratify v2.0 verbatim.

The resulting ambition remains **beyond-Acharya depth**: exceptional integration, discriminating insight, meaningful temporal understanding, accountable prediction and disciplined discovery. This is an aspiration to demonstrate, not a present universal superiority claim. Neither evocative language nor a panel's approval establishes predictive validity.

## 2. Corrections the successor must carry

### 2.1 Mission and proof

MACRO_PLAN §Ultimate goal lists chart depth, beyond-working-memory connections and calibrated, correctable prediction. Keep all three explicit. The source does not literally assign equal weighting. Reject v2.0's claim that prediction is the only checkable leg: source fidelity, computation, interpretation specificity and discriminating explanations have their own tests, as v2.0 itself recognizes. Remove the unsupported assertion that no existing astrology product records accountable predictions. No market-wide verification supports it. [G1]

### 2.2 Typed claims and proportionate inquiry

Preserve separate types for computed facts, interpretations, activation conditions, external-outcome forecasts, historical statements and research hypotheses. A computed daśā boundary is not a life-event forecast requiring human probability confirmation. Capture and freeze prospective outcome claims; do not score every sentence containing a date. Model probability, operator confidence and empirical calibration remain distinct. [G2, G3]

PPR-02 applies to **significant interpretive judgments**, requiring three candidates or an explicit monitored waiver. PPR-05 makes rivals and falsifiers inspectable affordances, not obligatory prose. PPR-15 preserves the factual-lookup frame-check/escalation carve-out. The opening promise must not become “every fact has three alternatives” or “all data is read before any answer.” Future non-natal methods need separately admitted completeness contracts; this vision does not waive existing natal discipline. [G2]

### 2.3 Privacy classification is not evaluation purpose

The safety annex classifies LEL life events, birth data, sensitive conversation and health/relationship content as **C1**; prediction ledgers, outcomes, Brier and calibration are **C3**. v2.0 incorrectly groups life events and sensitive readings into C3. C1 may enter authorized synthesis under provider controls; C3 must not enter synthesis prompts or leave to providers. Separately, PPR-31 forbids life events/outcomes feeding prospective generation. Thus authorized retrospective inquiry is not universally banned, while C1 classification never permits outcome leakage. Any future review projection needs its own compliant contract, not an assumed exception. [G2, G4]

### 2.4 One standard, distinct authorization and transport

Keep one reading-quality standard and one prose register. Managed portal/MCP evidence and receipt parity is a target invariant; direct MCP does not inherit managed final-reading guarantees. It still requires authorized access, truthful envelopes and correct operators. “No reading guarantee” is not “no safeguards.” Distinguish subject kind, disclosure class, operator role and transport; the proposed audience rings mix these dimensions. New personal-service audiences require an explicit policy decision, not inferred research enrollment. [G2, G3]

### 2.5 Reproducibility and deletion

PPR-20 already requires **snapshot-on-consume** and immutable evidence packages so settled readings survive chart rebuilds. A current storage gap is a conformance gap, not reason to redefine prior readings as inherently irreproducible. Distinguish recovery of an exact preserved reading from generating new prose under a later model. Preserve governed withdrawal and tombstone rules; hashing alone does not establish anonymity or justify indefinite personal-data retention. Do not promise universal erasure and universal content immutability simultaneously. [G2]

### 2.6 Ratified versus proposed decisions

NCD-2's five-artifact separation is ruled; the CURRENT architecture, decision register, baseline and verification matrix have different jobs. NCD-3 already ratifies receipt v1, the B.4 serving trigger and typed confidence. NCD-7 already ratifies calibration activation defaults; NCD-8 already rules pre-dispatch spend caps. These are conformance inputs, not fresh product choices. Conversely, OT-4 guest build rights and OT-6 MCP durable memory remain in the register's **proposed, awaiting ruling** section. Do not describe either lean as settled doctrine. [G3]

### 2.7 Baseline, outcomes and learning

Measure the as-is product first. Turning several safety, receipt and interpretation flags on is an intervention requiring separate testing and release authority, not a read-only baseline. Compare authorized candidate configurations afterwards. A closed-and-reviewed forecast demonstrates lifecycle operation; one outcome does not prove predictive skill or justify a blanket pause in upstream improvement while waiting for life events. [G2, G3]

For prospective studies, enforce frozen claims and knowledge cutoffs. Requiring emission before an entire broad window starts is a proposed study restriction, not existing universal doctrine: a later forecast may validly concern the still-future remainder if that remainder and scoring rule were frozen before its outcome. Never reset claim time through rebuilds. [G1]

Raw observations must not automatically feed current generation. This does not permanently prohibit governed learning: MACRO_PLAN permits independently supported, shadowed, versioned, reversible future updates. Existing collect-only/no-leakage boundaries and parked calibration writes remain binding until separately authorized work changes them. [G1, G2, G6]

## 5. What ratification would and would not do

Ratification would establish a strategic product reference: consumer promises, signature experiences, target responsibilities, evidence standards and priorities for the next planning pass. It would permit deriving an acceptance portfolio and contribution map within the user's requested strategic scope.

It would not amend roles, grants, consent classes, model/provider policies, safety gates, spend caps, macros, calibration methods, L5 promotion authority or Nirmāṇa membership. Any such change requires a separate ADR or the applicable existing governance mechanism, with exact affected rules, owners and verification. No application implementation belongs in this strategic conversation.

The next useful sequence is: agree the definition; establish reference inquiries and as-is evidence; distinguish target importance from readiness; qualify upstream and serving contracts together; produce bounded execution briefs in other tasks. Prospective observations accrue alongside permitted engineering work. Empirical promotion waits for evidence; unrelated correctness and consumer-value work need not wait for an outcome to occur.

Independent AI review helped identify questions and inconsistencies. Its votes are not an Acharya panel, an empirical study or certification. A future expert panel must be genuinely conducted under its approved protocol; agreement does not replace independent outcome evaluation.
