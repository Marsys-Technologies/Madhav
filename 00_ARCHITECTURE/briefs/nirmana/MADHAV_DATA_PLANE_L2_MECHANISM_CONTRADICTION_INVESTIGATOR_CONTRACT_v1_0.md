# L2 mechanism, contradiction and investigator contract

## Graph and mechanism

`bo_bimba` owns typed configuration nodes; `bo_karanajala` owns signed edges and
contradictions; paths/motifs own ordered discovery candidates; a
`bo_yantra_mechanism` object is admitted only when an executable qualified rule
can return false. Otherwise it remains a candidate. Centrality, topology,
alphabetical order, first fact or fixed verification constants cannot supply
causal, temporal or truth authority.

Mechanisms retain all member roles, ordered supporting/opposing relationships,
necessary/optional structural conditions, shared-root groups, cancellation,
contradictions, rivals, missing discriminators and scope (including D1-only).
They expose no activation gate, probability, event window, manifestation or
causal verdict.

## Multidomain and contradiction

`bo_sangati`, `bo_cdlm_summary` and `bo_pratijna` retain the complete domain-role
map, both sides of each pair, positive and negative drivers, and underlying IDs.
Summary direction is navigation. Doṣa proxies require qualification before use
as negative evidence. Compatible tensions, genuine contradictions, method
disputes and unresolved rivals remain distinct and no winner is forced.

## Grounding, discovery and quality

Grounding binds the exact source/rule, qualification, target kind/id and citation
granularity; abundance is not corroboration. Embeddings, anomaly scores, broker
scores, lenses, gestalt and digests remain navigation/candidate surfaces that
require hydration. Low-ranked high-consequence and decisive second-domain items
must remain available.

Every quality PASS/flag names a reachable detector and scope. Required detector
families cover orphan roots, context/generation mismatch, incomplete domains or
ledgers, sign/polarity loss, duplicate shared roots and producer integrity. A
constant-green flag is invalid.

## Practice eligibility

`bo_upaya` is attributed practice eligibility only: source, target, constraints,
contraindications and unknowns. It supplies no prescription, efficacy, clinical,
timing or guaranteed-relief claim. DP-SD-015 removes new resolved daśā-window
emission and preserves existing legacy rows without rewriting them.
