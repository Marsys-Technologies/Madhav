# L1 condition, relation, varga, clock and sensitivity contract

## Conditions

Dignity, avasthā, motion, combustion, friendship, ṣaḍbala, bhāvabala and AV remain
separate components with their own units, source roots and reasons. Raw constituents,
totals, normalizations and judged classifications are never interchangeable. The current
`ga_condition` atomic rows now receive semantic IDs; the existing composite table remains
a derived judgment, not a replacement for constituents.

## Relations and shared roots

`TypedRelation` requires relation type, actor fact, target fact, method, non-negative orb
when relevant and identity-deduplicated constituent facts. Aspect, conjunction, exchange,
dispositor and argalā primitives are geometry/rule outputs only. L1 emits no structural
meaning, causal claim, independent-support count or manifestation claim. Repeated aliases
to one constituent root cannot inflate support.

## Vargas and sensitivity

Varga output retains formula, domain, own-lagna house, degree/dignity and D1 ancestry.
Different vargas are method-specific views, not votes. `SensitivityResult` records the
fact/context, target varga/formula/domain, D1 comparison, perturbation, baseline and
perturbed inputs, computed boundary distance, changed/unchanged/unsupported classification
and reason. The first slice invokes the admitted production D9 Navāṃśa formula; replacing
its formula identity with an unknown value fails. Relevant boundary crossing must change
the declared result; irrelevant in-segment perturbation must not.

Persisted sign-keyed AV is retained. Contributor-level prastāra remains
`unavailable/computed-discarded`; no new payload is added without a named consumer and
cost/compatibility proof.

## Exact clocks

`ClockInterval` uses aware ISO half-open intervals, hierarchy level, stable interval and
parent identities, applicability, coverage and uncertainty seconds. Non-root intervals
require a parent; the supplied stable interval ID must match exact context/system/parent/
level/start/end identity, and end must be after start. `ga_dashas` now normalizes all transient tree
UUIDs to deterministic UUID5s using parent identity plus chart, ayanāṃśa, system, level,
lord, exact start/end and KP discriminators before persistence. Tājaka annual rows also
receive stable identities. The KP beyond-sub-sub cap remains a legal level-4 interval;
Prāṇa is stored as `dasha_scope_cap.level_5_not_computed` with
`method_inapplicable`, not as an impossible level-5 interval.

Daśā, Tājaka, Sāḍe-sātī and transit-anchor clocks retain their own method/frame/horizon.
Condition peak/weak-period ancestry is constrained to the row's exact ayanāṃśa and build.
Daśā concurrency is evaluated only between systems in the same ayanāṃśa and with
`start_iso`/`end_iso` half-open intervals, never date-truncated bounds. L1 does not truncate
to dates for consumers and never infers activation or outcome.

Sensitive and structural boundaries require complete finite geometry. Missing Lagna,
graha longitude, vara, exact birth instant/coordinates/timezone, sign or house aborts the partition; transit anchors
require all nine grahas. These gates prevent the historical Aries/house-1/longitude-0/
Sunday/fixed-hour-day and partial-anchor fallbacks from becoming ordinary facts. Sensitive
day/night state is recomputed against astronomical sunrise/sunset for the local birth date.

## Restricted capital

Birth Pañcāṅga is not an arbitrary-day service; Praśna remains dormant; medical output is
traditional and non-diagnostic; Āyurdāya remains restricted scholarly capital with no
individual mortality/lifespan delivery; Vāstu is not a complete spatial assessment.
