## Tension 2 — "eager century-wide work is not required user value" vs "deferral alone cannot earn elevation"

**VERIFIED.** Both quotes exist, and they bear on the *same* asset.

- **REG:140** (`ka_gochara_v3_century_materialize` row): "P/Q/H: existing hold remains; **eager
  century-wide work is not itself required user value.** No rematerialization here. DP07/16."
- **STRAT:230-234** (immediately after the P6 benchmark table): "The century materializer remains
  protected by its existing build/authority hold while these alternatives are studied. Its final
  asset disposition must choose between an elevated complete materialization and a qualified compact
  substrate with explicit refinement semantics. **Deferral alone cannot earn full asset elevation.**
  Existing retained data is preserved through the decision."

**How they conflict:** REG:140 gives the asset a *substantive* reason its eager century-wide
materialization should not be built — the work is not required user value — and pairs it with an
operational instruction ("No rematerialization here"). STRAT:233 then rules that not doing the work
cannot yield elevation. Together they leave the asset in a closed loop: the register forbids the
only action (eager century materialization) that STRAT's first disposition branch ("an elevated
complete materialization") would consist of, while STRAT forbids the state the register's
instruction produces (hold/defer) from counting as elevation. The escape STRAT names — branch two,
"a qualified compact substrate with explicit refinement semantics" — is *not* mentioned in REG:140
at all, so the register's guidance, read alone, points only at the closed branch.

Note a second-order effect on the campaign's headline metric: `CLAUDE.md §E` sets the metric at
`Accepted N/22` over 22 active identities, and `ka_gochara_v3_century_materialize` is one of the 22
(it is `is_active: true`, `catalog_status: 'CURRENT'` at `asset_registry_seed.ts:2198-2199`), unlike
`ka_gochara_sweep` which is explicitly excluded. So an asset the register tells not to build and the
strategy tells cannot be accepted by not building it still sits in the denominator.

**Bears on:** whether the denominator is 22 or 21; whether STRAT branch two ("qualified compact
substrate") is the native's intended disposition; whether REG:140 needs the branch-two option added.
