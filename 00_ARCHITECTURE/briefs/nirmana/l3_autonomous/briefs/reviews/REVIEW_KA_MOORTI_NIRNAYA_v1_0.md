## 1. Verdict

**REWORK.** The brief's three diagnoses point at real things, but the recommended remedy for the first cannot be executed under the invariants the brief itself preserves (option §10.1(a) fails migration 670's conjunct (f) at the orchestrator's post-write gate, so the build still fails — one step later), the mechanism of the second is stated with the wrong sign and a badly understated magnitude (the noon-UT knot lags the ingress instant by 0–24 h, never leads it; roughly half of all graded rows are expected to carry a nakṣatra index one ahead of the instant value, not "a fraction … far from noon"), which inverts two proof rows and the §3 example; one proof row targets a branch the writer's own completeness gate makes unreachable; and both integrator consumers the brief claims are not live on this base (Gochara's engine never reads the prefetched `moorti_rows`; Kṣetra's `build_moorti_primitive` has no caller).
