# Madhav data plane — L2 Bodha strategy

## 1. Decision and authority

`DP-SD-015` approves this L2 strategy and its paired execution brief under Product
Definition v3.0, DP-SD-009, accepted L0 revision `f6fed12c7`, and accepted L1
terminal revision `18503e9c2` with reviewed implementation tip `a9c44c298`.

This is a new data-plane semantic elevation of Bodha. It does not reopen,
overwrite or recertify the historical L2 Nirmāṇa campaign, its 22/22 formal
receipt, or its production rebuild. Those remain evidence for their own
definition, revision and claims. They do not establish that the current Bodha
portfolio consumes the new L1 generation/context contract, preserves every
material signed relationship through an investigator, or satisfies Product

The following controls are binding:

1. Execution starts from exact accepted L1 terminal revision `18503e9c2` on the
   permanent branch `codex/madhav-data-plane-execution`.
2. The current data-plane producer census is exactly 23 `bo_*` writer identities.
   `bo_grounding` is operational support outside the historical frozen formal
   campaign denominator of 22. Examining and qualifying it here does not rewrite
   that historical denominator or its receipts.
3. L2 owns personalized structural meaning: propositions, configurations,
   typed signed relationships, full domains, tensions, alternatives, discovery
   candidates and inspectable whole-chart organization. It does not own L1
   computation, L3 activation, L4 manifestation, L5 evaluation or final prose.
4. L1 facts remain authoritative. Bodha references their exact stable identities,
   contexts, generations, units, missingness and verification; it never silently
   re-derives or contradicts them.
5. Occurrence support and delivery condition are separate. Cancellation preserves
   the polarity and target of what it cancels. Graph connectivity, centrality,
   salience, embeddings and co-occurrence are navigation or candidate evidence,
   not causation, temporal necessity, manifestation or independent testimony.
6. The accepted Bhāvat Bhāvam source package remains `UNQUALIFIED_SOURCE`.
   Existing code or maps cannot turn it into a qualified positive proposition.
7. The first L2 execution ends at `PRODUCER_READY`. No L3 edit, retrieval cutover,
   managed-channel integration, push, PR, merge, deployment, live database
   rebuild, consumer-value claim or empirical claim is authorized.

## 2. L2's distinctive contribution

Bodha converts qualified chart facts into a connected and challengeable account
of what the chart structurally supports, opposes, leaves uncertain or permits as
an alternative. A material Bodha proposition must answer:

- what the proposition or mechanism is, under which admitted rule and method;
- which actors, relations, targets, configurations and domains participate;
- which exact L1 facts support, oppose or condition it, without double counting
  aliases, totals, components or repeated wrappers;
- whether the underlying configuration occurs, partially occurs, fails, is
  cancelled, is inapplicable or is blocked by an unqualified source;
- whether its delivery condition is strong, impaired, contradicted or unknown,
  without reversing the meaning of an affliction or cancellation scale;
- which rival interpretations and alternate routes remain live;
- which relationships are direct, transitive, contextual, merely co-occurring
  or only discovery candidates; and
- what a downstream investigator must hydrate before making a temporal or
  manifestation claim.

The earned contribution is not a larger signal table or a stronger score. It is
the prevention of isolated-placement readings, first-domain loss, false causal
direction, cancellation-polarity reversal, correlated-evidence inflation,
summary-only synthesis and attractive but unsupported structural claims.

## 6. Internal interplay and adjacent boundaries

The qualified producer path is:

`accepted L0 rule/meaning + accepted L1 fact/configuration generation -> Bodha proposition -> signed relationship graph -> contradiction/rival/mechanism package -> discovery and whole-chart navigation -> later investigator hydration`

Mandatory separations:

- `bo_laksana` is the proposition root; satellite emitters and reranking extend or
  project it but do not create independent support merely by restating it.
- `bo_bimba`/`bo_karanajala` own graph identity; paths, motifs and mechanisms
  preserve their exact edge roots and do not infer causal or temporal necessity.
- `bo_sangati` keeps both members of every domain pair and their signed drivers;
  `bo_cdlm_summary` is a derived navigation projection.
- `bo_pratijna` keeps occurrence and condition axes plus ledgers. Any legacy
  grade is compatibility data, not the sole semantic authority.
- frame-specific writers contribute legitimate perspectives but are correlated
  projections of shared chart roots, not additional witnesses.
- `bo_samskara`, `bo_drishti`, `bo_anveshana`, `bo_chart_gestalt` and
  `bo_samvada` discover or organize evidence. They do not replace hydration.
- `bo_grounding` qualifies source linkage; `bo_pramana_mapa` qualifies only the
  exact invariants its executable detectors measure.
- `bo_upaya` remains an attributed practice-eligibility surface. Structural
  relevance, priority, efficacy and outcome are four different claims.
- No Bodha producer may import an L3/Kāla engine or persist resolved activation
  windows as L2 truth. Existing `bo_karanajala` temporal output is removed from
  new generation semantics; legacy schema/history remains readable. Exact L1
  clock references may survive only as non-interpreted upstream evidence.
- L3 may later consume stable structural IDs and signed ledgers, but L2 emits no
  activation gate, window, event probability or temporal verdict.

## 7. First producer slice

`L2-SLICE-RESOURCE-MECHANISM-01` is the first accepted producer slice. It
consumes the exact synthetic non-person L1 bundle
`L1-SLICE-RESOURCE-CONFIG-01` at digest
`25c46b559def7e1a9f8e1a05114be5a6c306a846a23b2665b1b5c917128d3279`.

The slice must emit a deterministic structural bundle containing:

1. exact accepted L0/L1 release, context, generation and content pins;
2. stable proposition, configuration, relationship, contradiction and mechanism IDs;
3. all participants with actor/relation/target and configuration roles;
4. a complete domain-role map distinguishing resource creation, receipts,
   retention and relief where the admitted evidence supports those distinctions;
5. separate occurrence and condition ledgers with original units/polarity;
6. signed supporting and opposing paths, shared-root/dependence groups and
   independent-support count only where earned;
7. cancellation target, original polarity and resulting structural role;
8. rival/alternate propositions, unresolved discriminators and explicit
   `unqualified|inapplicable|unavailable|failed|unexplored` states;
9. grounding and epistemic lineage back to exact L0 rules and L1 facts; and
10. discovery/lens/gestalt pointers that require hydration and cannot substitute
    rank, centrality or embedding similarity for evidence.

The Bhāvat Bhāvam arm must remain `UNQUALIFIED_SOURCE` and its positive doctrinal
path `NOT_REACHABLE` unless an already-admitted L0 dossier at the accepted release
satisfies the qualification contract. Execution cannot create or admit such a
dossier. Producer readiness may pass with **zero qualified positive doctrinal
propositions** when the accepted L0 release supplies none. An explicitly
engineering-only fixture must exercise positive, negative, partial and
cancellation state machinery while remaining `ENGINEERING_ONLY` and incapable of
promotion. An already-admitted qualified non-Bhāvat rule may be added as an
additional control only when WP0 pins its exact accepted L0 authority; it is not
a completion prerequisite.

Required perturbations:

- change one qualifying condition or cancellation and observe only the declared
  condition/route change while geometry and unrelated propositions remain fixed;
- reverse cancellation target polarity and prove that cancelling opposition is
  not treated like cancelling support;
- reorder domains and aliases without semantic change;
- duplicate one L1 root through two signals/paths without increasing independent
  support;
- add an unrelated configuration without changing the selected mechanism;
- place a decisive distinction only in a second domain and below default rank,
  then prove it remains in the producer package; and
- attempt wrong chart/context/generation and unqualified-rule joins and prove
  they fail closed.

This is producer evidence only. It does not prove retrieval, managed delivery,
consumer comprehension, timing, manifestation, empirical value or causation.

1. Freeze the 23-identity data-plane census and reconcile the historical 22
   denominator without mutating campaign state.

   consumers, projections, historical findings and current source at the exact

   cancellation, evidence-dependence, epistemic/missingness and generation
   contracts.
5. Elevate in dependency order: root signal/proposition; graph and domain
   fan-out; paths/mechanisms/contradictions; frame-specific and practice
   projections; discovery/grounding/investigator/quality projections.
6. Implement and freeze the deterministic non-person first slice.
7. Prove compatibility, idempotency, correction, invalidation, replay and
   rollback locally; do not mutate live data.
8. Run focused and proportionate broad tests, then obtain independent read-only
   challenge of all 23 dispositions and every terminal claim.
9. Return one terminal producer-ready packet to Strategy and stop with L3
   waiting.

`PRODUCER_READY` requires 23/23 current writer boundaries to adopt or be proven
compatible with the L2 contract; exact L0/L1 pins; no unresolved hidden hard DAG
edge; stable structural identities; complete signed/multidomain ledgers; honest
qualification, dependence and missingness; deterministic replay; first-slice
negative controls; independent PASS with no owned material finding; and zero
prohibited-surface change.

It does not mean `INTEGRATED`, `DEPLOYED_OPERATIONALLY_ACCEPTED`,
`CONSUMER_VALUE_DEMONSTRATED` or `EMPIRICALLY_EVALUATED`.
