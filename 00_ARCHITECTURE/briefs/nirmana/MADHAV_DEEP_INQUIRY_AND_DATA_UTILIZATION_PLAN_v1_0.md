# Deep inquiry and full-value data utilization

> Historical proposed companion. Reconcile against the [adopted v3.0 master](../../MADHAV_PRODUCT_DEFINITION_v3_0.md) before deriving execution briefs. The original parent, requirements and source examples remain below for preservation; they are not a completed v3.0 data-plane plan.

## 1. The decision this plan makes

Madhav must behave as an **investigator over a rich, connected knowledge system**, not as a prompt followed by a single retrieval batch and a fluent answer. Quality of understanding, breadth, relevant completeness and predictive usefulness outrank quick response time. Each identified relevant, authorized fact must reach the response with its individual or conjoint significance.

Keep the six-layer ownership model. Strengthen the connections among those layers and the inquiry process rather than flattening them into one undifferentiated store. Make qualified user evidence a first-class resource with explicit consumers and purposes. Execute the elevation through short, high-value dependency slices, with minimum necessary process and strong automated tests.

These requirements revise the actual product definition in v2.2. They are not only an optional annex to unchanged v2.1 language. The source code, data, flags, frozen build contracts and active campaigns remain unchanged.

### Decision delta from v2.1

| Earlier emphasis | Revised requirement |
|---|---|
| Deep reasoning, proportionate response, evidence beneath it | Iterative investigation with explicit concept/relationship closure; the complete relevant findings belong in the delivered response. |
| Quality alongside latency/cost | Interpretive quality comes first; progress/resumption handle latency. Resource limits cannot masquerade as complete investigation. |
| Relevant evidence considered by the planner | Independently challenge what the planner never selected; discover adjacent evidence and unexamined concepts. |
| Assets must contribute | Both builders and query planners must discover field-level capabilities and consume canonical shared definitions. |
| Life-history inquiry and protected evaluation | A full subject-observation lifecycle and layer-by-layer use plan; a separately proposed context-conditioned future mode, without weakening the present firewall. |
| Governed execution | Reuse existing controls; automate proof; avoid duplicated documents, repeated unchanged audits and artificial waiting. |

## 3. Keep ownership layers; add reliable connections

Keep L0 global/reference, L1 computational, L2 structural, L3 temporal, L4 manifestation/claim, and L5 observation/evaluation responsibilities. Most L1–L5 materializations remain chart/subject-scoped. Qualified shared model artifacts or services retain their explicit scopes; do not force them into a misleading “every row is chart-specific” rule.

Separate three kinds of movement:

- **Build dependencies:** reproducible forward computation from pinned upstream inputs; existing writer and orchestrator ownership remains intact.
- **Inquiry access:** authorized navigation across all relevant layers, including revisits, adjacency and source clarification. It is not restricted to one linear L0→L5 pass.
- **Observation/evaluation/improvement:** purpose-specific subject evidence and separately admitted future model changes. These must not become hidden feedback into the original claim.

A relationship index or query workspace may span layers, but its entries reference canonical facts and interpretations. It does not become a new truth store. This gives connected reasoning without abandoning separation of facts, interpretations and evidence of outcomes.

## 4. The investigation loop

### 4.1 Understand the question before solving it

Extract the subject and authority, exact question, subquestions, domain, event definitions, horizon, method scope, input uncertainty and requested comparison. Distinguish “money received” from retained wealth, or promotion from greater responsibility without compensation. Ask the person only about ambiguities that materially affect the investigation. Do not repeatedly ask for information already supplied and usable for the purpose.

Construct a revisable obligation set: facts needed, concepts whose applicability must be checked, structural relationships, temporal hypotheses, alternatives, uncertainties and safety restrictions. Fixed reading floors establish a minimum, not a ceiling. New evidence can add obligations; the initial plan cannot certify its own completeness.

### 4.2 Investigate, connect, challenge, repeat

1. Load the appropriate whole-chart foundation and qualified domain evidence.
2. Hydrate complete relevant records—not only their ranks or summaries—and preserve original evidence identities.
3. Build supported relationship paths: actors, roles, signs/houses, configurations, conditions, domains and applicable temporal links. Every edge has a semantic basis; shared topology alone is not a causal rule.
4. Apply qualified concepts and enumerate material alternatives, exceptions, cancellation and contrary evidence.
5. Identify the next frontier: an unresolved prerequisite, missing part of a chain, unexamined concept, conflicting evidence, sensitive input or a neighboring domain that could change the answer.
6. Retrieve or compute that evidence through its owner. Update the finding set and continue while material obligations remain tractable.
7. Run an omission challenge against the question, capability map and evidence—not just a second LLM rereading the same short summary.
8. Assemble the complete findings; check that each supported relevant fact and conjoint interpretation is delivered. Only then settle the reading and any forecast.

Independent candidate evaluation can use parallel computation where appropriate; consistency, context and evidence qualification must still be preserved. More model calls or more tokens are not themselves proof of better reasoning.

### 4.3 What is retained between passes

Use an explicit workspace of question obligations, immutable evidence references, relationship paths, candidate findings, unresolved questions and completion state. Preserve the source versions and cumulative findings when a context window or transport needs continuation. A compact navigation summary may point into this workspace but cannot replace the underlying evidence.

This workspace records inputs, results, rationale and open questions; it does not expose private internal deliberation. The consumer receives an evidence-based explanation of the conclusions, not a stream of hidden reasoning.

Avoid fetching the same unchanged page repeatedly. Cache by evidence identity, subject, generation, purpose, query scope and policy version; invalidate when any relevant dependency changes. Cache hits must not turn a prior conclusion into new independent support.

### 4.4 When may the system finish?

Three completion checks are required:

- **Concept coverage:** relevant applicable concepts are applied, explicitly inapplicable, unsupported by available method, blocked by missing inputs, or unresolved. A tool call is not an “applied” state.
- **Relationship coverage:** material discovered paths have been investigated to their meaningful boundary, or their unresolved frontier is reported. No arbitrary hop limit silently defines astrological relevance.
- **Response coverage:** every identified relevant authorized fact, contrary fact and conjoint finding maps into the delivered response or an explicit permitted exclusion. Duplicates may map to the same passage.

Closure can include an honest unresolved interpretation. It cannot silently include unperformed checks. The engine should return one of complete-for-declared-scope, needs-clarification, incomplete-resumable, or blocked-with-specific-gap. “Complete for scope” is bounded by the declared method/evidence universe, not a claim that no unknown principle could exist.

Time alone is not an interpretive stopping criterion. Detect cycles and diminishing information gain, but do not use them to waive known material omissions. Checkpoint and resume when infrastructure limits are reached. Existing caps must be addressed through a separate scoped capacity decision; waiting longer is not authorization for unlimited spending.

## 5. Completeness of the delivered answer

The standard response contains a synthesis followed by a complete, organized findings section. A finding includes:

- The relevant facts and their source/computation standing.
- Their individual significance where useful, and what they mean in combination.
- The domain and temporal consequence being considered.
- Important supporting and opposing conditions, alternatives and uncertainty.
- The conclusion actually supported, with a forecast contract if one is made.

Group repeated facts without erasing their role in different chains. Use concise language, not forced brevity. Do not reduce connected evidence to a list of disconnected planet statements, an aggregate score or citation chips that require the person to reconstruct the analysis.

The delivered artifact must already contain the complete findings. Expandable sections may organize that artifact, but must not conceal an omitted payload behind a future retrieval. Large managed-MCP answers may use durable numbered parts or a referenced complete result package fetched by the managed client before it claims completion. The first summary must identify any unfinished delivery. Downloads and exports retain the same facts, limitations and corrections.

Both managed doors owe the same substantive investigation and findings. Raw tool access must expose complete authorized evidence, capability limits and pagination; Madhav cannot guarantee what an uncontrolled external model will choose to say. A client requiring the product reading guarantee should use the managed investigation endpoint/result contract, not raw snippets alone.

## 6. Foundational semantics and discoverability

### 6.1 Canonical identity contract

The likely asset remembered by the native is **bg_ontology**. In inspected source, its Sun entity has aliases including Surya, Ravi, Aditya, Sūrya, Arka and Bhaskar. The task is broader than adding missing strings to two lists.

Every shared entity/concept needs a stable semantic identity, canonical owner, qualified aliases/transliterations, ambiguity rules, scope, applicable method variants and version. Downstream computation, ingestion, retrieval and rendering must agree on identity while retaining storage conventions through explicit adapters.

Generate or validate Python/TypeScript vocabularies and other offline snapshots against the same released definition. Never require a database call per token merely to avoid a local constant. Generated constants are efficient and legitimate; independently invented meanings are not. An unknown or ambiguous alias must produce explicit resolution behavior, not a plausible invented identity. Do not merge method distinctions such as node conventions or different rule variants through loose normalization.

### 6.2 A shared capability and meaning map

Extend the existing registry/contracts rather than defaulting to a new registry. For each significant output, describe: producer; exact field/relationship; semantic grain and units; source/method scope; aliases and concepts; prerequisites; query conditions that make it relevant; access path and permissions; completeness/pagination; computation/qualification status; intended consumers; version/invalidation; and known limits.

Two routes must work:

1. **Question-to-data:** a domain, concept, entity or newly discovered relation identifies every potentially material qualified capability, including adjacent domains and low-ranked witnesses.
2. **Data-to-consumer:** each enriched field declares the downstream computation and answer obligation it is meant to improve. It must not depend on the next engineer remembering a document or discovering it by chance.

Material build contracts must declare their selected upstream inputs and account for required-but-unused fields. This does not mean every consumer reads every field. The point is a deliberate, tested choice, not accidental ignorance.

Maintain distinct inventories of missing source/computation; computed-but-discarded information; persisted-but-unserved information; and available-but-unqualified information. Unknown consumption remains unknown until verified; it is not proof of redundancy.

### 6.3 The Bhāvat Bhāvam proving case

First qualify the concept and operator scopes from their proper sources. Then trace the relevant question through applicability, prerequisite facts, computation status, all required relationship fields, retrieval, iterative expansion, synthesis and response.

Do not confuse the producer's prohibition on recursively amplifying an amplifier with a prohibition on investigating a connected chain. Do not silently replace the current narrow doctrine with unrestricted derived-house arithmetic. The target requires coverage of the appropriate concept; whether to enrich an existing operator, add a qualified variant or revise a projection follows the scoped doctrinal and consumer analysis.

Recover the actual missed example in a later authorized replay, if available. Its absence does not block designing tests, but a synthetic reproduction cannot be reported as the original incident's root cause.

## 7. Life-event utilization across the data plane

### 7.1 One subject resource, multiple uses

Treat life events as first-class subject observations, not global knowledge and not merely chat memory. Chart creation, bulk import, direct event entry and a Paripraśna disclosure should converge on the same canonical intake contract. Inventory and reconcile existing ledgers; do not create a competing diary because one legacy route is insufficient.

The intake flow is: receive the account → preserve provenance → propose a structured observation → clarify consequential ambiguity → obtain appropriate confirmation → publish a versioned subject observation → derive only purpose-admitted features → notify affected consumers.

Do not require a click to confirm each harmless sentence, but never turn ambiguous conversation, speculation, quoted third-party stories or the assistant's own suggestion into confirmed autobiography. Bulk confirmation may be appropriate when provenance and review are clear. Evidence reliability and the significance assigned by the person are separate properties.

Minimum observation contract:

| Group | Required distinctions |
|---|---|
| Identity/authority | Stable observation and subject IDs, source channel/import ID, actor, consent/purpose and any related-subject permission. |
| What happened | Event versus ongoing state versus intention, outcome category and subtype, optional magnitude/valence, original description and normalized representation. |
| Time | Event start/end or interval, precision and uncertainty, location/time zone where material, first reported/ingested time, confirmation time and correction time. |
| Evidence standing | User-reported, corroborated under a defined procedure, disputed or uncertain; source attachments/references when supplied. |
| Exposure | Whether a prior reading was shown before reporting, what was already known at forecast emission, and study/holdout restrictions. |
| History | Append-only revisions, supersession/deduplication links, withdrawal, provenance and conflict resolution. |
| Derived use | Feature/calculation versions, source-event lineage, purpose, availability cutoff, dependent products and invalidation status. |

Do not infer precise dates from approximate memories. Silence is not a non-event. Duplicates across chart creation and chat must reconcile without doubling evidential weight. A correction changes the current observation version while preserving what earlier analysis actually knew.

### 7.2 Layer-by-layer use matrix

| Owner | Valuable use of observations or derived features | What must remain separate |
|---|---|---|
| L0 | Defines common event/state/activity vocabulary and mappings; separately approved research can identify vocabulary gaps or produce qualified shared artifacts. | No personal diary or one person's outcomes become global source truth, automatic rules or population priors. |
| L1 | Computes authorized event-time astronomical context from time/location inputs; represents interval uncertainty and alternate admissible inputs. | Event occurrence is not an astronomical calculation. Biography does not alter natal facts. Rectified birth hypotheses remain separately identified and tested. |
| L2 | In a historical/contextual projection, compares observed patterns with structural mechanisms; identifies supported, contradictory and unexplained links. | Keep the event-free structural build and its evidence distinct from event-conditioned findings. Do not upgrade chart-derived strength because an event seems to fit. |
| L3 | Reconstructs event-time clocks/contacts; compares recurrences, unmatched activations and non-event periods; studies timing discrimination. | Historical overlays are not prospective predictions. Freeze event-free searches before outcome comparison when evaluating blind timing. |
| L4 | Distinguishes actual manifestation from alternative expressions; clarifies real objectives and admissible practical constraints. | User-reported outcome versus predicted manifestation versus causal inference. Proposed forecast conditioning cannot bypass current policy. |
| L5 | Matches candidate outcomes, captures disagreement/unknowns, evaluates frozen forecasts, tracks coverage and supports separately admitted future learning. | A suggested match is not confirmed success; test outcomes and C3 data stay outside serving prompts; parked learning remains parked. |
| Inquiry/serving | Remembers authorized context for the permitted purpose, avoids repeated questions, keeps chronology and exposes corrections. | Purpose-filtered context, not unrestricted history spliced into every model call or cache. |

“Across the layers” means every justified consumer has an explicit use, not every table stores the diary. Some consumers need only a date interval, an event class, a practical constraint or a derived comparison; give them the minimum complete input for that role.

### 7.3 Four analytical modes—do not mix their evidence

**A. Event-free chart forecast.** Uses the allowed chart/reference evidence and permitted query specification without life-event/outcome conditioning. This retains the current prospective firewall and provides a distinguishable baseline. Its frozen output is never retroactively edited after observation.

**B. Historical or contextual inquiry.** Uses authorized observations to investigate fit, misfit and meaning. It can be deeply personalized and useful, but explicitly says the event was known during interpretation. C1 permission remains subject to privacy/provider controls; it does not admit C3 outcome-ledger material into synthesis.

**C. Protected evaluation.** Compares already-frozen claims with admissible, confirmed observations using isolated handling, original knowledge cutoffs and complete outcome taxonomy. Scoring/reporting does not route prohibited C3 material through an LLM provider. Original probabilities, versions and emission times remain unchanged.

**D. Context-conditioned future forecast—PROPOSED, not currently authorized.** Potentially valuable for questions whose future meaning depends on known circumstances. It would use explicitly admitted, pre-cutoff context features, not future outcomes or arbitrary access to the event store. The target claim, prior exposure, input set and conditional nature are frozen; it is evaluated against an appropriate context-aware baseline and separately from A.

D requires a narrow explicit amendment to PPR-31 and any affected purpose/classification contracts. A derived summary is not automatically safe: it can encode the protected target. Keep C3 serving/provider prohibitions intact. Before admission, test indirect leakage through rectification, model selection, summaries, priors, caches and background rebuilds. No user-history material is admitted to prospective generation merely because its event date precedes the forecast; the full derivation and knowledge-time lineage matter.

For now, implementable designs must obey A/B/C boundaries under their actual authority. D can be specified and challenged as part of the target, not quietly activated. This preserves maximal legitimate use without making the product's predictive record circular.

### 7.4 Revisions and propagation

A new or corrected event publishes a subject-scoped revision. Dependency mapping identifies which historical projections, comparisons, inquiry views and evaluation candidates need recomputation; unrelated natal calculations do not rebuild automatically. Mark affected outputs stale before reuse, then atomically publish a coherent new generation. Retried ingestion and recomputation are idempotent.

An event correction may change an evaluation result under a versioned adjudication record. It cannot change the original forecast or erase an inconvenient miss. A birth-input correction has a different propagation path: rebuild the affected chart-derived products while preserving earlier reading packages and any explicit hypotheses.

Withdrawn consent invalidates downstream use and triggers the applicable verified deletion/retention process, including derived projections, caches and any affected research/model-artifact review. Hashes do not by themselves anonymize sensitive data. Failed consumers retain pending status; success is not inferred from the event being stored.

## 9. Acceptance: test the omissions, not only the happy path

| Test | Required result |
|---|---|
| Known-miss replay | When the actual query/trace is available, account for the missing concept and chain from input to delivered finding; distinguish replay from synthetic fixture. |
| Concept-state matrix | Positive, computed-zero, inapplicable, outside operator scope, missing input, failed and skipped all remain distinguishable. |
| Adjacent-chain discovery | A decisive second-domain or later-hop relationship outside the initial top results is found, qualified and explained. No unsupported edge is invented. |
| Projection sentinel | A decisive field available only outside the default projection reaches synthesis and the response. Citation existence alone fails this test. |
| Alias parity | Qualified L0 aliases give identical semantic selection across Python, TypeScript, imports and both managed channels; unknown/ambiguous cases are explicit. |
| Evidence dependence | Repeated aliases, duplicate events, totals/components and recycled summaries do not multiply independent support. |
| Relevant/irrelevant perturbation | A relevant changed input alters its declared consequence; an unrelated field or array reordering does not. |
| Response completeness | Compare expected finding identities/meaning against all delivered parts; a correct summary with a missing relevant finding fails. |
| Long inquiry | Required evidence beyond the normal iteration/context boundary is retained and investigated through continuation; an exhausted limit yields incomplete, not fabricated completion. |
| Intake parity | Equivalent events from creation/import/chat have the same subject and meaning, with provenance differences preserved and no duplicate weighting. |
| Temporal admissibility | Late-entered, corrected, approximate and already-exposed events cannot silently enter a blind historical or prospective evaluation. |
| Indirect leakage | Known outcomes encoded in summaries, rectification or model choice are rejected from prohibited purposes; a coarse feature is not an automatic exception. |
| Revision isolation | Correcting one subject/event invalidates affected derivatives without altering unrelated charts or original predictions. |
| Value test | Richer investigation yields correct additional distinctions, better-supported uncertainty or safer abstention against a simpler baseline; extra length is not counted as improvement. |

Evaluate concept coverage on declared known cases and novel combinations; a denominator chosen by the same planner that omitted a concept is insufficient. Expert review of domain applicability is distinct from a software test and from empirical validation of astrology. All tests here are proposed, not reported executed.

Use one concise brief per coherent slice, one owner, a reused source of state and automated evidence. A successful producer contract need not wait for a fully rebuilt downstream system: label it producer-ready, then complete the planned integration. Reuse historical accepted evidence for unchanged behavior; reopen only changed or unproven claims.
