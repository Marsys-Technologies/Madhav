## §2 · D-32 — the critical path merges first

1. Merge order is a priority, not first-come: (a) Wave 4 spine items and packages (P01–P09, J-*), (b) items that unblock a `ready` or `review` item, (c) everything else.
2. While a priority-(a) PR is open and mergeable within one main-merge, no priority-(c) PR is queued ahead of it. Small PRs rebase behind the critical path, not the other way round.
3. P06 (#3516) and P07 (#3512) are brought up to date and queued before any further child PR.

## §3 · D-33 — no per-change edits to shared files

1. **`ci.yml` is not edited per change.** Test jobs discover new test files by pattern (per directory or marker), so a packet adds tests, not workflow jobs. One reviewed change introduces the discovery; after it, a PR that edits `ci.yml` needs a reason in its description.
2. **Generated files are not committed per branch.** Writer digests, the capability census and `kala_protected_migrations.txt` are regenerated by the merge-queue run (or a post-merge job on `main`) and committed by it, so two branches never conflict on them. Until that lands, the launcher's main-merge (D-34) absorbs most of the conflicts.
3. Plan-model edits stay in their own small PRs (A3 D-25), batched per hour, never inside a code PR.

## §4 · D-34 — the launcher brings a branch up to date before pushing

Applied 2026-10-16 ~05:40Z in `cx_post.sh` (backup `cx_post.sh.bak-20261016-a5`): after the agent's commit, the launcher fetches `main` and merges it into the branch; on a conflict it aborts the merge, leaves the copy clean at the agent's commit, pushes, and records `main merged: conflict:<files>` in `post.json` for the cockpit to route a rework. Tested on a scratch repository: behind-main branch gained main's commits and pushed; conflicting branch aborted cleanly and still pushed.

## §5 · D-35 — a verdict survives a main-merge; completion applies inheritance itself

1. When the only change since an ACCEPTED verdict is a merge from `main` with no conflict in the item's own paths, the verdict is inherited at the new head without a new review job or a hand-recorded entry. This is the plan's own inheritance predicate (v2.7 KS-08); the completion step applies it automatically.
2. The eight merged items waiting only on a re-recorded verdict are completed under this rule as soon as it is in force.
3. A merge that does conflict in the item's own paths, or any non-merge commit, still needs the normal review under A2 D-17.

## §6 · D-36 — launch-on-ready launches every ready build item

When a `ready` build item has no packet, `autolaunch.sh` builds the task from the plan model's own entry (title, brief, contract, tests, owns, depends_on) and the shared header, and launches it; "ready but no packet" is no longer a reason to wait. Verdict, decision and join items stay excluded. The cockpit keeps review, acceptance and merge.

## §7 · D-37 — non-blocking children are batched per parent

Amends A2 D-17.3. Every non-severe finding is still its own tracked child item with its own detector. But the children of one parent found in one review round are built and merged as **one branch, one PR, one review**, not one PR each. The PR closes all the children it carries. A child that needs a different owner or a migration of its own may still be split out, with the reason recorded.

## §8 · D-38 — reporting cadence

One owner report at 06:00Z with the measured numbers, and one on any stop, breach or hard-stop event. No hourly or per-tick narration. The cockpit's own journal remains as detailed as it likes.

## §9 · D-39 — the dashboard shows "built, waiting on gate"

Merged items whose only open dependency is a decision gate or another item are shown as their own class, separate from "in review", so pre-built work reads as progress and the gate it waits on is named.
