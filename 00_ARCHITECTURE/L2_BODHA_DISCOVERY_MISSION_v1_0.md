# L2 Bodha — The Discovery Mission (the absolute goal) v1.0

## §1 — The problem statement (native, 2026-06-19) — the real north star
An acharya / human brain holds ~7±2 chart factors in working memory. A chart has thousands of deterministic facts
+ millions of multi-entity relationships across 30 vargas × 5 ayanamshas. The acharya, BY COGNITIVE NECESSITY,
PRUNES — looks at the 10th lord, a couple of yogas, the dasha — and reasons from those. The consequential insight
that lives in the interaction of the 40th-salient factor with the 200th, three hops down a chain, in D24, they
NEVER SEE — not from lack of skill, but because no human brain can hold it. **THIS is why we do research-level data
engineering of the astrological profile: to unearth the insights that exceed human working memory.** The absolute
test of L2 Bodha is therefore: *does it surface the consequential pattern below the waterline of human attention?*

## §2 — Honest assessment: substrate built, ENGINE missing
What the 8 assets ACHIEVED toward discovery (real, credited): refused to prune (no-threshold-drop, all 14 L1
assets, full enumeration — the buried factor EXISTS); the deep multi-entity graph + full-length chains (the
buried relationship is computable + stored); the anti-tunnel-vision lens; the pivot/common-cause analysis. The
RAW MATERIAL for discovery is present.

**The gap:** everything is QUERY-DRIVEN — it ANSWERS when asked. The discovery goal is the OPPOSITE causality —
the insight must ANNOUNCE ITSELF even when no one asked the right question (the acharya didn't know to ask). We
built a substrate from which discovery is POSSIBLE; we did NOT build the engine that DISCOVERS. Three specific holes:
- **No non-obviousness surface** — we score salience (strong) + convergence (agreed) but NOT how BURIED a pattern
  is. High-impact + deeply-buried = the literal definition of the target insight, and we have no metric for "buried."
- **No cross-pattern ANOMALY detection** — we compute per-node graph metrics but not "what is anomalous in THIS
  chart relative to its own baseline" — and anomalies are where discovery lives.
- **Discovery deferred to QUERY time** — "synthesize at query time" is right for ANSWERS but wrong for DISCOVERY:
  it makes discovery hostage to the LLM asking the right question (the exact failure we're escaping). Discoveries
  must be PRE-COMPUTED at build time as first-class artifacts.

## §3 — The fix: bo_anveshana (the 9th asset) + a data-engineering shift
**bo_anveshana (अन्वेषण = investigation/discovery)** — MINES the other 7 assets at build time for consequential
NON-OBVIOUS patterns; outputs a pre-computed, ranked, provenance-bearing DISCOVERY LIST the digest LEADS with.
Brief: CLAUDECODE_BRIEF_BO_ANVESHANA_v1_0.md. Four deterministic discovery primitives:
1. **Non-obviousness score** = high structural consequence × LOW surface salience (the buried-gold detector).
2. **Graph mining** — structural holes / brokers (the factor bridging two otherwise-disconnected worlds),
   community binders, motif-frequency anomalies, articulation points (igraph discovery algorithms — we used ~30%).
3. **Embedding outliers** — the signal whose meaning-vector is far from every cluster (the unused surprise primitive).
4. **Distributional within-chart anomalies** — Nσ outliers vs the chart's OWN baseline.

**The data-engineering elevation:** STOP using the graph + embeddings as retrieval INDICES and START using them as
discovery INSTRUMENTS — same storage, same determinism, but run the ANALYTICAL algorithms, not just the LOOKUP ones.

## §4 — Why this is aligned + safe (doesn't corrupt anything)
- It CONSUMES the 7 producing assets; reopens nothing.
- Deterministic (graph algorithms + embedding math + statistics); no LLM, no fabricated insight.
- Anti-drift ABSOLUTE — a discovery is a FOUND pattern that traces to real substrate, NEVER an invented one (a
  fabricated "insight" is worse than none; this is the gravest error class here).
- Serves BOTH pillars supremely: completeness (the buried pattern was already stored — now it's FOUND) +
  retrievability (the insight is reachable WITHOUT anyone knowing to ask — the digest leads with it).
- It is the L2→L5 bridge: the discovery NOVELTY register (which discovery-classes recur across charts) feeds the
  empirical research mission (L5 Mīmāṃsā) — turning the instrument from "reads one chart deeply" toward "advances
  astrology as a discipline."

We optimized each asset for HOLDING + SERVING maximum information (a supreme library). bo_anveshana optimizes for
FINDING the consequential thing no one would have looked for (a researcher). **The 8 assets make the instrument
COMPLETE + RETRIEVABLE; the 9th makes it DISCOVER.** Without it: a magnificent library. With it: the research
instrument the native actually described. The layer is now 9 assets.
