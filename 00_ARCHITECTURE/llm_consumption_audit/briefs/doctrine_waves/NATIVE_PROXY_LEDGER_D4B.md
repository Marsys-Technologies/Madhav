## NP-D4B-001 — 2026-07-21 — DR-17 grading weights: verbatim consumption by B-1

sub_peak=0.75, elevated=0.5, neutral=0.0, contra=−0.5, anti-hit double-weight=−1.0 for

scoring call?

**Ruling: RATIFIED VERBATIM — B-1 may consume all six weights exactly as proposed. No amendment
is required, and none is warranted.** This entry IS the ratification act DR-17 §4 and
D4B_PREREGISTRATION_PACKET_v1_0.md §9 stage ("the Binder/Adjudicator ratifies or amends before

2. **The encoding is a faithful, monotone translation of that ratified substance.** sub_peak at

## NP-D4B-002 — 2026-07-21 — "Cheaper-null" circular time-shift control: refused as primary; admissible only as a pre-registered, non-gate-bearing diagnostic

**Question.** A D-4b kickoff message proposed a "cheaper-null" control for compute-heavy
contenders: circular time-shift of the LEL events against the SAME chart's curve, in place of
birth-shifted recomputation. This method appears nowhere in the doctrine corpus (verified this
session: `grep -rn -i "circular\|time-shift"` across `doctrine_waves/` returns exactly one hit —
D4B_PREREGISTRATION_PACKET_v1_0.md line 63, about circular *reasoning* on the birth event, not a
control method). Only DR-15(c)'s coverage-matched shuffled-birth control, with antiphase as a
secondary check, is documented. May B-1 use circular time-shift at all, and under what
constraints, or must it stick to the documented DR-15(c) method only? (Note: this proxy has not
read the kickoff message itself — no path was supplied; the ruling is issued against the
question's characterization of it, and holds for any proposal matching that characterization.)

**Ruling: circular time-shift MAY NOT substitute for the DR-15(c) shuffled-birth control in any
gate-bearing computation, for any contender, at any compute cost. It MAY run only as a clearly
labeled supplementary diagnostic in the antiphase class — and only if pre-registered via a packet
version bump BEFORE B-1's first scoring call. For contenders whose shuffled-birth control is
genuinely compute-infeasible, the remedy is pre-committed scope-reduction, never null
substitution.**

**Reasoning (extrapolated from the native's own record):**

1. **The comparator IS the integrity gate.** `skill = 1 − CRPS_model/CRPS_control` (DR-15(b),
   packet §3, gate criterion 2) is defined BY the shuffled-birth control; DR-17 §1's `neutral`
   grade ("statistically indistinguishable from its coverage-matched shuffled-birth control") and
   §3's percentile-of-manifestation curve ("versus the MIRRORED shuffled-birth control") are
   likewise defined by it. Swapping the null redefines the win criterion, the grade vocabulary,
   and the calibration curve simultaneously. ESCALATION_POLICY §1 is explicit that integrity-gate
   assertions are never re-baselined below the native; the standing session rule forbids loosening
   a pre-registered threshold post-hoc. A comparator substitution whose strictness direction is
   unknown is treated as a loosening until proven otherwise — and here the direction is not even
   unknown, see (2).

2. **The cheaper null is plausibly a WEAKER null — on the merits, not just procedurally.**
   Shuffled-birth holds the real life fixed and varies the chart: it tests birth-specificity, the
   discipline's actual claim. Circular event-shift holds this one chart's curve fixed and
   displaces the events: it tests only temporal alignment against a single curve, and any model
   whose intensity concentrates in life-stages where events genuinely cluster (career events in
   the 20s–40s; the dasha spine is age-locked) beats its shifted null by base-rate capture alone —
   pseudo-skill the shuffled-birth control is immune to, since every synthetic chart's curve is
   equally age-structured. Wrap-around additionally scrambles event-age plausibility (a marriage
   displaced to age 3). Substituting an easier-to-beat null for the null that produced the
   campaign's most damning red (D-3: −16.1pp, the REAL chart scoring WORSE than shuffled-birth;
   robust to coverage-matching per STATE_D-3's A1 check) is control-shopping — the precise
   "gate-gaming" DR-15(d) names, and the precise temptation ADJUDICATOR_CHARGE §4 orders stopped
   at ("if you find yourself reasoning toward why a marginal or red gate is 'really' a pass,
   STOP").

3. **Pre-committed branches over improvisation.** DR-15(c) is native-ratified doctrine; packet §6
   is FROZEN and commits the mechanism verbatim ("ratified, unchanged"), with antiphase explicitly
   subordinated ("secondary robustness check, not the primary comparator"). A method appearing
   nowhere in the corpus cannot enter the binding harness by kickoff-message improvisation — that
   is the exact failure mode NP-D4B-001 refused for the grading weights, applied here to a surface
   with even higher gaming exposure. The D-4a Foundry's shuffled-birth + antiphase implementation
   is built, tested (52/52, REPORT_D-4A), and gated; a novel null would also be UNTESTED code on
   the integrity-critical path.

4. **Scope-reduction over compute — the native's own preference resolves the honest kernel of the
   request.** The compute concern is not frivolous (D-5's record shows a full sweep materialization
   is multi-hour per chart), but the packet's own §6 asserts `curve(chart, event_class, [t1,t2])`
   is "deterministic and cheap per the DR-15(c) contract." The pre-committed remedies, in order:
   (a) a contender claiming compute-infeasibility must first DEMONSTRATE it — a measured, cited
   cost figure against the actual harness path, not an assumption; (b) if demonstrated, reduce N
   for that contender (below the packet's proposed 1000) via packet v1.1+ committed BEFORE scoring,
   with the widened control-distribution uncertainty reported per CHARGE §1.5 (rule on the
   interval, never a bare point); (c) if even reduced-N is infeasible, the contender is scored but
   flagged `control_not_evaluable_under_primary` and EXCLUDED from champion selection — a contender
   that cannot run its DR-15(c) control cannot be named winner, because winning is DEFINED as
   beating that control; its row enters the per-model per-event table honestly, feeding the
   no-winner branch if applicable. Honest exclusion over a substituted green.

**Binding conditions on the diagnostic use (the only permitted use):**

- **(a) Pre-registration first.** Circular time-shift runs in B-1 only if a packet version (v1.1+)
  committed BEFORE the first scoring call specifies: the offset-sampling scheme, N, the
  wrap-around handling, seed discipline (same sha256-derived scheme as packet §6), and DR-13(e)
  mirroring (every scoring loosening applies identically to the shifted events). Not committed
  before first scoring call → it does not run in B-1 at all. No mid-run addition, ever.
- **(b) Non-gate-bearing, everywhere.** It never enters `CRPS_control`, never defines `neutral`,
  never feeds gate criteria 2/5/9, never influences champion selection or the no-winner branch.
  Served/reported rows carry a distinct label (e.g. `control_class:
  circular_event_shift_diagnostic`) so no downstream consumer can mistake it for the primary
  control — §N.6 density discipline: never flattened into one undifferentiated control column.
- **(c) Weakness disclosed with every use.** Any report citing it states that a same-curve null
  does not test birth-specificity and is vulnerable to age-base-rate capture (reasoning point 2).
- **(d) Asymmetric admissibility.** As a diagnostic it may only ever make a result LOOK WORSE,
  never better: a contender beating shuffled-birth but failing circular-shift is a finding worth
  reporting; a contender failing shuffled-birth but beating circular-shift changes nothing. This
  is the same asymmetry the antiphase secondary already carries in practice (BIND_D-4A:
  pratyantar_lord beating antiphase in 4/5 domains did not soften its shuffled-birth red).
- **(e) Scope of this entry.** This ruling covers control construction for B-1 only. It does not
  touch the packet's §9 pending items (week-tier tolerance, N=1000/seed ratification, 54-vs-56
  subset), which still require their own adjudication entries.

**Precedents relied on:** DR_14_15_16_TEMPORAL_DOCTRINE_v1_0.md DR-15(b)/(c)/(d) (native-ratified
control definition; pre-registration; post-hoc adjustment = gate-gaming); DR-17 §1/§3/§5
(shuffled-birth wired into `neutral` + percentile curve; never widened post-hoc to convert red to
green); D4B_PREREGISTRATION_PACKET_v1_0.md frontmatter + §6 + §9 (FROZEN; amendment = new version
BEFORE next scoring call; antiphase subordinated); BRIEF_D4B §1 B-1 hard rules ("same controls per
DR-15(c)"; no-winner branch pre-committed; anti-gaming verifier) + §5 gate criteria 2/5/9;
DR_13_EVENT_SCORING_SEMANTICS_DRAFT (DIS.026) mirroring rule; STATE_D-3 §G check (c) + A1
coverage-matched control check (the −16.1pp red and its robustness — the null being replaced is
the one that found it); REPORT_D-4A A-3 (controls real, 52/52, gated); ADJUDICATOR_CHARGE §1.2
(integrity-gate assertions never re-baselined) + §4 (stop where green-softening temptation is
strongest); ESCALATION_POLICY §1 (integrity-gate carve-out, native-only) + §2.1;
NP-D4B-001 (pre-committed branches over improvisation; verbatim-adoption-as-scope-reduction);
native session charge (honesty over green; scope-reduction over compute).

**Falsifier (what would change this ruling):** (i) a committed, pre-registered calibration study
(synthetic or cross-chart, zero sealed-test-split contact) demonstrating that circular time-shift's
null distribution is as-strict-or-stricter than shuffled-birth's on this corpus class — i.e., the
"weaker null" reasoning in point 2 is empirically wrong; presented to the native at B-6, this
would open (native-only, never proxy-ruled) the question of promoting it to co-primary in a fresh
packet version with full re-scoring; or (ii) the native at B-6 rules the birth-specificity framing
itself is not the question B-1's control must answer. Neither finding retroactively validates any
score computed under a substituted null before such a ruling — those would remain void.

**Reversibility:** fully reversible in the permissive direction — this entry forbids an action
rather than taking one; the diagnostic door stays open via packet version bump, and per-model
per-event tables (BRIEF_D4B §1 B-1) persist so re-scoring under any future native-amended control
is a deterministic recompute. In the restrictive direction it is deliberately hard to reverse:
promotion of circular time-shift to a gate-bearing role is carved out as native-only (falsifier
(i)/(ii)), consistent with the integrity-gate carve-out this proxy is bound by.

*— end NP-D4B-002*

## NP-D4B-003 — 2026-07-21 — §4 tie-band widths (±3d/±7d/±45d/±180d): adopted as this run's pre-registered operational constants, with a mandatory DR-13(d)-width sensitivity check

*(Ledger note: this file was re-materialized into the d4b-open worktree this session from the
scratchpad recovery copy; NP-D4B-001 and NP-D4B-002 above are carried verbatim, unmodified.)*

**Question.** D4B_PREREGISTRATION_PACKET v1.1 corrected the one confirmed Opus-verifier defect:
its §4 tie-band table (day ±3d / week ±7d / month ±45d / year ±180d) had been mis-headed "DR-17
§2" and committed "as the binding values for B-1" — but DR-17 §2 names no such table; it defines
the tie-band only as the model's own pre-registered date-uncertainty band and points to DR-13(d)'s
date_confidence-scaled tolerance (exact ±45d / month ±75d / year-only secondary battery), which
the day/month rows contradict and which contains no week or year day-count at all. v1.1 re-casts
the four widths as this packet's OWN newly-proposed operational constants, explicitly not
doctrine-derived, pending this seat's ruling. May B-1 adopt them, or must they be replaced with
DR-13(d)'s actual 2-tier values, or some other disposition?

**Ruling: ADOPTED — B-1 may consume the four widths (day ±3d / week ±7d / month ±45d / year
±180d) as THIS RUN's pre-registered operational tie-band constants, under the v1.1 corrected
attribution (packet-proposed, NOT doctrine-derived) and the binding conditions below — the most
important of which is condition (d), a mandatory, deterministic DR-13(d)-width sensitivity
recompute, because the day and month widths as adopted are NARROWER than the doctrine-pointed
alternative and narrower tie-bands cut in the model-favorable direction. Replacing the table with
DR-13(d)'s 2-tier values is REFUSED as the binding choice, but those values are NOT discarded —
they are carried as the pre-registered sensitivity comparator. Promotion of either set to doctrine
status is native-only, at B-6.**

**Reasoning (extrapolated from the native's own record):**

1. **This is a ratification-stage choice, not a post-hoc change — and the four widths are the
   pre-committed branch.** DR-17 §4 lists "the tie-band mechanism" itself among the PROPOSED
   harness-spec elements the Binder/Adjudicator ratifies or amends before B-1 consumes them; the
   native's registered substance (STATE_D-5 gate_run_2) covers graded acceptance, multi-modal
   legitimacy, and anti-farming-via-frozen-tie-bands — not any specific day-count. So NEITHER
   candidate table is ratified doctrine for the tie-band role; both stand before this seat. Between
   them, the four widths are the ones actually committed in packet v1.0 AND v1.1 (v1.1 changed
   only the attribution, not one numeral), no scoring call has consumed anything, and the ruling is
   issued BLIND (no contender score or per-event grade has been computed under either table by
   this proxy). Swapping to DR-13(d) numbers NOW would itself be the improvised fresh-numbers move
   this ledger refused twice already (NP-D4B-001 pt.3, NP-D4B-002 pt.3). Adopting the committed
   table verbatim is the pre-committed, scope-reducing path.

2. **The mechanism-mismatch argument is accepted on the merits.** DR-13(d)'s tolerances answer
   "does a served point count as a HIT against the true date"; the tie-band answers "when are two
   served peaks ONE signal rather than two." The packet's §4 correction states this distinction
   honestly and the doctrine record supports it: DR-13(d) was authored (DIS.026) with no
   tie-band concept in view, and DR-17 §2's pointer to it reads as the spec author reaching for
   the only existing confidence-scaled numbers, not as a considered native choice of dedup widths.
   Forcing the 2-tier values into the 4-tier role also fails concretely: (i) the corpus genuinely
   spans four date_confidence granularities — the week tier exists because the NATIVE's own
   date-tightening (item #12, the anti-hit specimen EVT.2022.XX.XX.02) introduced it, and DR-13(d)
   simply has no value for it; (ii) any hybrid (DR-13(d) where defined, packet values elsewhere)
   is non-monotone — day ±45d wider than week ±7d — meaning a model declaring SHARPER confidence
   would receive a COARSER dedup band than a vaguer one, which is incoherent as a function of
   declared uncertainty. The adopted table is monotone (3 ≤ 7 ≤ 45 ≤ 180) and each width is the
   natural scale of its tier's own uncertainty (a day-confidence model's near-duplicate peaks are
   plausibly one signal within ~a few days, not within a month and a half).

3. **The strictness direction is honestly named, not waved off — and it is bounded by symmetry.**
   For the day (±3d vs ±45d) and month (±45d vs ±75d) tiers the adopted bands are NARROWER than
   the DR-13(d)-pointed alternative, and a narrower tie-band is the model-favorable direction for
   the gaming vector DR-17 §2 exists to block: near-identical peaks separately graded and SUMMED
   can farm multiple `sub_peak` credits for one real signal. Two bounds contain this: (i)
   **control-mirroring (DR-13(e), non-negotiable)** — the identical tie-band widths apply to the
   shuffled-birth control's own scoring pass, so any farming inflation is symmetric on both sides
   of every control-relative comparison (the DR-17 percentile-of-manifestation curve is defined
   "versus the MIRRORED shuffled-birth control"); and (ii) the primary win criterion is CRPS
   (DR-15(b)), which does not consume tie-bands at all — the tie-band feeds gate criterion 9's
   DR-17 weighted census and B-3's work, never champion selection by weighted sum (NP-D4B-001
   condition (c)). What symmetry does NOT bound is absolute-level readings of a single contender's
   weighted census — hence condition (d) below, which is this ruling's price of adoption.

4. **Refusing the DR-13(d) replacement is not refusing DR-13(d).** §3's point-tolerances (exact
   ±45d / month ±75d / year-only secondary battery) stand untouched as the HIT criterion — DR-11's
   figure unamended, per DR-13's own non-scope. This ruling assigns DR-13(d)'s numbers no NEW role,
   and strips them of none they actually hold.

**Binding conditions (tightening, not loosening):**

- **(a) This-run constants only, corrected attribution permanent.** The four widths bind THIS
  run's harness under the v1.1 framing: packet-proposed operational constants, NOT doctrine. No
  future artifact may cite them as "DR-17 §2 values." Promotion to doctrine status (a DR-17
  amendment naming a tie-band table) is native-only, at the B-6 batch review — never this seat.
- **(b) Frozen at first scoring call; refusal-guard covers the full vector.** DR-17 §2's
  harness-refusal guard applies to all four widths as a unit (same extension NP-D4B-001 condition
  (a) made for the grade weights): the harness raises and refuses to run if its live tie-band
  parameters differ from the packet-committed values. Any change after B-1's first scoring call
  requires a new packet version committed BEFORE a fresh, complete re-scoring run, prior runs'
  persisted tables retained and superseded openly.
- **(c) Control-mirroring asserted, not assumed.** The identical four widths apply to the
  shuffled-birth control's (and any pre-registered secondary control's) scoring pass. The harness
  refuses to run under any real-vs-control tie-band asymmetry — this is DR-13(e) applied to the
  tie-band, stated here so it is checkable, not inferred.
- **(d) Mandatory DR-13(d)-width sensitivity check (the doctrine-pointed alternative is the
  comparator, not the discard).** After B-1's scoring, the harness recomputes the DR-17 weighted
  census per contender under the substitute widths day ±45d / month ±75d (week and year tiers
  unchanged at ±7d/±180d — DR-13(d) defines no value there), as a deterministic re-grade over the
  persisted per-model per-event tables — no re-scoring, no fresh model calls, no sealed-split
  contact. B-1's report discloses per contender: the count of specimens where the two tables
  disagree (peak-pairs merged under one width and summed under the other), and both weighted
  totals. If ANY contender's gate-criterion-9 outcome or any cross-contender census ORDERING flips
  between the two tables, that contender's DR-17 result is flagged `tie_band_sensitive: true` in
  its served/reported rows (§N.6 — a density flag, never silently flattened) and the flip is
  surfaced to the native at B-6 as a named finding. The sensitivity check is diagnostic under
  NP-D4B-002 condition (d)'s asymmetry: it may make a result look WORSE or less certain, never
  better — a contender failing under the adopted widths is not rescued by passing under the
  comparator widths.
- **(e) No role leakage.** The tie-band table governs peak-deduplication ONLY. Any harness code
  path consuming these four widths as a HIT tolerance (or consuming §3's ±45d/±75d as a tie-band)
  is a defect requiring halt-and-fix before scoring proceeds — the two mechanisms stay distinct in
  code as they now are in the packet's prose.
- **(f) Scope of this entry.** This ruling covers the §4 tie-band widths ONLY. Still pending their
  own adjudication entries before first scoring call: §3's week-confidence POINT-tolerance (±7d —
  numerically aligned with the week tie-band by the packet's own de-circularized framing, but a
  separate constant in a separate mechanism; this ruling does not ratify it by adjacency), the
  control sample design (N=1000, seed scheme), and the 54-vs-56 scoring-subset recommendation
  (per NP-D4B-001 condition (e), unchanged).

**Precedents relied on:** STATE_D-5 `native_disposition_gate_run_2` (DR-17 substance registered;
numerals left to ratification); DR_17_18_MANIFESTATION_CENSUS_DOCTRINE_v1_0.md §2 (tie-band
mechanism + refusal guard; the DR-13(d) pointer whose misreading v1.1 corrected) + §4 ("proposed,
