## §1 — The declared intent (native's words, formalized)

The cosmos continuously generates configurations — grahas streaming through rāśis, bhāvas,
nakshatras, kakṣyās, read differently by different darśanas. The instrument shall look at these
permutations THE WAY THE CHART LISTENS TO THEM: identify, relative to a natal chart, the subset
of configurations above a stated confidence for a given event-class — auspicious AND
inauspicious, symmetric — and answer four questions as views over one object, the per-chart,
per-event-class intensity λ_e(t | chart):

RETRODICTION (score the lived past) · ACTIVATION ("when will X likely manifest?") · FORECAST
("what does window T hold?") · ELECTION/AVOIDANCE ("when to act; when NOT to act" — muhurta as
argmax over the signed field, suppression windows served as first-class answers).

Deep natal understanding is the tractability principle: the chart is a SPARSE FILTER. The sky's
millions of permutations are projected onto the chart's resonance map — its graha degrees,
cusps, mechanism nodes, sensitive degrees, arudhas, yoga constituents, daśā-lord portfolios —
not enumerated. Events UNFOLD (point / interval / chain with irreversibility milestones, per
DR-13); the engine speaks in that grammar and never with more temporal precision than an
event-class's shape supports.
