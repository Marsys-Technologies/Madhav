# Jaimini Upadesa Sutras — Adhyayas 3–4 Corpus Gap

## Status

**CONFIRMED GENUINE GAP.** No English translation of Jaimini Upadesa Sutras
Adhyayas 3–4 is publicly available. Resolution requires a Sanskrit scholar.

Carry-forward: v3.7+ corpus expansion.

## Procurement Attempts (v3.6 pass)

### Attempt 1 — B.S. Rao 1955 Edition
- Source: `archive.org/details/Jaiminisutras1955EditionByBSRao`
- Result: Contains Adhyayas 1–2 only. Already ingested in prior MCPT phases.
- Finding: No Adhyaya 3–4 content in this edition.

### Attempt 2 — K.V. Abhyankar (KVA) Edition
- Source: `archive.org/details/jaimini-kva-a-4-size`
- Table of contents: Lists Adhyaya 3 heading
- Result: Actual sutra content terminates after Adhyaya 2 Pada 4 (17,187 lines checked)
- Scholarly note from KVA text: *"The commentaries and translations terminate at the end of
  the first two Adhyayas; only the Sutra Patha of the last two Adhyayas is available
  and that too…"*
- The KVA djvu.txt (alternative Adhyaya 1–2 translation) downloaded to this directory
  as `jaimini_kva_abhyankar_djvu.txt` for optional supplemental ingestion.

### Attempt 3 — 1911 Archive Edition
- Source: archive.org 1911 edition search
- Result: Contains *Mimamsa Sutras* (philosophical text by Jaimini the philosopher),
  NOT the Jaimini Upadesa Sutras (astrological text). Wrong corpus entirely.

## Currently Ingested

- B.S. Rao 1955 translation of Adhyayas 1–2 (ingested in MCPT v3.2)
- KVA alternative translation available locally (optional supplemental — not yet ingested)

## Resolution Path

Adhyayas 3–4 exist in Sanskrit only. A Sanskrit-to-English translation by a qualified
