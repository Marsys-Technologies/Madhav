# Madhav — the product we intend to build

## 1. The defining promise

Madhav is a Jyotish intelligence, prediction and discovery instrument. It connects the depth of the astrological tradition, the distinctive structure of a chart, the movement of time and disciplined investigation into understanding that a person can interrogate, retain and hold to account.

Its purpose is not to be a faster chart calculator, an elaborate placement report or a general chatbot decorated with astrological vocabulary. Its purpose is to extend the breadth of knowledge, depth of examination, number of relevant relationships, temporal resolution and continuity of inquiry that a human consultation can sustain. The center of the product is astrological: graha, rāśi, bhāva, kāraka, sambandha, bala, varga, yoga and kāla must contribute their qualified meaning, not merely their names or their row counts.

The intended experience is: “I understand something consequential that I could not see before. I see the connected basis, the alternatives, the timing where support permits, and what remains uncertain. I can challenge it, revisit it and tell whether the product was wrong.” Wonder is welcome. Clarity, specificity and earned trust are the obligations.

Three inseparable commitments define the product:

- **Understand:** reveal distinctive capacities, tensions, configurations, cross-domain relationships and their competing interpretations.
- **Navigate:** explain what persists, what changes, what may activate, how possible manifestations differ and what can responsibly be forecast or compared.
- **Account:** preserve the question, admitted evidence, interpretation, forecast and subsequent challenge; learn only through qualified, visible processes.

Prediction is central, not an optional ornament. Scholarship, exact computation, meaningful explanation and inquiry are also valuable in their own right. A reading need not invent a forecast to justify its existence.

### 1.1 The meaning of beyond-Acharya

“Beyond-Acharya” is our design ambition: to connect relevant knowledge and alternatives at a scale and depth beyond an individual's working memory. It is not a claim of demonstrated universal superiority, spiritual authority, supernatural access or established predictive accuracy.

The achievement must be earned on named dimensions: applicable domain coverage, correct relational depth, source fidelity, temporal discrimination, useful manifestation specificity, controlled comparisons and accountability. A larger corpus or a more persuasive AI answer does not establish that an astrological proposition is true. Expert recognition is evidence about interpretive quality, not proof of predictive validity. Public comparative claims must identify their test, comparison group, information budget and limitations.

The product must be able to discover both valuable interpretations and the limits of astrology's claims. It must not turn absent or allegedly lost knowledge into invented certainty. The ambition is expansive; the truth claims remain proportionate to evidence.

### 1.2 Quality before speed; completeness before polish

An interpretive question is an investigation. Processing speed is subordinate to depth, breadth, concept coverage, connected interpretation and predictive usefulness. The system must retrieve, examine and revise until its material obligations are met or its actual limits are declared.

There is no arbitrary mandatory pass count, premium-only intelligence tier or instruction to keep thinking indefinitely. Effort follows the question and the emerging evidence. Progress, cancellation, checkpoints and resumption make deep work usable. Existing spending and safety limits remain until separately aligned; exhausting a limit produces an explicitly incomplete investigation, not a shortened answer presented as complete.

### 1.3 The unit of value: an earned distinction

The unit of value is something the person can now understand, discriminate, prepare for or test that a competent simpler answer did not provide. A contribution may change the central conclusion, reveal an assumption, reduce uncertainty, expose counter-evidence, distinguish manifestations or prevent an unjustified forecast. It need not change the headline verdict to matter.

More assets, obscure techniques, agreement counts, words or diagrams are not intrinsically more value. Every addition must show what it contributes. The durable product is the inquiry and its evolving understanding, not an ever-longer report or a new universal database.

## 2. Who the product serves and what they should be able to ask

The primary consumer seeks serious understanding of their own life and may know no Jyotish terminology. The product also serves authorized source study, difficult interpretive comparison and separately qualified research. The native remains the governed proving context; broader enrollment or third-party access requires its own authority.

A person is not a chart. An interpretation does not establish moral worth, fixed identity, another person's thoughts or an inevitable destiny. Choices, circumstances and practical evidence remain real. The product must strengthen agency, allow disagreement and make stopping easy. It must not cultivate dependence through omens, fear, manufactured intimacy or endless follow-up prompts.

The following is a target portfolio, not a current availability list. “Core” means essential to the intended product, not automatically enabled for every audience. Horizons require separate subject, method and release decisions. The stable P identifiers preserve continuity with the previous draft.

An unanswered valuable question is an elevation requirement when a missing qualified operator is the obstacle; current implementation gaps must not permanently shrink the ambition. Genuine output exclusions remain exclusions.

## 3. The Jyotish substance: what depth actually requires

This section defines coverage obligations, not a compulsory sequence or a rule that every technique applies to every question. A method must have qualified sources, conventions, prerequisites, exceptions and an explainable contribution. Source richness without correct application is not domain completeness.

### 3.1 Graha as a contextual participant

The system must distinguish a graha's natural significations, chart-specific functional role, house lordships, kāraka roles, placement, relationships, condition and temporal participation. These roles can support different or opposing interpretations. A naturally benefic label does not settle functional benefit; a difficult condition does not erase every capacity.

Aliases, Sanskrit names, transliterations and symbols resolve to canonical identities in Brahmagyan. A downstream asset must not invent its own Sun, house, nakshatra, dignity vocabulary or relationship type merely because the names look equivalent. Identity equivalence does not erase different doctrinal meanings.

### 3.2 Rāśi, bhāva, lord and kāraka as a relational architecture

Domain understanding must consider the relevant house, its occupants, lord, significators, associated houses and connected domains under the selected method. Sign position and house position are distinct when the method makes them distinct. Reference frame and house convention must be explicit.

The product must connect, for example, the interpretation of obtaining resources with retaining them, recognition with authority and responsibility, and a relationship with its wider life context. Such connections require an actual qualified rule or derivation, not free association from domain labels.

### 3.3 Condition, dignity, bala and avasthā

Relevant dignity, strength components, motion, combustion, proximity, planetary state and other qualified conditions must retain their separate meanings, units and scope. A single score cannot silently collapse capacity, beneficence, ease of expression, prominence and probability of an event.

Where a strength system's components disagree, preserve the disagreement and its contribution. An exact calculated number is not an exact life prediction. Missing computation is a named gap, never a neutral-looking default. Narration of the number requires semantic verification separate from arithmetic verification.

### 3.4 Sambandha and chains of influence within the interpretation

Aspects, conjunctions, exchanges, dispositors, nakshatra-linked dependencies, argalā/virodha and other admitted relationships must be typed and method-qualified. Conjunction alone does not give every planet the same role; the selected aspect tradition and any applicable orb or exception must remain visible.

The system must follow material chains beyond one hop, handle cycles, identify decisive intermediaries and test inhibiting or cancelling relationships. A chain may connect domains or resolve an apparent contradiction. It may also be incomplete or unsupported. Graph connectivity is neither a physical causal mechanism nor proof that the end event will happen.

Shared roots must remain visible. Several assets derived from one placement are not independent confirmations. Their different interpretations may still add value; count their contribution without manufacturing independent evidence.

### 3.5 Bhāvat Bhāvam and derived reference frames

When a qualified method makes Bhāvat Bhāvam relevant, investigate the actual derived-house relationship, its basis, participating lords/significators, conditions and interaction with the primary domain. State the supported scope rather than extrapolating a partial implementation into universal coverage.

Mentioning the term, calling a tool or returning an empty result is not proof of application. A settled reading must apply the concept where supported and relevant, explain why it is inapplicable, or identify its qualification/computation gap. The same standard applies to every other material concept the initial planner could omit.

### 3.6 Varga: domain-specific resolution with sensitivity

Divisional charts must be used with their qualified domain, construction method, reference conventions and input sensitivity. A varga is not an interchangeable magnifying glass or another independent vote on the same natal input.

Explain convergence and tension between rāśi and relevant varga findings, including what changes when uncertain birth inputs cross a boundary. Distinguish domain support, configuration repetition, vargottama and other qualified conditions. Do not infer precise manifestation from a fine division whose required input precision is absent.

### 3.7 Yoga, doṣa, cancellation and manifestation

Each named configuration requires its actual formation conditions, participants, strengths and limitations, applicable exceptions, cancellations and source/method identity. A catalog label is not a confirmed formation. Formation is not full effectiveness; cancellation of a difficulty is not automatically a rāja-yoga outcome.

For Nīcha-bhaṅga Rāja Yoga, for example, distinguish the stated debility, the specific cancellation condition, the further qualifications for the stronger interpretation, the participating mechanism and possible expressions. The product must not upgrade relief from impairment into promised power or wealth.

Named configurations are only part of interpretation. Ordinary combinations, conflicting indications and periods without spectacular yogas deserve equally serious analysis. No doṣa label may become a moral verdict, frightening identity or commercial remedy funnel.

### 3.8 Nakshatra, pada, symbolic meaning and specialized significators

Nakshatra and pada data must connect to qualified roles, relationships, timing and interpretation rather than remain decorative labels. Symbolic, deity-related or mythic material must retain source context; it is not literal biographical evidence.

KP or other specialized sub-lord/cusp/significator approaches must operate on their own qualified conventions and complete prerequisites, including applicable nodal agency and exceptions. A shared vocabulary does not authorize importing a school-specific rule into a different system without explanation.

### 3.9 Ārūḍha, special lagnas and alternative perspectives

Ārūḍha, special lagnas and other admitted reference perspectives can distinguish resources, appearance, standing or other method-specific facets that a single view conceals. Their computations, exceptions and interpretive scope must be explicit.

The product should reveal meaningful differences between underlying capacity, perceived status and realized circumstances when qualified support exists. It must not force agreement across frames or pretend alternative perspectives are independent observations of life.

### 3.10 Kāla: structure meeting time

The temporal layer is a major source of distinctive value because it asks which enduring structures are engaged, how and when. Daśā systems and their nested periods, gochara, relevant transit conditions, annual/return methods, Tājaka, tithi-praveśa, Sudarśana, aṣṭakavarga, vedha, sensitive contacts and other admitted temporal instruments retain method-specific roles.

Compute boundaries, calendars, location/time-zone conventions, hierarchy and reference frames correctly. Distinguish a background period, enabling interval, specific contact, inhibiting condition, recurrence and inferred manifestation. A transit coincidence is not a complete activation mechanism. A precise astronomical timestamp does not confer equivalent precision on a forecast.

Compare nearest and stronger later windows using a named criterion. Explain why several apparent triggers differ, what they share, what opposes them and what remains unknown. The search horizon, resolution and method coverage bound any “no eligible window” conclusion.

Kāla must support cross-domain trajectories: how work, resources, relocation, relationships or responsibility may interact over a chapter; what is stable, which qualified windows overlap and which expressions compete. Do not impose a universal preparation–opening–success–consolidation story or infer that one forecast event causes another. Moving from a long chapter to a short interval must preserve the same evidence identities.

### 3.11 Pañcāṅga, Praśna and Muhūrta are distinct practices

General calendar context, personalized relevance, the casting of a question-moment chart, and selecting an initiation interval are different operations. Each needs its own inputs and applicability. Paripraśna is a conversational channel; it is not by its name proof that a Praśna method has been executed.

Electional work must incorporate the actual undertaking, constraints, location and applicable method; distinguish initiation suitability from eventual outcome. Existing Praśna cast and Muhurat Finder services are reusable partial capital, not proof of fully qualified specialized practice. A calendar entry must not acquire a personal prediction merely by being shown on a chart page.

### 3.12 Remedies, practice and the breadth of the tradition

Voluntary, attributed remedial or spiritual practices may help someone understand the tradition and make an informed choice within the product's safety boundaries. Distinguish source testimony, symbolic significance, practical burden and demonstrated efficacy; the last must never be inferred from the first.

The product can study Jyotish across its mathematical/astronomical, natal/interrogational/electional and broader contextual traditions. Siddhānta, Horā and Saṃhitā supply a wider intellectual horizon than a natal report. Institutional descriptions support this taxonomy, not the empirical validity of all associated claims. Qualified non-natal, relational, entity, spatial/Vāstu or collective work requires separate scope; corpus presence does not enable it automatically.

## 4. Scholarship, method plurality and discovery

Six kinds of knowledge remain distinguishable throughout the product: source testimony; qualified rule; computed configuration; interpretive inference; real-world claim; and evaluation. A faithful passage establishes what a source says, not that every application is true. Correct computation does not choose a unique manifestation. A plausible interpretation does not validate its forecast.

Build a coherent reading within each applicable method before comparing methods. Preserve conventions, sources, exceptions and disagreements. Do not average incompatible schools, count them as interchangeable votes or resolve every disagreement by “the oldest book wins.” Distinguish input differences from doctrinal disagreement, and unresolved disagreement from missing implementation.

### 4.1 From a source to its actual interpretive consequence

The person should be able to move from a rights-permitted passage, chapter or whole text to its language, transliteration/gloss where useful, context, edition, translation, variants and commentary. A summary is not the passage. Source relations can confirm, contradict, partially support, extend or remain silent about a proposition.

An inspectable rule dossier joins textual testimony, scope, prerequisites, exceptions, disputed readings, implementation qualification, worked examples and evaluation status. It is a view over existing authorities, not a new independent rule store. “Show me why this exception matters” should reach the actual difference it makes in a qualified example or reading.

### 4.2 Discovery at three levels

First, discover overlooked implications of already qualified knowledge in a chart. Second, recover meaningful distinctions through source scholarship and comparison. Third, propose genuinely new hypotheses for testing. These levels have different authority and must not collapse into one confident narrative.

Allegedly lost knowledge may be investigated through identifiable witnesses, manuscripts, editions, commentaries and explicitly labeled reconstruction. AI must never fabricate a recovered verse, source or ancient doctrine. A full critical-edition/manuscript laboratory is a valuable separately scoped scholarly horizon, not a prerequisite for ordinary qualified source learning.

### 4.3 A contrastive observatory

The research ambition is to learn where an interpretation helps and where it breaks: configurations without the proposed outcome, outcomes without the proposed configuration, exceptions, ordinary cases and rival explanations. Predeclare selection/similarity criteria before held-out outcomes are exposed. Account for correlated cases, missingness, repeated model selection and multiple exploratory tests.

The output is a scoped support/exception map, uncertainty and a discriminating next study—not a collection of confirming biographies. Synthetic cases test engineering, not real-life predictive validity; public biographies are not automatically qualified cohorts. Research access, fitting, promotion and service activation require separate authority and independent evaluation. Failures remain visible when a refined hypothesis is proposed.

## 5. The investigator: from a question to an earned reading

The engine must understand and, where useful, reformulate the question; identify the intended outcome and horizon; ask only consequential clarifications; establish authorized subject and method; consult the whole-chart foundation; retrieve relevant facts and rules; explore material relations, prerequisites and adjacent domains; examine alternatives; and revisit its analysis as new evidence changes the problem.

New evidence must be able to trigger further retrieval or qualified computation, not merely another paragraph. Adjacency is justified by a relationship to the question, not unlimited exploration. A research question, precise lookup and personal interpretation require different obligations.

Interpretive queries retain the governed whole-chart Bodha consultation before domain detail, including the relevant synthesis and cross-domain links. Pinpointed factual lookups retain B.11's frame-check/session-pin and escalation carve-out; interpretation must not be disguised as a lookup to avoid that floor.

### 5.1 A coverage discipline that challenges the initial planner

Maintain an explicit, expandable map of question obligations, applicable concepts, facts, relationship paths, candidate findings and unresolved frontiers. An independent omission check asks what the planner never selected: an important rule, missing exception, severed chain, contradictory configuration, unexamined reference frame or unavailable source field.

Each material obligation ends as applied, inapplicable with reason, unavailable, unqualified, contradictory/unresolved or still unexplored. These states are not interchangeable. A settled interpretation requires three closures: applicable-concept coverage; material relationship investigation; and delivery of the resulting relevant findings. It may be complete about an unresolved disagreement. It cannot call an unexplored applicable concept resolved.

This is accountable completeness against an explicit scope, not mathematical exhaustiveness over an open-ended tradition. The map must expand when novel combinations expose an omission. Tests must deliberately remove Bhāvat Bhāvam, a decisive cancellation or a link in a chain and verify that the investigation notices.

### 5.2 Competing interpretations and typed confidence

Significant interpretive judgments retain the current PPR-02 requirement for at least three candidate interpretations or an explicit monitored waiver. Do not fabricate alternatives to satisfy a count, and do not force three rival answers to an exact factual lookup. Explain the selected interpretation, material rivals and what would change the conclusion.

Keep deterministic fact, structural prior, classical prior, empirically calibrated claim and unresolved interpretation distinct. A probability, a comparative structural grade, an astronomical timestamp and a reliability interval are not substitutes. Numeric confidence must not imply empirical calibration that has not passed its gates.

### 5.3 The answer must carry the findings, not hide them behind a drill

The delivered reading begins with a clear synthesis and includes every identified relevant, authorized fact and its interpreted contribution. Where significance is conjoint, explain what the connected facts mean together. Include counter-evidence, inhibiting conditions, alternative manifestations and unresolved findings. Group repeated facts once with cross-references; do not silently discard them for brevity.

The complete findings belong to the same delivered reading. Detailed passages, calculations and comparison records may sit beneath them, but a later follow-up question must not be required to discover an already identified material finding. Transport limits require numbered durable parts and an explicit completion manifest; the first summary is not the complete answer.

The user receives evidence, derivations and decision rationale, not private internal deliberation. Plain-language explanation must preserve conceptual precision. Technical language, persona, provider or display preference cannot create a lower substantive standard. Unsupported prose cannot be introduced after evidence admission merely because it reads well.

Keep the semantic objects distinct: evidence is admitted support; a claim is a typed proposition; a reading is the coherent answer at that moment; its receipt binds coverage, derivations, gaps, qualification and provenance to what was delivered; an inquiry is the continuing question; and an observation is purpose-qualified testimony or adjudication. They are responsibilities over existing authorities, not an instruction to build parallel stores. Receipt fields need real checks or honest nulls.

### 5.4 The question compass

Suggest a few consequential next investigations: a real uncertainty, a sensitive input, an unqualified exception, a contradictory relation or a distinction between outcomes. State why the investigation matters, the smallest admissible next step and its burden. The next step may be computation or source study; it is not automatically more private biography.

Rank by probable information value and practical consequence, not emotional intensity, user retention or fear. Sometimes the right answer is that more analysis is unlikely to add material value.

## 6. Controlled comparison and two states of understanding

Three comparisons must remain distinct:

| Comparison | What changes | What it can establish |
|---|---|---|
| Input sensitivity | A declared uncertain input or computational convention | Which findings survive, weaken, disappear, reverse or cannot be assessed under the tested variants. |
| Method comparison | A qualified rule, school or interpretive operator | Dependence on that method and meaningful agreement/disagreement. Removing a method does not simulate removing a planet's influence. |
| Feasible-choice comparison | Actual options, objectives, constraints and timing assumptions | Differences in supported interpretation and uncertainty alongside practical considerations—not experimentally observed alternate lives. |

Each comparison requires real alternate computation or qualified application, pinned inputs/versions and matched claim identities. Two unconstrained AI essays are not a controlled comparison. Stability across tested conventions does not itself establish real-world truth.

The person must also be able to compare two states of understanding: what changed because of corrected birth input, a different question, new source knowledge, a changed method/model, newly available computation or an earlier error—and what did not change. Preserve the original claim and reason for revision. Faithful replay is not recomputation; regeneration is not faithful replay.

Optional material-change notifications may support continuity, but remain quiet, permissioned and consequence-based. This definition does not create a monitor, notification service or cross-channel transcript replication.

## 7. Prediction: specific when earned, accountable when emitted

The central chain is:

**formation and condition → eligible structural mechanism → applicable temporal activation → qualified manifestation alternatives → permitted external-outcome forecast.**

Every link needs its own support. The chain may stop before the last link; say precisely where. A catalog match is not formation, formation is not manifestation, and temporal activity is not proof of an event. Conversely, where qualified support exists, Madhav should make the permitted forecast with appropriate specificity. Avoiding all affirmative claims would not fulfill the product promise.

### 7.1 Manifestation and temporal precision

A useful promotion inquiry distinguishes title, authority, workload, recognition and pay. A financial inquiry distinguishes receiving money from retaining it and temporary relief from structural recovery. A marriage inquiry must clarify the event meant where that changes the forecast. Do not force spectacular events when the support concerns ordinary expression.

Compare closest eligible triggers and better-supported later windows under explicit criteria, uncertainty and coverage. “Strongest” is not an unexplained scalar. “None found” includes the searched horizon, resolution, qualified methods and unavailable dependencies. It must not become reassurance about an unexamined risk.

### 7.2 Preserve the forecast actually delivered

An external-outcome forecast in a completed reading is structured at emission with proposition, outcome definition, timing, assumptions, information cutoff, evidence, method/model versions and original wording. Detection is a safeguard, not proof that every forecast was captured correctly.

Preserve incomplete-turn content and provisional exposure, but keep it interrupted and excluded from prediction detection under PPR-19. Do not silently turn an unsealed claim into a scored forecast. A model-stated probability is immutable from detection under the current contract; a later operator band is separately identified and immutable from confirmation. Neither becomes empirical calibration by being recorded. Misparsed windows are dismissed and re-registered, not silently overwritten.

Computed dates, retrodictions, research hypotheses and external-outcome forecasts have different claim types. A real prediction cannot escape accountability by later being called “symbolic” or “only an activation.”

### 7.3 Evaluate without hindsight or leakage

Freeze the evaluated proposition and any probability before the relevant outcome becomes known or occurs under the declared protocol. Distinguish pre-window forecasts, the still-future remainder of an ongoing period, nowcasts and retrodictions. Do not count already occurred events as a new forecast's successes or reset chronology after a rebuild.

Keep partial, censored, lapsed, disputed and unverifiable outcomes visible. Missing observations are not automatic failures, successes or permission to remove a difficult case. Report eligibility, capture, confirmation, observation and scoring denominators separately.

Life events and outcomes must not feed prospective generation under PPR-31. C3 prediction-ledger, outcome and calibration material must not enter synthesis prompts or leave to providers. Summaries, selectors, rectified inputs, personal weights and cached narratives cannot launder protected evidence across that boundary.

### 7.4 Learning can change or limit a claim family

Captured, prospectively eligible, scored, evaluated and empirically calibrated are different achievements. A functioning feedback loop proves lifecycle behavior, not forecast accuracy. Calibration and useful discrimination are different measures.

Evaluate by outcome, horizon, population, model/version, effective evidence, coverage and uncertainty. Distinguish insufficient evidence, no demonstrated improvement, negligible improvement, underperformance and a computational/source defect. Each calls for a different response. A probabilistic miss is retained honestly; its significance depends on the claim and wider evidence.

Where evidence warrants restriction, revise, demote or withdraw the affected claim family through governance and explain the consequence to users. Attributed traditional interpretation may remain where permitted, but cannot disguise the same unsupported real-world prediction. Future learning uses only admitted, independently evaluated and versioned outputs; no parked capability or personal adaptation is activated by this master.

## 8. Life-event evidence: fullest appropriate use across the data plane

Information supplied at chart creation or in a Paripraśna session must not remain trapped in that chat, a native-only file or a disconnected timeline. The target is a common subject-scoped observation process, preserving original testimony, reporter, event-time precision, when the system learned it, source, permitted purpose, dispute/confirmation and later corrections. Reported experience is significant testimony, not automatically independently verified fact.

Use purpose-qualified projections and derived features instead of copying the raw diary into every asset. A projection retains provenance and restrictions; derivation does not make protected information unrestricted. Distinguish events, the user's interpretation of events, satisfaction with an answer and adjudication of a specific forecast.

### 8.1 Four pathways, not one undifferentiated feedback loop

| Pathway | Legitimate purpose | Non-negotiable separation |
|---|---|---|
| Event-free chart forecasting | Prospective interpretation from admitted chart/method evidence | No life-event/outcome conditioning, including indirect conditioning, under present PPR-31. |
| Permitted historical inquiry | Examine fit, misfit, alternative manifestations and temporal alignment | Declare hindsight, information cutoff and chronology; independent-then-compare where required; never relabel fit as prospective validation. |
| Protected outcome evaluation | Adjudicate frozen eligible claims and study qualified performance | C3 firewall, appropriate restricted handling, explicit denominators and no provider-prompt leakage. |
| Future context-conditioned forecasting | Potentially use narrowly admitted pre-cutoff context in a distinct model | **Proposed amendment only:** requires explicit policy/architecture authorization, feature lineage, leakage tests and separate evaluation before implementation. |

The target is extensive utilization without changing the present boundaries. The fourth row is neither current authority nor a workaround for the first three.

### 8.2 Layer-specific contribution without contamination

| Internal layer | Valuable use of observations where authorized | What must not happen |
|---|---|---|
| L0 — Brahmagyan | Shared event vocabulary, precision/provenance definitions and qualified study methodology | Private diaries or personal outcomes becoming global doctrine/reference truth. |
| L1 — Gaṇita | Event-time astronomical calculations from admitted dates and separate historical contexts | Altering birth facts or chart calculations to fit biography; silently replacing natal inputs. |
| L2 — Bodha | Historical comparison of proposed structural interpretations, contradictions and rival expressions | Making biography-dependent support appear to be event-free chart structure. |
| L3 — Kāla | Align reported intervals with independently established temporal mechanisms; inspect unmatched windows | Using the observed event to choose the supposedly prior trigger or then feeding that selection into a forecast. |
| L4 — Phala | Refine historical manifestation distinctions and preserve exact forecast outcome definitions | Rewriting the original forecast to fit the eventual story. |
| L5 — Mīmāṃsā | Protected adjudication, misfit analysis, admitted research and qualified calibration | Automatic personal tuning, outcome leakage or unqualified learning promotion. |

These are semantic responsibilities, not permission to add tables, alter assets or switch on dormant writers. The next data-plane plan must resolve actual authority paths, identity, correction propagation, feature lineage, access and invalidation using existing machinery wherever adequate.

### 8.3 A fair reading of history

Invite peak events, ordinary periods and relevant non-events voluntarily; do not assume an incomplete diary is a complete history. Preserve uncertain dates and what could reasonably have been observed. Explain supported, mixed, unsupported and unassessable alignments, including unmatched activations and alternative explanations.

A voluntary observation brief can freeze rival propositions, what would support or contradict each, the period and date precision, ambiguity, insufficient observation and whether/when the person saw the interpretation. It must not demand constant self-monitoring. An observation after exposure is not automatically independent, and chronological association does not establish causation.

Corrections must propagate to the authorized consumers of the corrected evidence while retaining version history where lawful. Snapshot-on-consume preserves the original reading; it does not require continuing to display a known error without notice. Withdrawn access, deletion and required retention must be reconciled explicitly rather than promising both unconditional erasure and unlimited replay.

## 9. The experiences the person should be able to live through

These are connected experiences, not a compulsory six-view or any fixed-screen architecture.

1. **First encounter:** establish the intended subject, uncertain inputs, conventions and permissioned scope; ask a real question and see what this chart can answer now. Explain correction impact without making onboarding an obstacle course.
2. **An explorable understanding:** move from an important finding to the relevant graha, house, configuration, relationship, domain, period and source. The graph is a navigable explanation, not a decorative network or a build-dependency diagram.
3. **A deep investigation:** clarify intent, discover missing concepts, compare interpretations and retain complete findings with their conjoint meaning. The system can say that further analysis will not resolve the real uncertainty.
4. **A temporal landscape:** distinguish enduring structure, active mechanisms, overlapping windows, recurrence and possible manifestations. Move between a life chapter and a narrow interval without changing evidential identity.
5. **A thoughtful choice comparison:** examine feasible options and timing against the person's objectives, with practical evidence clearly separated from astrological interpretation. The product does not make the decision on their behalf.
6. **A source-to-application companion:** read, compare and understand the tradition, then see what a qualified rule or exception actually changes in an example or reading.
7. **A truthful history and forecast review:** enter observations, see fit and misfit, inspect what was predicted before the event and preserve failed expectations without retrospective repair.
8. **A continuing inquiry:** retrieve earlier readings, branch a question, challenge it, compare two states of understanding, export or share authorized meaning, and close the inquiry when appropriate.
9. **A serious inquiry into meaning:** investigate tensions between aspiration, responsibility, relationships, service and inner life through qualified Jyotish interpretation. Dharma, artha, kāma and mokṣa can supply attributed lenses, not reductive scores or a declaration of cosmic assignment. Clarify supported relationships and open questions without inventing spiritual rank, past-life biography or deserved suffering.

The experience must work for ordinary charts and ordinary periods, not only dramatic named yogas or the repeatedly studied native chart. Plain language should be as intelligent as technical language. Accessibility and language support must preserve the meaning of terms, uncertainty and distinctions; translation quality must be evaluated, not assumed.

## 10. Every portal surface must carry the same product promise

A capability is not fully realized because it exists in an asset, service, tool or page. The intended consumer must be able to discover it, invoke it with the right context, understand its contribution and retain the reading without loss of meaning.

The final source review reconciled fourteen surface families. Counts and source limitations belong in the accompanying review record; the table below defines their target contribution, not their present completion status.

| Surface family | Contribution required by the product |

| Construction and readiness | Question-relative availability, qualification, freshness and partiality. A technically complete build is not complete interpretive readiness. Show which missing dependency limits this question and what a rebuild would change. |
| Consult and Paripraśna | One coherent deep inquiry through live stream, partial result, stop/resume, persistence and replay. Flag-gated pages and aliases must not masquerade as independently complete experiences. |
| Inquiry organization and user controls | Projects, folders, branches, search, admitted attachments and open investigations form a durable workspace. Separate uploaded testimony from embedded instructions. Persona, language, provider and length controls cannot silently change truth or omit material findings. |

### 10.1 Channel parity and continuity

Equivalent authorized requests through Paripraśna and the managed MCP reading door must receive equivalent substantive findings, conjoint interpretation, qualification, safety and receipt semantics. They need not have identical prose or transport. Meaning must survive streaming, persistence, continuation, export and replay.

Raw MCP supplies qualified evidence/tools to an external orchestrator. It owes authorization, correct scope, provenance and honest envelopes, but it does not certify the external orchestrator's completed interpretation. A consumer must be able to tell which guarantee they are receiving.

Permissioned inquiry continuity is a target where valuable; it is not mandatory transcript replication. Existing restrictions on cross-channel transcript portability and MCP memory remain until a separately approved continuity contract exists.

### 10.2 Graceful incompleteness and context integrity

Expose meaningful progress and unavailable dependencies without dumping internal infrastructure jargon into the reading. A failure must identify what was and was not established, retain safe completed work and permit appropriate resumption. A stale snapshot cannot silently become current because a chat is replayed.

Changing charts, dates, locations, methods or projects must preserve explicit context and invalidate only the dependent work. No default or retired subject ID may override the person's actual selection. Availability badges, completion indicators and quality claims need detectors for the exact claims they make.

## 11. The retained data plane and its contribution contracts

Retain L0's global reference foundation and L1–L5's principal chart/subject-specific semantic ownership. Existing shared or global evaluation, model and service resources retain their proper registered ownership; they need not become L0 assets or per-chart build rows. This internal architecture is a strong foundation; the product does not require a new truth layer or preservation of an old six-view design. Externally use Brahmagyan, Gaṇita, Bodha, Kāla, Phala and Mīmāṃsā.

| Layer | Product responsibility | Proof that matters |
|---|---|---|
| L0 — Brahmagyan | Qualified vocabulary, sources, rules, constants, reference systems, ephemeris/calendar foundations and method boundaries | Canonical identity, source fidelity, qualified semantics, reachable interfaces and reusable doctrine; no private history as global truth. |
| L1 — Gaṇita | Reproducible subject calculations under declared inputs/conventions | Correct fact identity, values, units, precision, provenance and qualified verification. Downstream consumers reference facts, not recalculate their own versions. |
| L2 — Bodha | Whole-chart structural understanding, configurations, relationships, mechanisms, tensions and alternatives | Qualified contributions beyond isolated placements; complete prerequisite/exception paths and transparent dependence on L1. |
| L3 — Kāla | Which structures are engaged by which clocks, when, under what conditions and with which alternatives | Structure–time intersections, temporal hierarchy, inhibiting/enabling factors, recurrence, comparison and coverage—not a collection of unjoined timelines. |
| L4 — Phala | Qualified manifestation and permitted outcome interpretation | An explicit bridge from configurations and timing to named life distinctions; no unqualified composite score or fictional precision. Calibration remains owned by L5. |
| L5 — Mīmāṃsā | Challenge, adjudication, discovery, scope of validity and qualified learning | Preserved failures, independent evidence, correct denominators, leakage-free evaluation and separately governed promotion. |

Cross-cutting resources—concept coverage, identity resolution, provenance, subject observations, claim identities and question-to-capability discovery—are logical contracts over existing authorities. They are not automatic proposals for six new registries or a seventh layer.

### 11.1 Preserve first, then identify the value delta

Inventory the complete useful capital: registered assets, supporting writers, non-writer services, rules/corpus, deterministic answer shapers, retrieval, channel adapters and consumer surfaces. A campaign's frozen denominator is not the product denominator. Previously accepted assets remain subject to value analysis; they are not presumed deficient or automatically reimplemented.

For each component record:

- The consumer distinction and astrological concept it enables.
- Its authoritative inputs, qualified transformation, outputs and downstream consumers.
- The useful kernel to preserve: data, rules, computation, interface, identity, tests and evidence.
- Missing depth, semantic conflict, overlap, unreachable value or unnecessary duplication.
- Disposition: preserve, wrap/connect, enrich, qualify, consolidate, replace only the inadequate part, or retire after dependency analysis.
- A discriminating test showing the value difference and any new error or burden.

No compromise on needed value; no rework for its own sake. Two similar assets can be complementary or dependent corroboration. Conversely, different names can hide the same computation. Consolidate only after understanding semantics, consumers and provenance, with separately authorized changes.

### 11.2 Eliminate stranded knowledge

The discoverable path must connect **question → concept → qualified rule → canonical fact → relationship/temporal mechanism → manifestation/insight → delivered finding**. A planner needs to know not only that a tool exists, but which concepts/fields it can supply, its prerequisites, scope, completeness and gaps.

Trace six distinct evidence states: source-present; method-qualified; consumed by the intended downstream component; traceably transformed; actually served through the intended channel; and evaluated for consumer value. A built row, registered tool, successful invocation or passing schema does not establish all six.

Answer-shaping aggregation deserves the same scrutiny as writers. An assessment score, ranked finding, bundle, deterministic sentence or final AI synthesis can lose domain meaning even while every input row is correct. The same concept/relationship coverage and no-fabrication standards apply end to end.

## 12. Concrete readings that define the standard

The following are fictional acceptance sketches, not chart readings, calculations or personal predictions. Window A/B are placeholders; no date, graha condition or event is asserted for a real person.

### 12.1 “When does my financial promise activate—and when does strain end?”

Clarify whether the question concerns receipts, retained surplus or durable relief from obligations. Examine relevant qualified structural relationships, strength/condition, domain-specific varga support, applicable Bhāvat Bhāvam, configurations and their cancellations. Follow material dispositorship or other admitted chains, including counter-evidence that distinguishes obtaining resources from keeping them.

Then ask which qualified clocks engage those same participants and conditions. Window A might support nearer activity or receipts while carrying an unresolved retention constraint; Window B might have more coherent support for the stated sustained outcome. The reading explains the actual reasons and shared dependencies rather than saying that “more planets agree.” Where the manifestation bridge is not qualified, stop at supported activation and identify the missing link. Where it is qualified and permitted, preserve a specific forecast.

The delivered finding contains the connected interpretation, not ten isolated favourable factors. It must neither infer investment advice nor use the person's supplied financial history to condition the event-free forecast. A separate permitted retrospective inquiry can investigate history with hindsight declared.

### 12.2 “When does my Nīcha-bhaṅga Rāja Yoga trigger?”

Check the actual formation, cancellation condition and further qualifications for the stronger interpretation before searching time. Explain whether the support is for cancellation, a qualified rāja-yoga interpretation, partial expression, dispute or an unimplemented rule—not simply “yoga present.”

List eligible activation routes, closest contact, better-supported windows and opposing conditions with scope and uncertainty. A participant's nearest transit contact is not automatically the activation of the whole configuration. If several manifestations remain plausible, name and distinguish them. If the label is unsupported, the product still explains the actual configuration and its value without preserving a flattering false premise.

### 12.3 “You predicted a promotion. It did not happen.”

Retrieve the original completed reading and frozen claim. If it forecast a formal promotion, later increased responsibility alone does not turn it into a hit. Respect the original outcome definition, uncertainty and observation window; retain unknown or partial states where appropriate rather than forcing a score.

Investigate an input error, missed cancellation, overstated manifestation bridge, weak temporal specificity or simple model error through the permitted evaluation path. Do not blame the person's faith, insufficient remedies or failure to act on an unstated condition. Explain what changes in future practice and what cannot yet be concluded from a single case.

### 12.4 “What would remain if we excluded this disputed method?”

Run the actual controlled comparison. Report the findings that survive, those that weaken or reverse and those that cannot be recomputed. Show which assumption made the difference and which connected evidence is shared. Preserve both versions and distinguish interpretive sensitivity from real-world validity. If excluding the method makes no material difference, say so plainly.

## 13. Boundaries that remain binding

This master preserves existing safety and access authority; it does not silently liberalize or tighten a native ruling. Consult the ratified architecture and decision register for exact operational requirements.

- No individualized death dates, lifespan classes or mortality windows, including indirect equivalents.
- No individualized illness forecasts, diagnosis, clinical reassurance or medical timing advice. Sensitive health/mental-health work retains its governed seal, two independent adversarial passes and separate sign-off where applicable. A suicide/self-harm-adjacent request receives the governed support-oriented response, without astrological analysis or retrieval planning.
- No fertility diagnosis, promised conception, child-sex prediction, harmful hierarchy, caste-based worth, gender inferiority or moral blame. Historical material can be studied critically without licensing harmful application.
- Practices/remedies remain voluntary and attributed; no harmful prescription, unsupported efficacy, coercion, monetized fear or commercial pressure.
- No unauthorized third-party interpretation, identifying publication or disguised research enrollment. Minors remain excluded from cohorts and subject to the existing narrow guardian-serving rule. Public disclosure remains fail-closed until its governed protocol exists.
- No invented numerical computation, source, detector, confidence, empirical score or claim of exhaustive coverage; no outcome laundering into prospective generation.
- No automatic rectification, outcome-driven personal tuning, autonomous decisions for the person or representation of astrological association as established practical causation.

**Current consent posture is preserved:** `SUBJECT_CONSENT_ENFORCEMENT` remains `OFF_BY_DESIGN` under the existing native ruling, with no new trigger condition introduced here. This does not abolish authentication, subject scoping, authorized disclosure or the separate boundaries on research, third-party access and public release. General statements of the target do not flip the mechanism on or rewrite that decision.

Privacy, faithful history and correction must coexist through explicit retention/access policy. A retained hash or tombstone is not necessarily anonymous and must not reconstruct deleted content. An unavailable reading explains its unavailable state. One intelligence standard does not mean unrestricted access to every dataset.

## 14. Proof of the product, not merely proof of its machinery

Use a balanced scorecard; one high score cannot conceal failure elsewhere. Existing metric and activation gates remain binding. New evaluation protocols are to be specified in their execution briefs, not treated as implemented by this definition.

| Obligation | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Source and domain fidelity | Correct source/context, method identity, applicability, exceptions and genuine source-to-rule-to-application correspondence. |
| Computational correctness | Authoritative inputs, reproducible calculations, units/conventions, sensitivity and independent verification where required. |
| Concept and relationship completeness | Seeded and novel omission cases; detect a missing Bhāvat Bhāvam application, lost cancellation, severed chain or unsupported generalization. |
| Interpretive fidelity | Material rivals, qualified manifestation bridge, counter-evidence, dependence accounting and no unsupported prose added after synthesis. |
| Distinctive understanding | Correct useful distinctions against competent simpler baselines, ordinary as well as dramatic cases, and added errors counted alongside added insight. |
| Consumer understanding and agency | Can the person explain the important distinction, what depends on an assumption and what would change the conclusion? Measure burden as well as usefulness. Resonance or amazement alone is insufficient. |
| Temporal integrity | Correct boundaries, zones, hierarchy, horizons, nearest/strongest separation, structural joins and absence-versus-unavailable semantics. |
| Predictive performance | Frozen eligible claims, valid comparison baselines, observation coverage, discrimination, calibration and uncertainty without leakage or selective denominators. |
| Delivery fidelity | Complete findings survive managed-channel equivalents, stream/save/resume/replay/export and authorized redaction; no wrong-chart carryover. |
| Safety and operation | Real authorization/firewall/retraction behavior, earned statuses, recovery under failure, actual cost and reliability; no operational metric misrepresented as astrological validity. |

Expert assessments should begin with independent readings before exposure to Madhav, declared information/resources, difficult and ordinary questions and recorded disagreement. Development on the familiar native chart is not independent evidence of generalization. Subject substitution and plausible-looking distractors should expose generic answers, but chart matching alone does not validate person-level interpretation.

Test changes by ablation where meaningful: remove a relationship, concept, source exception or answer-shaping component and observe what correctly changes. A legitimate contribution can alter uncertainty or exclude a false claim without changing the central conclusion. Ensure the comparison tests the proposed contribution rather than a proxy such as row count.

The first proving set should include a deep structural question without a forced forecast; a structure–time question with accountable permitted forward claims when earned; and a historical challenge that actively seeks misfit. Engineering and interpretive tests can proceed while future observations accrue. No requirement to wait for years of events before fixing upstream semantics, and no inference that a few successful cases prove universal prediction.

## 15. Strategic horizons beyond today's portal

These are valuable directions, not currently activated capabilities or excuses to delay core elevation.

- **Relational inquiry:** distinguish each authorized individual's structure from qualified interaction, complementary capacities, asymmetries, conflicting expectations and timing of a shared undertaking. Do not infer private thoughts or reduce relationships to compatibility scores.
- **Entity inquiry:** distinguish an entity's own qualified foundation from founders or participants. Competing definitions of an organizational beginning remain explicit; never select a convenient event retrospectively for a compelling chart.
- **Specialized Jyotish:** deepen qualified Praśna, Muhūrta and other method-native practice from existing partial services. Non-natal and collective/spatial methods require their own subject, inputs, output boundaries and evidence—not a natal engine with renamed fields.
- **Scholarly laboratory:** critical witnesses, translations, variants, rules and worked examples can support disciplined investigation of neglected or lost knowledge. Reconstructed hypotheses remain labeled; rights and expert qualification remain necessary.
- **Contrastive research workbench:** examine scoped rules across qualified cases, negative examples, alternative assumptions and prospective tests. No automatic diary mining, cohort activation, model fitting or research enrollment.

Promote a horizon only when its distinct consumer value, qualified method, necessary data, authorization and evaluation route are concrete. More technique names without such a path are not elevation.

## 16. How this master governs all subsequent work

This is the final adopted **target product definition for this planning baseline**. Future substantive changes require an explicit successor or amendment with reasons; “final” does not prevent evidence-led improvement. It does prevent parallel drafts from quietly becoming competing product authorities.

The sequence is product definition → cohesive data-plane plan → L0–L5 layer plans → asset/interface deltas → separately authorized execution → consumer-level verification. Retrieval, answer-shaping instruments, managed channels, source experiences and evaluation must be aligned alongside the data plane, not left until every writer is technically closed.

Every layer/asset brief must state its consumer contribution, relevant Jyotish concepts, preserved kernel, exact delta, authoritative dependencies, manifestation/temporal role where applicable, serving path, testable value and remaining gates. It must consider accepted and supporting assets as well as residual campaign items. This definition does not order a live campaign to stop, reopen a seal or claim that dependent plans are already aligned.

Execute with velocity: reuse working data, code, contracts, tests and evidence; automate mechanical checks; use one useful record rather than parallel ledgers; require human decisions only for consequential meaning, risk or authority changes. Do not repeatedly recertify unchanged work or inflate governance into its own campaign. Correctness, privacy and genuine acceptance remain part of delivery, not optional overhead.
