# Yogini Signal Extraction — M9-A-S1

## Yogini System Overview

The Yogini Dasha system operates on a **36-year cycle** of 8 Yoginis. Unlike Vimshottari Dasha
(120-year cycle based on Moon's nakshatra at birth), Yogini measures **period character** — the
qualitative flavour of time — rather than the natal strength of a planet. A Yogini period modulates
all domains simultaneously: during Bhramari (Mars), conflict and energy intensify across career,
health, and property regardless of which Vimshottari sub-period is running.

| Yogini   | Duration | Ruling Planet | Primary Domain Character                         |
|----------|----------|---------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Mangala  | 1 year   | Moon          | Emotional volatility; new beginnings; maternal themes |
| Pingala  | 2 years  | Sun           | Authority crises; health; visibility; government |
| Dhanya   | 3 years  | Jupiter       | Wealth; progeny; dharmic activity; guru          |
| Bhramari | 4 years  | Mars          | Conflict; energy; property disputes; litigation  |
| Bhadrika | 5 years  | Mercury       | Learning; commerce; communication; siblings      |
| Ulka     | 6 years  | Saturn        | Hardship; discipline; karmic reckoning; isolation |
| Siddha   | 7 years  | Venus         | Prosperity; relationships; arts; luxury; fulfilment |
| Sankata  | 8 years  | Rahu          | Sudden reversals; hidden forces; fear; unexpected crisis |

**Total cycle:** 1+2+3+4+5+6+7+8 = 36 years. Repeats continuously from birth.
