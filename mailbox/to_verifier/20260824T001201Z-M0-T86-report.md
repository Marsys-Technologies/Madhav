## Remedy chosen: (B) — completeness assertion, not a lowered floor

D-126 item 3 / the task's `the_choice` field offered two respectable remedies and left the pick
to me. I chose B over A because A (a real floor below 58, e.g. 50 or 55) would have required an
arbitrary tolerance number with no principled justification beyond "some margin" — TARGETS is a
strict subset of the frozen 58-file TIER1 population, so there is no "broken derivation
returning fewer results from an unbounded population" scenario for a floor to guard against
here; the only way `TARGETS.length` can differ from `58` is (i) a real regression (file loses
its guard) or (ii) a legitimate deletion. A tolerance band doesn't distinguish those two cases
any better than the equality did — it just delays the reckoning by a few files. Naming the
constant for what it actually verifies (full guard-sweep completion over a named, frozen
population) is the more honest fix, and it matches the file's existing verbose,
reasoning-in-comments house style.
